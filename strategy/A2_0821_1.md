## 6. Strategy recommendation

The organizing facts: (1) every invertible-transform mechanism is now a proved dead end on the frozen cores; (2) the frontier is five avatars of what is plausibly one missing signed estimate; (3) exactly one lane — the short-window cluster moment — pays out *partial* exponent progress for *partial* savings, while every M9 lane is all-or-nothing at its own scale; (4) the program's one estimate-producing in-house method is the sampled-variation/reciprocal-curvature technology of R103–104; (5) the program now legally owns, at `proved_external_dependency`, the only method known to beat 1/3 globally (repaired Li–Yang / Guth–Maldague), fully hypothesis-audited. The recommendations:

**S-1 (Round 114, the permitted core attack). Attack the balanced packet through its mean-square reformulation, as a decidable fork.** The proposed node `M9-M2-dual-square-root-spacing-mean-square` — `sum_(h≍L) |sum_(k≍K) a_(L,K)(h,k) e(sqrt(Xhk))|^2 <<_eps L^(1/2) K^(3/2) X^eps` — is diagonal-critical exactly at `K ≍ L`, which is exactly the balanced regime (`K/L = X/D^2 ∈ [1,16]` ⟺ `D ≍ X^(1/2)`). Its `k=k'` diagonal is `≍ LK`, and at `K ≍ L` the exact multiplicative collisions `hk = h'k'` contribute a truncated `1*chi_4`-correlation mass of order `L^2` polylog — the target `L^2 X^eps` is *tight at the diagonal with no room*, and (by `r_2/4 >= 0`) no sign cancellation is available on the diagonal itself: any proof must control the near-collision off-diagonal `0 < |hk − h'k'| << L^2/…`, whose phase `≈ e(sqrt(X) m/(2 sqrt(n)))` rotates by `>> X^(1/4)/L >> 1` per unit shift `m`. Design the round as three gates. *Gate 0 (connector):* prove or refute the implication chain mean-square (`K ≍ L` variant, quarter shifts and dictionary atoms included) `=>` 113.S10; the first doubtful step is whether the `G_sigma`-weighted atom family assembles into an `h`-indexed family with `ℓ^2`-controlled weights compatible with Cauchy in `h`; the `K >= L` scope of the existing node must be extended to the balanced case.
