## 2. What was actually established

**Reciprocal character cancellation.** For a smooth actual divisor rectangle DM comparable to N, D,M>=1/2, N<=T^(3/2), amplitude derivatives bounded by N^(-3/4)D^(-j)M^(-k), and real R comparable to T,

    |sum_(d,m) chi4(d)a(d,m)e(Rsqrt(dm))|
      << T^epsilon[(T/N)^(1/2)+T^(-1/2)].

Poisson summation in m leaves the linear character phase R^2d/(4h), amplitude b_h and h comparable to RD/sqrt(N). The exact chi4 Fourier expansion reduces each d fibre to two geometric sums. Near resonances are counted through the integer products h(4k+/-1) close to R^2. Summing their moduli gives

    A0 T^epsilon[H log(2+D)+D].

The stationary errors and all nonstationary tails are summed explicitly. This is an actual-character estimate, but its complete high-norm consequence is the already known square-root tail bound. It gives no improved estimate for the remaining middle owner.

**A real-phase distinction that must be preserved.** Exact quarter-frequency resonances give imaginary character fibres. The stationary shift e(-1/8) and original circle shift e(-3/8) multiply to -1, so their leading real contribution is zero. However, the admissible detuning alpha_h=L/4+c/D, with h=L, R^2=L^2+4Lc/D and c=1/32, gives a positive real fibre comparable to A0 D on a smooth nonnegative rectangle. Thus neither the complex nor the real individual-fibre sum has a uniform square-root bound. The real coherent contribution is only of order T^(-1/2); this control supplies no lower bound for the full wave or its moment. Any successful use of the exact zero must retain the signs across nearby frequencies or use the radius integral.

**Exact external-source comparison.** Kowalski--Robert--Wu Proposition5, including alpha=beta=1/2, applies to the actual sharp balanced mask after orienting the divisor pairs and completing each moving interval at logarithmic cost. Product partial summation preserves the interval condition. The normalized source bound is

    T^epsilon(1+N/T)^(-A)
      [T^(1/8)N^(1/16)+N^(1/4)D^(-1/2)+D^(1/4)+T^(-1/2)].

The aspect-independent first term controls on the present residual range. Its full source ceiling3/16 is worse than beta. These are limits of these displayed upper bounds, not lower bounds for the actual wave. The primary source was checked at printed p.303, not inferred from a summary: Kowalski, Robert and Wu, *Small gaps in coefficients of L-functions and B-free numbers in short intervals*, Revista Matematica Iberoamericana23 (2007), 281--326, DOI10.4171/RMI/496, [publisher PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/38194).
