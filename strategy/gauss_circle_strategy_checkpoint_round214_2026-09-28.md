## 4. Concrete next candidate and its first missing inequality

For each critical N, define the exact torus wave

    F_N(x,R)=sum_(m in Z^2, m!=0) c_m e(x.m+R|m|),
    c_m=|m|^(-3/2) Psi(|m|/sqrt(T)) v(|m|^2/N).

At x=0, integer norm multiplicity gives precisely the physical r2 block.
Cover the full comparison radius interval by O(T/sqrt(N)) windows with
centers R_j and length comparable to sqrt(N). Let chi be a fixed
Schwartz cutoff with small compact Fourier support, bounded below on
the required central cube. Use the same fixed cutoff for every window,
with a sufficiently large fixed dilation when necessary. Put

    f_j(y,u)=chi(y/N,u/N)
              F_N(y/sqrt(N),R_j+u/sqrt(N)).

Its exact coefficients retain c_m e(R_j|m|). They cannot be chosen
independently between windows or replaced by arbitrary phases in a
positive proof. Let W(y)=C_A(1+|y|^2)^(-A), with fixed sufficiently
