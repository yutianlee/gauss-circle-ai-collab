## 1. Executive decision

The Round-130 strategy is revised in four material ways.

1. **Round 131 becomes a terminal park-or-bank gate for the graded top shell.** It is not authorization for another open-ended sequence of internal transforms.
2. **The decisive graded requirement is now explicitly cross-ray.** Cancellation confined to one fixed primitive outer ray cannot by itself produce a strict global exponent improvement.
3. **After Round 131, the project enters a structured import phase.** The first two import questions are the exact small-cap-decoupling/high-low hypothesis map and the Bombieri--Iwaniec double-large-sieve map.
4. **The endpoint architecture remains primary.** The graded `W=Y^(7/16)` lane is a mechanism laboratory and possible exponent-improvement lane; it is not a substitute for the endpoint conjunction `M9-M2` + `M9-M1`/GAR + endpoint uniformity.

The following A2 conclusions are adopted:

- the lower-shell refinement is rigorous and leaves only `B asymp D` exponent-critical;
- the resolved energy is `H^*H` or `K_(B,+)^*K_(B,+)`, not `G^2`;
- `p` is a bounded-multiplicity determinant-residue lift, not an independent long variable;
- the internal sampled-variation/curvature mechanism is close to exhausted on the graded lane;
- Round 131 should test the actual determinant-residue zero mode and possible Salié structure;
- the next phase should test genuinely noninvertible external inequalities rather than repeat Poisson/B-process self-returns;
- the recurrent repository-state hygiene defects should be automated rather than re-reported.

Four qualifications are imposed on A2's proposals.

- `Y^(31/48)` is the natural bank from a **square-root** complete-character estimate in the length-`L` residue variable; it is not asserted as an absolute theorem-level ceiling for every conceivable per-ray argument.
- Salié structure is a question to be proved from the literal weight. The quarter phases and `chi_4` factors do not by themselves authorize the word “Salié.”
- All external decoupling, double-large-sieve, Kloosterman, trace-function, and moment inputs remain `source_audit_required` until their statements and parameter maps are complete.
- Probabilistic and homogeneous-dynamics evidence remains `diagnostic_only`; it cannot certify a fixed-centre pointwise bound.

## 3. Capacity calibration governing all future graded work

### 3.1 Persistence ladder

If the complete graded fixed-block bound is

\[
|\mathfrak O_i|\ll Y^{\beta+\varepsilon},
\]

then the accepted local-moment bridge has pointwise exponent

\[
\Theta(\beta)
=
\max\left(\frac{7/16+\beta}{3},\frac{\beta}{2}\right).
\]

The key values are

\[
\begin{array}{c|c|c}
\beta & \text{block scale} & \Theta(\beta)\\ \hline
35/48 & \text{current internal graded block} & 7/18\\
27/48=9/16 & \text{persistence threshold} & 1/3\\
24/48=1/2 & \text{determinant target} & 5/16.
\end{array}
\]

Therefore:

- any global improvement below `1/3` requires a **strict** complete-block exponent below `9/16`;
- reaching `9/16` exactly is not enough;
- reaching the determinant target `1/2` yields `5/16`, which improves the internal `1/3` and the current audited external benchmark, but still does not prove the quarter conjecture.

### 3.2 Cross-ray cancellation is mandatory

On the top shell, the present triangle has the schematic capacity

\[
D\cdot L\cdot \frac{K_D}{L}=D K_D=Y^{35/48+o(1)}.
\]

Here the factors are:

- outer-ray `ell^1` capacity `D`;
- `L` physical numerator increments per ray;
- the Round-129 inner estimate `K_D/L`.

A generic square-root cancellation in the length-`L` residue variable would give

\[
\frac{D K_D}{\sqrt L}=Y^{31/48+o(1)}.
\]

Even an ideal collapse of the entire increment/alias family to one term per ray gives only

\[
\frac{D K_D}{L}=Y^{27/48+o(1)}=Y^{9/16+o(1)},
\]

which is exactly the persistence threshold. Hence no theorem confined to one fixed outer ray can by itself yield a strict global exponent improvement. A strict improvement must also beat the outer-ray triangle.

In the normalized correlation grading

\[
|\mathfrak O|\ll D K_D^{1-\sigma}Y^\varepsilon,
\]

one needs

\[
\sigma>\frac{8}{11}
\]

to cross below `9/16`, while the determinant target corresponds to `sigma=1`.

This is the principal new strategy constraint. Any proposed graded mechanism must identify where its cross-ray gain enters. A per-ray theorem with no outer correlation is a local bank only.

### 3.3 The inner reciprocal estimate is no longer the main frontier

The top-shell inner estimate is at the classical second-derivative scale. Under the standard exponent-pair parameterization, replacing the present second-derivative estimate by Bourgain's exponent pair would improve the inner exponent only from about `11/48` to about `9.96/48`; even the exponent-pair conjectural ceiling would leave it near `9.5/48`. This calibration is not a proof-level impossibility theorem, but it shows that repeated inner-sum optimization cannot supply the required distance to `9/16`.

Accordingly:

- do not open another round whose only new mechanism is a better one-variable exponent pair;
- an exponent-pair estimate is relevant only inside a larger cross-ray dispersion theorem;
- most remaining graded power must come from outer-ray anti-alignment.

### 3.4 Self-similarity of the terminal graded object

Exact Stieltjes, Möbius, and complete-lift recombination returns the all-owner original `(h',d')` product wave. Thus the top-shell survivor is not a discounted toy problem: it has returned to the same structural class as the original twisted divisor wave. This justifies a hard stop on further normalization-only rounds.

## 4. Round 131: terminal determinant-residue zero-mode gate

### 4.1 Exact objective

Starting from the physically reassembled `B asymp D` scalar, write

\[
\mathfrak O^+_{i,D}
=
\sum_{r=(a,b)} A_i(r)e\!\left(\frac{ca}{\kappa_i b}\right)
\sum_n \mathcal W_{i;r}(n)
\,e\bigl(\Phi_{i;r}(n)\bigr),
\]

where `n=aq-bp` and `mathcal W_{i;r}(n)` contains, without separation:

- the bounded-to-one physical `p`-lift;
- the exact M1 or M2 character factor;
- Stieltjes thresholds and equality atoms;
- Möbius divisors and common lift character;
- determinant taper;
- reciprocal stationary aliases;
- smooth/hard profiles, stars, floors, sign sectors, cells, and half-open owners;
- the actual outer coefficient `A_i(r)`.

The round must separate the periodic residue dependence from the nonperiodic threshold/profile envelope. Because the arithmetic characters have level `4`, the natural preliminary Fourier modulus is `4|a|`, not merely `|a|`:

\[
\mathcal W^{\rm ar}_{i;r}(n)
=
\sum_{\ell\bmod 4|a|}
\widehat{\mathcal W}_{i;r}(\ell)
 e\!\left(\frac{\ell n}{4|a|}\right).
\]

The relevant resonant mode may be shifted by the M1 quarter carriers or the M2 numerator character. The round must compute the **physical resonant mode**, not assume that it is the naive Fourier zero mode.

### 4.2 Salié-identification subtask

The round should test whether, after a legal completion, one obtains a complete sum of the form

\[
\sum_{x\bmod q}^{*}
\chi(x)
 e_q(Ax+B\bar x)
\]

or a finite, exactly controlled combination of such sums. The following must be proved before the term “Salié” is used:

1. the exact modulus `q` and unit condition;
2. the precise quadratic or `chi_4` character;
3. the inverse-variable phase and its coefficients;
4. the completion formula for the literal interval, taper, threshold, and owner package;
5. the total completion and boundary cost;
6. the pullback of the resulting square-root phase to the outer-ray variables.

An explicit Salié evaluation is not itself the desired estimate. Such an evaluation usually returns square-root phases and may reproduce the existing self-similarity. The only bankable analytic consequence is a proved bound for the literal incomplete physical coefficient or a source-audited moduli-average theorem.

### 4.3 Optional parallel diagnostic: centre-averaged second moment

With all coefficients and heights frozen, compute or sharply reduce

\[
\int_{Y}^{2Y}
|\mathfrak O^+_{i,D}(c)|^2\,dc.
\]

This task is `diagnostic_only` unless it produces a fully proved identity or estimate. Its purpose is to distinguish:

- a diagonal-dominated average with the difficulty concentrated in an average-to-maximum upgrade; from
- a large off-diagonal average identifying the exact determinant/residue classes responsible for the excess.

No averaged result may be promoted to a prescribed-centre theorem without an explicit large-value or maximal inequality.

### 4.4 Required hostile controls

The Round-131 candidate must survive:

- the same-denominator M1 aligned packets;
- the same-denominator M2 aligned packets in the positive-numerator regime;
- sign-crossing packets, with `chi_4(|a'|)` handled literally;
- threshold equality atoms;
- support births and deaths;
- the top-shell owner versus all-owner distinction;
- coefficient vectors aligned with the proposed residue mode;
- the distinction between the physical scalar and positive outer energy.

### 4.5 Terminal exit trichotomy

Round 131 closes under exactly one of the following labels.

#### Bank

A literal theorem proves one of:

- exact vanishing or target-safe reduction of the physical resonant mode;
- complete-character structure plus a square-root residue saving;
- a stronger actual-coefficient residue estimate.

A generic square-root bank reprices the top shell

\[
Y^{35/48}\longrightarrow Y^{31/48}.
\]

Such a result is valuable local progress but changes no global exponent. The graded lane may continue only through a cross-ray import mechanism; no second per-ray internal round is authorized.

If an exceptional argument reaches the ideal per-ray floor `Y^(27/48)`, it still reaches only the persistence threshold. A strict cross-ray gain remains mandatory.

#### Obstruct

The exact resonant coefficient has a nonvanishing physical main term or a coherent literal packet of natural size. Promote the narrow physical obstruction and park the graded lane.

#### Degenerate

The induced residue weights are neither shown to cancel nor shown to contain a coherent main term, and no new noninvertible inequality is identified. Record the exact first unavailable identity and park the graded lane as exhausted for internal-only methods.

Under no outcome may Round 131 claim a new global exponent, `M9-M1`, `M9-M2`, `M9`, or `GC-target`.

## 6. Endpoint full-proof architecture after the import maps

The import phase is intended to select mechanisms for the endpoint parents, not to displace them.

## 6.1 M2 UNBAL: primary endpoint scalar

The endpoint UNBAL survivor is the prescribed-centre signed truncated-divisor wavelet, or equivalently its endpoint-complete character block. It is currently parked because scalar transforms and positive separated energies self-return.

### A. Modulus-aspect Kloosterman-fraction or Salié dispersion

This is the leading analytic-number-theory candidate. It must preserve the prescribed centre and the actual `chi_4` coefficients, and must win in a modulus average rather than at one fixed modulus.

### B. One-sided Beurling--Selberg/Vaaler majorant gate

Test whether the positive selector entering `E_chi` admits a one-sided band-limited majorant with:

- target-safe `L^1` excess;
- exact endpoint and owner control;
- finite Fourier support compatible with the literal characters;
- no replacement of the physical scalar by an unowned positive energy;
- a Poisson step used only on the majorant side.

This is a genuinely noninvertible inequality and is the most interesting untried internal move on UNBAL. It is only a feasibility gate. Park it immediately if the majorant excess, endpoint faces, or cross terms cost the missing power.

### C. High-low or large-value decomposition

If Round 132 yields a source-audited high-low theorem, use it to isolate coherent prescribed-centre packets and control the transverse complement. Average estimates remain insufficient without a pointwise tail theorem.

## 6.2 M2 BAL: broad--narrow cone route

The balanced survivor should be revisited only through an exact broad--narrow decomposition:

- narrow rational rulings and aligned packets treated arithmetically;
- broad caps treated by a source-audited transverse inequality;
- the full shift family, second gcd mask, determinant projector, and all owners retained;
- the target `L^3` energy compared against the blind `L^4` capacity.

If the decoupling map supplies no strict saving on the broad component, BAL remains parked.

## 6.3 M2 TOP: actual-vector cross-ray dispersion

Hard TOP remains the most rigid endpoint parent. It may be reopened only with a literal endpoint-coefficient property excluding the known aligned controls. Eligible imports are:

- an actual-vector double-large-sieve estimate;
- modulus-aspect signed trace/Kloosterman dispersion;
- a proved inverse theorem showing that excess forces a structured family that can be estimated separately.

Ambient operator norms, height-only filters, Schatten norms, and coefficient-uniform Bessel contraction remain frozen.

## 6.4 M1: lower-GAR primary backup, direct parents retained

The two direct M1 parents remain minimax-sharp at `1/3` under the accepted menu. The global angular-radial route has only the exact signed lower-radial aggregate open. This is the preferred M1 parallel lane because:

- the radial interface and nonlower complement are already proved;
- the survivor is one total-active scalar rather than two independent physical parents;
- uniform Bessel/stationary asymptotics can be applied without reopening the frozen canonical Gram.

The direct parents remain available if a new sign-sensitive block mechanism appears. No adjacent-profile telescoping or canonical-Gram return is authorized.

## 6.5 Endpoint uniformity and assembly

Uniformity is audited only after the analytic parents close. Every imported theorem must state:

- endpoint range in `D` or the corresponding conductor;
- strict/weak and star conventions;
- smooth/hard transition ownership;
- floor and Vaaler-height dependence;
