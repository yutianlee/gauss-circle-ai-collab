# Round 162 strategy: literal close-factor character bilinear gate

## 1. Frozen objective

Round 162 owns only the physical (t=1), (D\asymp L^2) face left open
by Round 161. Its target is

\[
 |\mathcal S_{L,1}|\ll_\varepsilon L^{3/2}X^\varepsilon,
\tag{162.S1}
\]

for every allowed hard-TOP block (1\ll L\ll H\le J^{1/2}), at one
arbitrary fixed real (J=\sqrt X). No claim about another radical channel
or a downstream owner is part of the round.

## 2. Inherited boundary

Round 161 proves the exact radical incidence, removes every fixed
(D\le CL) long-channel sector, and classifies exact collisions. It also
proves that a coefficient-uniform one-column common test has
(L^{2+o(1)}) capacity. Consequently this round must use the literal
(\chi_4(d_1))-weighted close-factor coefficient before a positive norm.

The accepted Round-137 obstruction already warns that a scalar B-process
on the product coefficient returns to the reciprocal hard-TOP carrier.
Round 162 may reopen Poisson only because it retains the (t=1) incidence
and the character shift explicitly; it must test the self-return rather
than rename it as a gain.

## 3. Mechanism order

The analytic order is fixed.

1. Reproduce the coefficient, both primitive orientations, the even
   (d_2) branch, and the exact profile and endpoint support.
2. Expand (\chi_4) into its two additive quarter shifts without taking
   absolute values.
3. Derive the rank-one Hessian and the one-variable stationary transform.
4. Derive the simultaneous dual equations and price the broadened dual
   product collar.
5. Open (\mu^2(d_1d_2)) only if every coupled square allocation,
   rescaled support, boundary, and summation cost remains visible.
6. Invoke a source only after its coefficient and endpoint interface is
   literally identified.

The conductor seed records algebra to be checked, not accepted
mathematics. In particular, the heuristic dual collar

\[
 |(k-\sigma/4)\ell-X/4|\lesssim J/L
\tag{162.S2}
\]

and its positive (\sqrt{JL}) capacity require full derivation before
promotion.

## 5. Selection and review rule

The conductor will select the smallest compatible kernel. A positive
result must preserve (\chi_4), squarefreeness, coprimality, the hard cone,
profiles, endpoints, and the full (L,J,H,X) range. A no-go must state the
exact method class and cannot become a physical lower bound.

The selected kernel then receives separate reviews of coefficient
algebra, stationary geometry and collar width, restored powers and source
hypotheses, and downstream graph scope. Only a green State Patch may alter
the graph.

- `hard_top_t1_close_factor_target`;
