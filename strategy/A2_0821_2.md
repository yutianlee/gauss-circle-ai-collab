The corresponding hostile obligations: (i) verify the narrow branch is not the smooth-collar TOP energy in disguise (circularity check against the R80 interface); (ii) compute the capacity at the narrow/broad **threshold interface** — the known failure mode of two-regime arguments in this program is that the deficit reappears exactly at the matching boundary (`rho^(-1/2)`-class); (iii) the equal-product stratum of the squared formulation is a sum of nonnegative squares (`Σ_n |Σ_(uv=n) chi_4(u) A(u,v)|^2`, Jacobi-positive on the full divisor sum), of size `≍ L^2` polylog against the `L^3` energy budget — so, unlike my mean-square framing where the diagonal was tight, **the squared-shell formulation has a factor-`L` cushion at the diagonal and concentrates the entire difficulty in the shifted products `r = uv − u'v' ≠ 0`**, with both characters `chi_4(u) chi_4(u')` still present.
