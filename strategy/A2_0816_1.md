## 2. Honest difficulty calibration

Three internal facts, all established in the repo itself, fix the difficulty class of what remains:

(a) The smooth dual reduction (`M9-M2-smooth-dual-three-quarter-equivalence`) is labeled "equivalent-hard": bounding `T_{L,K} = sum chi_4(h) a(h,k) e(sqrt(Xhk))` at `M^(3/4) X^eps` is not an easier surrogate. Via Popov's audited truncated Voronoi (error `O(X^(1/4+eps))` at cutoff `N ~ X^(1/2)`), the complete-coefficient analogue of the M9 endpoint sums at top cutoff is the circle conjecture. M9 at `D ~ X^(1/2)` is the full conjecture in Vaaler-truncated normal form, not a stepping stone to it.

(b) The unsigned problem is provably false in the range that matters: W-1/UNC give `Sigma_abs(0<|N|<=M) >= c min(D^4, MD) - C_eps D^2 X^eps`, refuting the absolute fat-band target for `D >= X^(1/3+delta)`, and `c_abs >> X^(1/4)` at the endpoint. So any closing argument must extract genuine `X^(1/4)`-scale cancellation from chi_4 and the fixed Vaaler profile. Every coefficient-blind tool (decoupling, large sieve, exponent pairs after absolute values, spacing counts used as upper bounds) is therefore structurally insufficient — the repo has ten explicit adversary countermodels, e.g. `a(h,m) = chi_4(h) e(-J sqrt(hm))` making every summand of `R_m` equal 1.

(c) The transform orbit is exhausted: eleven recorded self-returns (Poisson, B-process, Kuznetsov+Voronoi, wavelet Abel, reciprocal Poisson) prove the standard moves are involutive on this phase, which is forced by structure — `sqrt(Xhm)` is homogeneous of degree one in `(h,m)`, so its Hessian determinant vanishes identically; there is no nondegenerate second stationary-phase step to take. The recurring `X^(1/20)` deficit (four independent routes) and the `X^(1/8)`/`X^(1/12)` top-block deficits are stable numbers, i.e. genuine bottleneck measurements, not accumulated slack.

Progress diagnosis: the graph is shrinking in specificity, not in difficulty. Real, permanent assets: the conditional bridge with a single audited external dependency (Vaaler); the exact normal-form cascade to one finite hard object; the barrier library (371 rejections, structured); the beta-branch closure; the R71–72 conductor extensions. But no numerical exponent has moved since R72, and R76 promoted an obstruction, not an estimate. On current course the rounds will keep producing correct diagnostics against an equivalent-hard core.

For calibration against the trivial and target scales at the top block: `T_L^end` has ~`L^2` terms, trivial `L^2`, target `L^(3/2)`; the proved envelope `min(L^2, X^(1/4) L^(1/2))` meets the target at both ends (`L` bounded; `L ~ H`) and has worst deficit `X^(1/12)` at `L = X^(1/6)`. The energy target `L^2 X^eps` against trivial `L^3` demands square-root cancellation in `h` for typical `m` — at the endpoint scale this is the strength of the conjecture.

## 4. Cross-area method assessment

The question asked: for the full proof, what can l2 decoupling, combinatorics, asymptotic analysis, and probability contribute? The answer is organized around the one structural fact that dominates everything: **the needed inequality is false for arbitrary unimodular coefficients** (repo adversary countermodels) and **the unsigned count is provably too big** (W-1/UNC). Therefore every method splits into a lawful role (on coefficient-blind-true obligations: counts, moments, spacing) and an unlawful role (closing the signed pointwise bridge), and the graph would benefit from tagging each obligation explicitly as coefficient-blind-true or coefficient-blind-false.

### 4.1 l2 / small-cap decoupling (Fourier analysis)

What it can do. Decoupling is the engine behind the only record movement since 2003: Li–Yang's first-spacing estimate uses Guth–Maldague's sharp small-cap decoupling for the cone inside a counting problem, where signs are irrelevant and coefficient-blindness is harmless. The lawful M9 role is identical: inside first-spacing-type counting inputs to any large-sieve/large-value module, and inside moment estimates over an X-window. If a transformed frequency geometry for the M9 corridor is ever written (the strategy review's ranked item 4), a small-cap input could improve a *count* that feeds the corridor.

What it cannot do, with mechanism. Three independent walls. (i) Coefficient-blindness: decoupling bounds `sup` over coefficient classes; the target fails for the class, so any decoupling-only proof of the bridge would prove a falsehood. (ii) Degeneracy: `sqrt(Xhm)` is degree-one homogeneous; the surfaces relevant here are cones through the origin traversed radially, which is exactly the rank-one/self-return geometry — there is no curved-surface transversality left for fixed X. The repo's guardrail example (`Q(u,v)=uv` has Hessian −1 yet `e(Q)` is identically 1 on Z^2) is the right warning: modulo-one arithmetic, not curvature, is the missing resource. (iii) Fixed-X: decoupling averages over the dual variable; the circle problem is a measure-zero slice (fixed X), and the passage from `L^p` in X to fixed X is the average-to-pointwise wall already formalized in `M9-M2-LFM-pointwise-equivalence` and the `D^(3/5) X^(3/20+eps)` propagation ceiling. Also note: the harmonic-analytic inputs are already sharp (Guth–Maldague small caps are optimal), so further first-spacing gains must come from arithmetic structure invisible to decoupling — gcd stratification and rational-slope rigidity, which is precisely the territory the M9 normal forms occupy. Recommendation: keep decoupling as a module for counting obligations only; require every proposed decoupling import to pass `coefficient-adversary` and `signed-vs-unsigned` (already policy); expect the Li–Yang input map (ranked item 3) to fail at the endpoint and record the failure certificate — the printed Case A/B ranges stop short of the raw Vaaler endpoint `H ~ M T^(-1/4)`, and the published output exponent (0.3145) exceeds the target (0.25) even where hypotheses hold.

### 4.2 Combinatorics / additive structure

The unsigned counting war is over and lost, by the project's own theorems (W-1, UNC, and Iyer's `n^(-k/2)` density of near-resonances externally). What remains combinatorial is the **structure of the resonant set**, and here I can add a concrete computation to the Round-76 survivor.

The resonant union is `{(a,b,k) : ||X(sqrt b - sqrt a)^2/(2k)|| <~ G_{a,b}^(-1)}` with weights and the ray-constant signs. Split by whether `ab` is a perfect square. If `ab` is a square with `(a,b)=1`, then `a = s^2`, `b = t^2`, `s,t` odd coprime, and `(sqrt b - sqrt a)^2 = (t-s)^2` is an integer — these are the algebraic (rational-phase) rays, and both R76 falsifiers, `(81,121) = (9^2, 11^2)` and `(25,49) = (5^2, 7^2)`, live here. On these rays two exact facts hold. First, the offset sign is inert: `q = (b-a)/2 = (t^2-s^2)/2 = (t-s)(t+s)/2` is always even (odd±odd is even twice over), so `(-1)^q = +1` on **every** odd-square ray — the chi_4-offset mechanism supplies no cancellation whatsoever on the entire square family. Second, at half-integer `alpha` the lift phase is constant: `e(g alpha) = (-1)^g = -1` for all odd `g`, i.e. full coherence, which is exactly the R76 parity theorem specialized to this family. So within a resonant square ray there is provably no cancellation; the survivor inequality can only hold through **sparsity of resonant k plus cross-ray/cross-mode cancellation**. For consecutive odd squares (`t = s+2`) the resonance condition degenerates to `||2X/k|| <~ G^(-1)`, i.e. `k` near divisor-structured locations of `2X` — a divisor-bounded set plus a metric term. This makes the square family the right first adjudication target: its resonant mass is a finite, exactly computable object (divisor sums against the actual `beta` weights), and the outcome is decisive either way. If the total weighted square-family mass is `<= L^2 X^eps` uniformly in X, the survivor passes its most structured stress test and the remaining enemy is the generic (irrational `sqrt(ab)`) part. If some admissible X-family pushes it above `L^2 X^eps`, then the survivor as stated is false and the route must be repaired before further rounds are spent on it (note R76 already correctly rejected the claim that a single coherent ray lower-bounds the full energy — cross-`h` interference within `R_m` prevents that — so a family-level computation is needed, not a single-ray one). Proposed obligation below (S-D).

For the generic part, the honest classification: bounding the resonant mass at **fixed** X is a simultaneous non-resonance statement about `{X sqrt(ab)/k}` — provable on average over X by metric theory, open pointwise, i.e. the same average-vs-pointwise wall in new clothes. This is also exactly where the project's formulation is genuinely novel relative to the literature: Bombieri–Iwaniec second spacing needs unsigned counts and is frozen; the project needs a **signed second-spacing statement** — cancellation across rays and reciprocal modes in a regime where the count is provably too big. There is no template for this in the literature; it is the project's unique contribution if it succeeds, and formulating it as a clean standalone lemma (S-E below) has independent value even if it stays open.

### 4.3 Asymptotic analysis / special functions

This is the area the project already uses at research-grade rigor, and the audits show it is the reason the repo is healthy: the R75 rejection of the frozen leading symbol (missing `g''(t_0)/(8 pi i x l)` correction), the two-saddle Fresnel normal forms, the BV symbol calculus, and the endpoint boundary term `E_h(X) = e(hX/(4y))/(1 - e(hX/(4y^2)))` with the exact `dist >= 1/8` separation are all uniform-asymptotics discipline correctly enforced. Assessment: this area supplies exactness and falsification power, not the missing cancellation; there is no finer expansion whose next term closes an `X^(1/12)` power deficit. One structural insight it does contribute: the one-sided cones (`m <= h <= 4m`) are partial/false theta objects — no modular transformation law, only quantum-modular/Stokes structure (the repo's level-four Appell source and the R63 rejection at `|q|=1` are consistent with this). That is the exact reason one-sided cones neither self-cancel nor return to a modular object, and it predicts that any "complete the cone to a modular sum" move will keep failing; completion should be treated as permanently barred rather than re-attempted. Heat-kernel/theta-smoothing intuition (from the user's home field) makes the same point quantitatively: after Gaussian smoothing at scale delta the circle error is trivially fine; the entire problem is un-smoothing across frequencies `n ~ X^(1/2)`, which is the `D = X^(1/2)` endpoint block — sharp-cutoff information at the top frequency is the whole problem, and no asymptotic refinement of the smooth part touches it.

### 4.4 Probability theory

Known-true probabilistic facts: `P(x)/x^(1/4)` has a limiting distribution with heavy-but-decaying tails (Heath-Brown 1992, Acta Arith. 60); moments through order ~9 (Tsang PLMS 1992; Ivić–Zhai; Zhai's series); mean square `~ c T^(3/2)` (Cramér 1922); so the conjecture is true on average, in L^2, and in distribution, and the pointwise problem is exactly an exceptional-set problem. The lawful probabilistic lane for this project is therefore **large values and exceptional sets** of the concrete almost-periodic polynomial `F(t) = sum_{n ~ N} c_n e(t sqrt n)` (`t ~ X^(1/2)`, `N <= X^(1/2)`, `c_n` the actual beta/chi_4 coefficients), where frequencies `sqrt n` are well-spaced relative to `1/t` at the endpoint. Mean value in L^2 is classical; L^4 is governed by the additive energy of `{sqrt n}` — the first-spacing counts again. Two calibrated observations. First, the endpoint target is `|F| <~ N^(1/2)` — the *square-root* threshold, far below the `N^(3/4)` regime where Guth–Maynard-type large-value machinery operates; no large-value theorem can reach it (at `V ~ N^(1/2)` the level set could be everything as far as L^2/L^4 information goes). So probability cannot close GC-target; that expectation should be written down once, as a barrier note. Second, and positively: the machinery the repo already owns (AP lemma, LFM kernel, fourth-moment expansion, near-collision taxonomy) is most of an **exceptional-set theorem**: `P(x) <<_eps x^(1/4+eps)` for all `x in [T, 2T]` outside a set of measure `<< T^(1-c)` with an explicit `c` — with improvements over the Chebyshev/moment baseline coming precisely from large-value input. This is a publishable intermediate deliverable that reuses the graph instead of fighting it (check against Ivić's large-values chapters for the divisor analogue before claiming novelty). Separately, a Guth–Maynard adaptation memo for `sqrt n` phases (their energy/incidence core replaces `log n`-translation structure with `sqrt n`-dilation structure) is a legitimate research bet for **partial** exponents; as of Aug 2026 nobody has tried it in print. Label any such node `proposed`; never `derived_under_assumptions` on the strength of analogy.

### 4.5 Synthesis: what a full proof would minimally need

Assembling the walls: a proof of M9 at the endpoint needs, simultaneously, (i) explicit sign algebra on the structured resonant families (square rays first, where chi_4-offset is provably inert and cancellation must come from k-modes or mass sparsity); (ii) a fixed-X equidistribution/non-resonance input on the generic family that no averaging argument supplies; and (iii) an exceptional-set/large-value mechanism if (ii) is only provable outside a thin X-set. No combination of currently published theorems assembles into (ii). That is not a criticism of the repo — it is the reason the problem is open everywhere — but it fixes what the project can honestly expect to produce on any foreseeable horizon: barrier theorems, the signed-spacing formulation, a square-family capacity theorem, an exceptional-set theorem, possibly partial-exponent movement if the corridor machinery is retargeted. The endpoint itself should be carried as a long-horizon target conditional on a genuinely new idea in (ii).

## 5. Should the strategy change?

Partly. Keep the architecture and the discipline; change the goal gradient and retire two illusions (that BW is a live reference, and that continued normal-form surgery alone converges to the endpoint).

**Keep.** The conductor protocol, 80/20 rule, blind rederivation + hostile audit + controls, conservative labels, and the Round-77 plan itself (certify the collar-extracted actual-symbol normal form and step-two variation first) are all correct. The BW withdrawal is an object lesson that the project's audit culture is aimed at exactly the failure mode (a domination step between two displayed quantities) that killed a famous paper; do not relax it.

**Change / add** (proposed obligations, with recommended labels):

- **S-A (bookkeeping round): exponent extraction.** Compute the best unconditional `theta` such that the *current* graph proves `P(X) <<_eps X^(theta+eps)`, by running the bridge with proved envelopes in place of open targets (TTY wedge, terminal divisor bounds, R71–72 conductor ranges, `min(L^2, X^(1/4)L^(1/2))` top envelopes, trivial bounds elsewhere). My crude reading: the M2-top corridor costs at most `X^(1/12)` over target, but open M1 pieces likely leave the global yield near trivial; the point is to replace guesses with a number and a named worst corridor. Label the outcome `proved_internal` (it is bookkeeping over accepted nodes). Expectation management: the route is endpoint-specialized; matching even van der Corput `1/3`, let alone `131/416`, through this bridge would itself be news about the route.
- **S-B (source hygiene, urgent, zero mathematical risk): retire Bourgain–Watt** (card → withdrawn primary source, unusable as dependency; strike from strategy rankings) and **adjudicate the Xiao citation** (presumed nonexistent; replace with Iyer 2404.01069, Steinerberger 2401.10152). Add a standing rule: no reference enters a strategy document without an existence check recorded on a card.
- **S-C (barrier work, already ranked but never executed): the Li–Yang exact input map** at the raw Vaaler endpoint, run to its expected failure, producing a certificate of exactly which hypothesis fails (anticipated: the Case A/B parameter ranges at `H ~ M T^(-1/4)`, and output exponent 0.3145 > 1/4 regardless). - **S-D (the concrete new mathematical target): square-family resonant-mass adjudication.** Within the R76 survivor, restrict to `a = s^2, b = t^2`, `s,t` odd coprime. Exact inputs: `(sqrt b - sqrt a)^2 = (t-s)^2 in Z`; `(-1)^q = +1` identically on the family; at resonance `e(g alpha)` is constant `(-1)` on each ray; for `t = s+2` the resonance condition is `||2X/k|| <~ G^(-1)` with `k ~ Jr/L`. Task: prove or refute `sum(weighted resonant square-family mass) <=_eps L^2 X^eps` uniformly in X, treating the divisor-structured k-set exactly and the metric k-set by counting; a finite diagnostic (within the 20% cap) charting the mass against L for adversarial X (e.g. `X = T^4` families) should accompany the analytic attempt. Outcome semantics: pass → the survivor stands its most structured test and the enemy is the generic family; fail → the survivor inequality is false as stated and the route forks *now* (collar/mod-4 repair, or an exceptional-X reformulation) instead of after more rounds. Label `proposed`.
- **S-E (formulation with standalone value): the signed second-spacing lemma.** State, as a self-contained object with the actual coefficient class, the cross-ray/cross-mode cancellation statement the survivor needs, in parallel with classical BI second spacing, and record the two theorems that make its unsigned shadow false (W-1/UNC) and its coefficient-blind shadow false (adversary countermodels). Even unproved, this is the project's exportable formulation; it deserves a clean statement independent of the internal normal-form stack. Label `open` (as a target), with the formulation task `proposed`.
- **S-F (probability lane): exceptional-set theorem + GM-adaptation memo.** (i) Extract from the existing L^4/AP/LFM machinery an explicit-measure exceptional-set version of the target on `[T, 2T]`; compare against Ivić-style large-values results for novelty before promotion. Labels `proposed`.
- **S-G (controls): add the Omega-gate.** Mahatab 2025: `P(x) = Omega((x log x)^(1/4) (log log x)^c (log log log x)^(-3/8))`. Any candidate whose mechanism would produce `P(x) << x^(1/4)` with fewer logs than the Omega shape is wrong; add to `control_models.md` as a one-line test alongside the existing nine.

This costs nothing and makes round decisions honest about which target they serve.

**Process fixes** (from §1): sync the failure ledger and thresholds; fix the draft scope line and R5-Full marker; reconcile kernel-file statuses; re-express the rejected blocker; populate the gap register from the reading packet's blocker list; add the three missing timestamps.

### Bottom line

The repo is in the best epistemic state it has ever been in — one audited external dependency, one explicit finite hard object, a real barrier library — and simultaneously at its clearest impasse: the surviving core is the conjecture, the unsigned and coefficient-blind routes to it are internally refuted, and the transform orbit is provably involutive. The strategy should not be abandoned; it should be *forked*: continue the Round-77 signed-resonant-union attack with the square-family adjudication (S-D) as its first decidable sub-question, and in parallel redirect a fraction of rounds to deliverables that are provable with current technology (S-A, S-C, S-E, S-F). Expect the endpoint to remain open; insist that what the project banks along the way — barrier theorems, the signed-spacing formulation, exceptional-set results, a calibrated exponent for the route — is real, exportable mathematics either way.
