## 2. Executive decision

A2's central strategic diagnosis is correct and should be adopted:

1. **The invertible-transform toolbox has reached an involution barrier.** Poisson/completion, adjoint reciprocal transforms, dual-square stationary transforms, metaplectic functional calculus, determinant carriers, owner completion, and adjacent transport repeatedly return the hard operators at equal capacity. Another exact-transform round on a frozen core has negligible expected value.

2. **The frontier has widened.** The canonical hard cores are not sufficient parents for the full physical routes. The current direct frontier consists of two M1 estimates, three M2 estimates, and endpoint uniformity. A reduction map is needed to prevent effort from fragmenting across formally different avatars of the same signed square-root-phase difficulty.

3. **Only inequality-producing, information-losing mechanisms can now change a capacity exponent.** The acceptable mechanism classes are actual-symbol mean-square inequalities, large-value or short-window moment inequalities, sign-sensitive shifted-convolution estimates, curvature/variation estimates, and literal external theorem imports whose hypotheses match exactly.

The previous A1 strategy is therefore revised as follows.

### Selected primary route

**Round 114 should attack the balanced smooth M2 packet through the proposed diagonal-critical square-root mean square, with an exact connector gate and an actual-symbol analytic gate.**

The direct broad--narrow/ray-defect idea remains useful, but it is moved from the primary formulation to one candidate mechanism inside the analytic gate. The first task is to determine whether the existing mean-square node actually implies the literal Round-113 packet target without changing the norm, the coefficient class, the quarter-shift difference, or the gcd-shell assembly.

### Elevated secondary route

The short-window cluster problem at

\[
W=Y^{7/16}
\]

is elevated from an optional side route to the principal **graded** route. Every genuine saving in its additive local-moment term produces a bankable internal exponent below \(1/3\), even if the full \(5/16\) target is not reached.

### M1 adjustment

The canonical M1 Gram remains frozen, but the **direct physical M1 route is not frozen**. It deserves one non-core minimization round because its two physical parents, created in Round 98, have never been reduced using the full accepted R82--R104 corpus.

### Standing termination rule

After two equal-capacity outcomes on one core from the same mechanism class, that core is frozen until a new inequality-producing mechanism is named with:

- an exact statement;
- a quantitative capacity gain;
- a literal coefficient and owner map;
- a false unsigned or adversarial analogue;
- a fast falsification test.

## 5. Round 114: balanced mean-square fork

### 5.1 Frozen physical target

Fix one literal balanced smooth positive-frequency block

\[
B=(X,j,r,+),
\qquad
R=\sqrt X,
\qquad
1\le K/L\le 16,
\]

with the Round-113 coefficient dictionary. Define

\[
Z_B(R)
:=
\sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma_L}
G_\sigma Q_{B,\sigma}^{\rm full}(R).
\]

The required estimate is

\[
\boxed{
|Z_B(R)|\ll_\varepsilon L^{3/2}X^\varepsilon.
}
\tag{BP}
\]

The absolute value stays outside the complete internal gcd-shell sum. There is no cancellation between distinct physical \(D\)- or \(L\)-blocks.

The persistent geometry is \(j=1\). The \(j=2\) balanced occurrence is the isolated exact-square boundary and should remain a separate control.

### 5.2 Proposed mean-square target

The existing proposed node is

\[
\boxed{
\mathfrak M_{L,K}(X)
:=
\sum_{h\asymp L}
\left|
\sum_{k\asymp K}
 a_{L,K}(h,k)e(\sqrt{Xhk})
\right|^2
\ll_\varepsilon
L^{1/2}K^{3/2}X^\varepsilon.
}
\tag{MS}
\]

Here \(a_{L,K}\) must be the actual normalized smooth symbol, not an arbitrary bounded array. At \(K\asymp L\), the right side is \(L^2X^\varepsilon\), and Cauchy has precisely the capacity to yield an \(L^{3/2}\) linear bound.

The repository currently records (MS) as stronger than the dual \(M^{3/4}\) target because an outer Cauchy step erases \(\chi_4(h)\). It does **not** yet contain a literal proof that (MS), in its present symbol and norm, implies (BP).

### 5.3 Gate 0: exact connector

Prove or refute the following connector.

> **Balanced mean-square connector.** The actual Round-113 packet, including the \(1/4-3/4\) difference, the internal gcd-shell sum, the literal smooth symbols, and all owner corrections, can be reorganized into finitely many actual-symbol mean-square families satisfying (MS), with total coefficient \(\ell^2\) cost \(X^\varepsilon\). Consequently (MS) implies (BP).

The connector must state:

- the exact map from \((\sigma,u,v,n)\) packet atoms to \((h,k)\) mean-square rows;
- whether \(\chi_4\) is inside the mean-square vector or lost only after the final Cauchy step;
- the precise cost of the \(G_\sigma\)-weights;
- how the two quarter shifts interact under squaring;
- why high gcd, exact square, near-square, and transform errors remain with their existing owners;
- the finite subdivision and blockwise norm scope;
- endpoint and exact-square boundary conventions.

**Gate-0 success criterion:** a proof-level implication with no shellwise absolute value and no hidden cross-block cancellation.

**Gate-0 failure criterion:** the first exact seam is named and its capacity is computed. Failure is useful: it prevents work on an inapplicable stronger theorem.

### 5.4 Gate 1: false-shadow control

The arbitrary-phase strengthening should be rejected explicitly. If arbitrary unimodular phases are permitted, choosing a phase-conjugating coefficient can make the inner sums coherent and exceed the diagonal-critical target. This proves only:

- coefficient-uniform versions of (MS) are false;
- the actual Vaaler/stationary symbol is essential.

It is not a lower bound for the actual packet. This control should be recorded once and then closed.

### 5.5 Gate 2: actual-symbol estimate

If Gate 0 succeeds, attack (MS) for the actual symbol. If Gate 0 fails but exposes a closely related actual mean square, attack that exact object instead.

The analysis should split the expanded mean square into:

1. **Row diagonal.** The \(k=k'\) contribution is target-critical and must be evaluated with the actual amplitude. There is no room to discard an extra power here.

2. **Separated off-diagonal.** Apply a theorem-shaped separation or Hilbert-type inequality only after verifying the literal frequency separation and coefficient variation. A generic spacing bound that loses the slanted symbol or quarter-shift structure is not sufficient.

3. **Near-diagonal or multiplicative-collision corridor.** This is the real analytic survivor. It must retain the actual coefficient and should be attacked by one or more of the following noninvertible mechanisms.

#### Mechanism A: sampled variation plus reciprocal curvature

Rounds 103--104 prove that centred sampled-\(k\) variation and reciprocal second-derivative cancellation yield genuine estimates on fixed and prescribed-polylogarithmic \(q\)-shells. The first attempt should be to extend that mechanism to the mean-square near-diagonal package without rowwise Cauchy over polynomially many \(q\)'s.

The required output is either:

- an \(L^{2-\delta}\) saving for a positive-power shell;
- a target-safe balanced subrange;
- or a proof that the row-correlation step reintroduces the full \(D^2\) deficit.

#### Mechanism B: signed shifted-product correlation

After grouping by product or near-product variables, seek a character-twisted shifted convolution that preserves \(\chi_4\) until the final estimate. The theorem must be stated for the actual smooth coefficient, not for an unsigned divisor count.

Any use of Kuznetsov, Voronoi, delta methods, or spectral theory is `source_audit_required` until the exact modulus, weight, conductor, sign, and endpoint ranges are matched.

#### Mechanism C: broad--narrow/ray-defect decomposition

The previous A1 broad--narrow idea remains a serious Gate-2 option. For

\[
f(u,m)=\frac{u^2}{m},
\]

one has the exact identity

\[
f(u+r,m+s)-f(u,m)-\frac{2u}{m}r+\frac{u^2}{m^2}s
=
\frac{(mr-us)^2}{m^2(m+s)}.
\tag{RD}
\]

The defect

\[
\rho=mr-us
\]

measures transverse displacement from a rational ruling. A narrow branch with small \(|\rho|\) should be parameterized arithmetically with the actual characters and lift multiplicities; a broad branch should be estimated using transverse curvature. The round must first derive the literal correspondence between (RD) and the Round-113 packet or mean-square variables. Phase resemblance alone is not a connector.

### 5.6 Round-114 acceptable outcomes

The round succeeds if it produces any one of:

1. `proved_internal`: (BP), hence the balanced smooth M2 parent closes;
2. `proved_internal`: the exact Gate-0 connector and a strict power saving over the current balanced envelope;
3. `proved_internal`: a target-safe nontrivial balanced subrange with a smaller named survivor;
4. `proved_internal`: a rigorous actual-symbol obstruction or equal-capacity return for the proposed mean-square mechanism;
5. `rejected`: a clearly false coefficient-uniform strengthening, while the actual-symbol target remains `open`.

A new exact transform with unchanged capacity is not sufficient to continue this route automatically.

### 5.7 Round-114 task briefs

**Task A: blind connector rederivation.**  
Derive the implication (MS) \(\Rightarrow\) (BP) from the Round-113 dictionary without seeing the proposed construction. Stop at the first missing datum.

**Task C: hostile seam and false-shadow audit.**

**Conductor.**

  \delta>1-3\theta_{\rm LY}
