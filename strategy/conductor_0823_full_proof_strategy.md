## 1. Evaluation and corrections

The four memos agree on the decisive structural facts and on the next
research direction. The following recommendations are adopted.

1. Round 131 is a terminal internal gate on the graded top shell, not the
   start of another sequence of invertible transformations.
2. The exact determinant coordinate is the right finite interface:
   (n=aq-bp), with bounded (p)-multiplicity for a fixed primitive
   top-shell outer ray after physical Mobius reassembly.
3. The physical resonant Fourier mode may be shifted by the M1 quarter
   carriers or M2 numerator character. The naive zero mode is not the
   object to test.
4. A Fourier expansion modulo (4|a|) is legal only after a separation
   lemma identifies the genuinely periodic arithmetic factor and prices
   the variation of every threshold, taper, support, and owner envelope.
5. Cancellation confined to one outer ray cannot strictly improve the
   global exponent. Even an ideal full length-(L) within-ray gain reaches
   only (Y^{9/16}), exactly the persistence threshold. A strict global
   improvement requires cross-ray anti-alignment.
6. After the terminal gate, the program should test source-audited,
   genuinely noninvertible cross-ray mechanisms and then return to the
   endpoint conjunction.

The following memo proposals are corrected or declined.

- Per-ray exact vanishing is not a valid primary target: the accepted
  same-denominator M1/M2 packets already realize coherent per-ray modes at
  special centres. Any useful resonant estimate must include the physical
  family sum over outer rays.
- Resonant windows are a constant-order part of the per-ray budget, not a
  sparse error. A claimed lower-order resonant term must exhibit sign
  cancellation, not merely count fewer windows.
- The word "Salie" is conditional on deriving the exact complete-sum
  modulus, character, inverse phase, unit condition, and completion cost.
  Quarter phases and `chi_4` alone do not prove Salie structure.
- All recent decoupling, Kloosterman, trace-function, and large-sieve
  suggestions remain `source_audit_required`. No theorem name is an
  accepted dependency until its exact statement and project parameter map
  are recorded.
- The historical A1/A2 task-owner assignments and percentage allocations
  are not imported. The current protocol uses one conductor, temporary
  functional roles, and one frozen objective per round.
- Positive outer energies, coefficient-blind square functions, and
  (G^2) completion remain forbidden substitutes for the actual scalar.

## 2. Capacity law

At

\[
W=Y^{7/16},\qquad D=Y^{1/2},\qquad L=Y^{1/6},
\]

Round 130 leaves the top-shell capacity

\[
DK_D=Y^{35/48+o(1)}.
\]

The relevant ladder is

\[
Y^{35/48}\quad\longrightarrow\quad
Y^{31/48}\quad\longrightarrow\quad
Y^{27/48}=Y^{9/16}\quad\longrightarrow\quad
Y^{24/48}=Y^{1/2}.
\]

Here (31/48) is the generic square-root residue bank, (27/48) is
the ideal one-term-per-ray floor and persistence threshold, and (24/48)
is the determinant target. If a complete fixed-block exponent is
\(\beta\), the accepted bridge gives

\[
\Theta(\beta)=\max\left\{{7/16+\beta\over3},{\beta\over2}\right\}.
\]

Thus \(\beta<9/16\) is necessary for a new internal pointwise exponent.
If a within-ray (L)-gain reaches (27/48) and a compatible cross-ray
theorem supplies (Y^{-\eta}), then the pointwise exponent becomes
(1/3-\eta/3). This would be a real intermediate theorem, not the
quarter result.

## 3. Round 131: terminal determinant-residue resonant-mode gate

The round starts from the literal physically reassembled (B\asymp D)
scalar and must perform the following operations in order.

1. Substitute the bounded-to-one chart (n=aq-bp) into the complete
   M1/M2 coefficient without separating thresholds or Mobius pieces
   prematurely.
2. Prove a periodic/BV separation lemma. State exactly which factors are
   periodic in (n\pmod {4|a|}), which depend smoothly or by bounded
   variation on (n), and the total variation and boundary cost.
3. Compute the shifted physical resonant mode for M1 and M2, including
   reciprocal aliases and the actual outer centre phase.
4. Decide whether the periodic factor is a genuine complete
   quadratic-Gauss/Salie-class sum or remains threshold-entangled.
5. Estimate the resonant contribution after summing the actual outer-ray
   family, or construct a literal coherent family-level obstruction.
6. State the surviving nonresonant matrix coefficient before any proposed
   source-audited continuation.

The exit is exactly one of:

- `bank`: a proved literal residue or actual-family power saving, with its
  survivor and capacity stated;
- `obstruct`: a proved coherent physical resonant contribution, after
  which the graded lane is parked;
- `degenerate`: neither cancellation nor a main term is provable from the
  literal structure, with the first missing identity recorded and the
  internal graded lane parked.

No Round-131 outcome alone licenses a global exponent, M9 component, or
quarter promotion. There is no second consecutive internal per-ray
top-shell round.

## 4. Successor mechanism audits

After Round 131, the next two campaigns are hypothesis-map rounds, not
black-box theorem imports.

### 4.1 Broad/transverse source map

Map the refined graded top shell and the balanced M2 broad branch to the
relevant small-cap/high-low and hyperbolic-paraboloid decoupling statements.
Same-ray, same-denominator, and rational-ruling packets remain the narrow
arithmetic part. The audit exits with an applicable positive margin, one
exact missing hypothesis, or a barrier table.

### 4.2 Cross-ray arithmetic source map

Derive an exact Bombieri--Iwaniec double-large-sieve or modulus-aspect
Kloosterman/Salie normal form. Record both spacing problems, coefficient
norms after Stieltjes recombination, completion costs, real-centre
conditions, and the resulting power of (Y). Fixed-modulus reasoning on
the length-(D^{1/3}) numerator interval is not the default route; any gain
must arise from a moduli or outer-ray average.

Every external candidate receives a source card with theorem number,
hypotheses, absolute-value placement, parameter map, owner errors, and
capacity. A source that merely resembles the project kernel is rejected.

## 5. Full endpoint architecture

The graded lane is a mechanism laboratory and possible sub-(1/3) route.
It is not the full quarter proof. The endpoint program remains:

1. M2 UNBAL first. At round-open choose exactly one surface: the physical
   prescribed-centre wavelet for modulus-aspect dispersion, or the parked
   positive energy for a one-sided band-limited-majorant feasibility gate.
2. M2 BAL second, only through a literal broad--narrow split with aligned
   rational rulings treated arithmetically.
3. M2 TOP third, only after an actual-vector cross-ray or signed
   trace/Kloosterman mechanism excludes the accepted aligned controls.
4. M1 proceeds through the signed lower-GAR aggregate as the preferred
   parallel route; the two direct parents remain available if a genuinely
   new sign-sensitive block theorem appears.
5. Endpoint uniformity is audited only after the analytic parents close.
   `M9-M2` requires TOP, BAL, and UNBAL; `M9-M1` requires both direct
   parents or the complete GAR alternative; `M9` and `GC-target` follow
   only after their graph dependencies and the final bridge close.

## 6. Standing stop rules

Do not repeat, except as explicit hostile controls:

- independent square-root credit for (p);
- product-window reversal followed by modulus;
- sequential unit-Hessian transforms followed by aliaswise modulus;
- resolved-(G^2) or coefficient-blind outer energy;
- character periodicity without the shifted-mode and separation lemmas;
- per-ray gains advertised as cross-ray gains;
- source names without exact theorem cards;
- average or probabilistic evidence advertised as a fixed-centre theorem;
- a local shell saving advertised as a global exponent while the complete
  exponent is at least (9/16).

This strategy prioritizes the smallest remaining signed interface, forces
one terminal decision on the internal graded lane, and then turns to the
cross-ray and endpoint mechanisms that a full proof actually requires.
