## 2. Exact statement and hypotheses

Fix a persistent literal critical \(j=1\) balanced block \(B\), with
\(L\asymp K\asymp X^{1/6}\), and retain the complete coefficient
\(a_B^<(h,k)\), both character factors, both low-gcd weights, both slanted
symbols, floors, support crossings, the real centre, and the strict gates

\[
 |h'k'-hk|>L,\qquad |hk'-h'k|>L.
\]

The critical owner is exactly

\[
 \mathcal R_B^{\rm osc}
 =\sum_{\rm df}a_B^<(h,k)\overline{a_B^<(h',k')}
 \left[e\!\left(\sqrt X(\sqrt{hk}-\sqrt{h'k'})\right)-1\right],
 \qquad
 |\mathcal R_B^{\rm osc}|\ll_\varepsilon L^3X^\varepsilon.
\tag{S1}
\]

Write \(h'=h+2s\), \(k'=k+q\).  On any finite, zero-extended geometric
\(q\)-fibre \(I\), consider the full class of coefficient-adapted
first-difference representations

\[
 \sum_{q\in I} w(q)f(q)
   =\sum_{q\in\mathbb Z}c(q)\bigl(f(q)-f(q-1)\bigr)
 \quad\hbox{for every }f\hbox{ supported on }I,
\tag{S2}
\]

where \(w\) may contain any chosen literal actual-symbol, character, gate, or
phase factor.  Then (S2) holds if and only if

\[
 c(q)-c(q+1)=w(q)\qquad(q\in I).
\tag{S3}
\]

Consequently, if \(P_u=\sum_{q=u}^{b}w(q)\), with
\(I=\{a,\ldots,b\}\) and \(P_{b+1}=0\), then every such primitive is
\(c(u)=C+P_u\) on the relevant sites and

\[
 \|c\|_\infty
 \ge \frac12\operatorname {diam}\{P_a,\ldots,P_{b+1}\}
 =\frac12\max_{a\le u\le v\le b}
       \left|\sum_{q=u}^{v}w(q)\right|.
\tag{S4}
\]

Thus, in the existing positive face closure, replacing the forced constant
ramp by an \(X^\varepsilon\)-bounded actual-symbol primitive already requires
the uniform incomplete signed correlation theorem

\[
 \max_{I'\subseteq I\ \mathrm{interval}}
 \left|\sum_{q\in I'}w(q)\right|\ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon.
\tag{S5}
\]

An averaged replacement for (S5) is logically possible, but it must come with
a new signed global face inequality before any positive norm.  Neither (S5)
nor such an averaged theorem is accepted or even isolated with a complete
literal power ledger.

The distinct remaining-label owner is not (S1).  It quantifies over every
literal smooth balanced residual block with \(1\le K/L\le16\) outside the
persistent critical child and asks for

\[
 \left|\sum_GG P_G\right|
 \ll_\varepsilon L^{3/2}X^\varepsilon,
\tag{S6}
\]

including noncritical persistent \(j=1\) scales, the isolated exact-square
\(j=2\), \(K/L=16\) boundary, all endpoints, and arbitrary real \(X\).

## 3. Proof or derivation

### 3.1 Weighted first differences do not yet furnish a new gate

Expand the right side of (S2).  The coefficient of \(f(q)\) is
\(c(q)-c(q+1)\), proving (S3).  Solving this recurrence from the right endpoint
gives \(c(u)=C+P_u\).  For any two suffix sums,

\[
 |P_u-P_v|=|(C+P_u)-(C+P_v)|\le2\|c\|_\infty,
\]

which proves (S4).  Conversely, the suffix sums themselves supply a primitive,
so the issue is genuinely an incomplete-sum issue rather than a missing
algebraic normalization.

Round 171's geometric and gate faces have unweighted positive capacity
\(L^3X^\varepsilon\).  The constant primitive has height \(\asymp L\) and
restores \(L^4X^\varepsilon\).  Within that same positive closure, an
actual-symbol primitive can remove the loss only after a bound of the strength
(S5), or after a separately proved signed average that pays every geometric,
gate, gcd, alias, and endpoint face jointly.  Merely defining \(c\) by partial
sums is therefore circular as a Round-172 identity gate: the new estimate is
already the missing nonlocal correlation theorem.

This argument is deliberately limited to coefficient-adapted first
\(q\)-differences.  It does not classify arbitrary two-dimensional,
composite, Weyl, or nonlocal operators.

### 3.2 Composite/nonlocal commutators lack a frozen interface

The Round-171 kernel quarantines undisplayed backward, composite, weighted,
and nonlocal commutators.  Accordingly they remain mathematically possible.
But the audited files contain no operator \(\mathcal C\), invariant summation
domain, exact identity for (S1), singular-set owner, or term-by-term
\(L^3\) ledger for such a \(\mathcal C\).  In particular, no candidate controls
both the surviving actual-real \((++)\) endpoint-swap complement and the
ramp-weighted arithmetic/support faces before applying a positive norm.
There is therefore no exact proposition to freeze and falsify in one round.

### 3.3 Rulingwise correlation is still the whole scalar problem

The accepted broad--narrow audit gives the exact fixed-ruling parameter

\[
 Q=\frac{\mu^2h}{d^2k}=Xc^2,
\]

with

\[
 \rho=hk'\frac{c^2-1}{c^2},\qquad
 \Delta=hk\frac{(k'/k)^2-c^2}{c^2}.
\tag{S7}
\]

At \(k'=k\), one has \(\Delta=-\rho\), so fixed-\(Q\) determinant-zero
rulings persist beyond both strict corridors.  Equal-rational lifts are
carrier-coherent; their amplitudes, divisor progressions, \(J\)-conditions,
and supports remain distinct.  Hence neither gate removal nor character
cancellation disposes of the narrow class.  The smallest owner-complete
survivor remains the full signed \((h,k,k',d,\mu,J)\) scalar.  Taking a modulus
per ruling would discard precisely the unproved inter-ruling/outer-variable
cancellation, while no accepted theorem estimates the complete signed ruling
assembly.  A phrase such as "rulingwise signed correlation" therefore does
not yet specify a narrower target than the open BAL scalar.

### 3.4 The remaining-label owner cannot repair the critical route

The hypotheses of (S1) include persistent \(j=1\) and
\(L\asymp X^{1/6}\).  The universal quantifier in (S6) includes blocks for
which those hypotheses fail, most visibly the exact-square \(j=2\),
\(K/L=16\) boundary.  The authoritative graph consequently places (S1) and
(S6) as separate blockers of full BAL.  No implication in either direction is
licensed.  Moreover, the accepted double-far/phase-free/ruling reductions are
attached to the critical child, whereas the remaining-label node presently
stops at the literal packet target (S6).  Choosing it now would first require a
uniform scale-and-endpoint reduction, not merely the missing factor-\(L\)
critical estimate.

### 3.5 Comparison with K26

The graph already proves the hard-TOP residual Fejer parity/gcd/scale
reduction.  At maximal scale \(R=M_L\), all shifts below \(R_0=\lceil L\rceil\)
are paid within the \(L^3X^\varepsilon\) energy budget, and the distinct even
medium/long aggregate (165.K26) is an exact sufficient remaining statement.
K26 still has a factor-\(L\) deficit and would close only the named residual
face, not hard TOP or M9--M2.  Nevertheless it has what the BAL alternatives
currently lack: one exact owner, one exact aggregate, and one proved
downstream implication.  It therefore wins the Round-172 readiness
comparison.

## 5. Required control tests and outcomes

1. **Erased-structure ramp control — PASS.**  Put \(w(q)=1\) on an
   \(N\)-point interval.  Then the suffix-sum diameter is \(N\), and (S4)
   gives \(\|c\|_\infty\ge N/2\), exactly reproducing the Round-171 forced
   ramp.
2. **Cancellation-scope control — PASS.**  Put \(w(q)=(-1)^q\).  Its interval
   sums are bounded, so a bounded primitive exists.  Thus the argument does
   not falsely rule out coefficient-induced cancellation; it identifies the
   precise incomplete-sum theorem that would have to be proved for the actual
   weight.
3. **Ruling survival control — PASS.**  Setting \(k'=k\) in (S7) gives
   \(\Delta=-\rho\); values with \(|\rho|>L\) satisfy both strict gates.
   Therefore a proposed proof cannot delete all fixed-\(Q\) rulings as corridor
   terms.
4. **Owner-quantifier control — PASS.**  The exact-square \(j=2\),
   \(K/L=16\) label belongs to (S6) and violates the critical-\(j=1\)
   hypothesis of (S1).  Promotion of the critical child cannot promote the
   remaining-label owner or full BAL.
5. **Power/capacity control — PASS.**  The surviving \((++)\) complement and
   complete positive critical ledger remain at \(L^4X^\varepsilon\) against
   the \(L^3X^\varepsilon\) target.  No audited weighted/composite/rulingwise
   proposal supplies the missing factor before a positive norm.

All controls are exact analytic checks; no numerical experiment or external
theorem is used.
