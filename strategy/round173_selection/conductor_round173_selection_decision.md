\[

\(\chi_4(d+2s)\chi_4(d)=(-1)^s\). For the exact one-sided link weight

\[

target-safe \(L^3X^\varepsilon\) absolute capacity. The remainder is exact,
owner-complete at the link level, and unproved. Unlike fixed-product fibre
centering, this operator acts across products and shifts before any positive
norm. It therefore survives the selection no-go and is a legitimate
falsifiable gate.
