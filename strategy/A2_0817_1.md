## 0. Headline assessment

Rounds 77–89 executed the adopted strategy competently and honestly. The M2 lane ran Gates I–III to completion and terminated in a **proved mechanism no-go** (Round 80): the parity/half-frequency-gap cancellation the resonance-geometry pivot hoped to exploit is cancelled identically by a carrier already present in the complete symbol, and the survivor returns literally to the original transposed `chi_4(h)chi_4(s)` cross-row energy. The M1 lane produced one genuine conductor-range extension (Round 81, via the properly audited Bourgain 2017 exponent pair) followed by seven consecutive rounds of real but range-neutral survivor-shrinking, now approaching an aperiodic core with the same structural signature as the M2 terminal object.

Both primary lanes have therefore reached (M2) or are close to reaching (M1) their irreducible cores, and the two cores are the same *type* of object: a signed correlation carrying the actual symbol, in a regime where every unsigned, coefficient-blind, or published-template route is either internally refuted or barrier-mapped. The program's discipline has successfully **localized** the difficulty. It has not produced the missing cancellation mechanism, and the marginal peel-round now buys a smaller stratum at constant cost. Meanwhile, none of the five adopted secondary deliverables (§5 of the conductor strategy) has been executed in 13 rounds. The strategy recommendation (§5 below) is: adjudicate R89 conservatively, install a termination/fork test on the M1 filtration, bank the corridor with the exponent-extraction round, formalize the two cores as standalone exportable objects, and break the single-lane scheduling monoculture.

## 5. Strategy recommendation

The organizing fact: both primary lanes have been driven to (M2) or near (M1) irreducible signed cores of the same type, every published template has been existence-checked, hypothesis-mapped, and recorded as a barrier, and the internal falsity theorems (unsigned capacity, coefficient-blindness, involution) fence off all shortcut classes. Under these conditions the correct posture is not more peeling; it is: freeze and formalize the cores, meter any further core attacks, and convert the accumulated graph into bankable outputs. Concretely, in priority order:

**R-1 (immediate). Adjudicate R89 conservatively** per §4. One conductor session; no new mathematics required.

**R-2 (one round, before any Round 90 core attack). Install the M1 termination/fork test.** Two components. (a) *Capacity ledger:* for the current survivor (cross-group operator with `R_* > ρ_*`, `𝔞 < M^2/ρ_*^2`, `J^(87/140) < |d| < Δ_b − J^(3/4)`), record its trivial-triangle capacity against the frozen budget `X^eps (D/B) J^(14/5)` as an explicit power of `J`, and retro-fill the same number for each R82–88 deletion — the M1 analogue of the M2 `rho`-ledger (80.F). Every future deletion round must state the capacity it removed; if the last `k` rounds removed `o(gap)`, the filtration is not converging and the lane forks automatically. (b) *Self-return theorem attempt:* prove or refute that the aperiodic cross-group tensor, under the lawful moves (reversal/transposition, completion, `p^(2j)` descent), maps to itself or to the R82 coherent offset correlation at equal scale. A proof is the M1 analogue of the R80 carrier identity: it freezes M1 at a canonical normal form and is promotable as an obstruction; a refutation identifies the first genuinely new degree of freedom and directs the next attack. Either outcome ends the open-ended regress; both are round-sized.

**R-3 (one bookkeeping round, high value). Execute S-A: exponent extraction.** Compute the best unconditional `theta` with `P(X) <<_eps X^(theta+eps)` that the current graph proves end-to-end, running the accepted bridge with proved envelopes in place of the open cores (R81/82 conductor ranges, the `min(L^2, X^(1/4) L^(1/2))` top envelopes, trivial bounds elsewhere), and name the worst corridor. This number is the honest measure of 89 rounds and decides whether M1 range movement even binds globally yet. Label the outcome `proved_internal` (bookkeeping over accepted nodes). Simultaneously instantiate `GC-partial(theta)` as a real graph family (S-H) so this and all corridor work has an honest home.

**R-4 (formulation rounds; the exportable output). Formalize both cores as standalone objects.** (i) The M2 core: state 80.G — the cross-row two-character density–discrepancy energy — as a self-contained signed second-spacing lemma with the actual coefficient class, recording its two false shadows (unsigned capacity `>= c·min(D^4, MD)`; coefficient-blind adversary `a(h,m) = chi_4(h) e(-J sqrt(hm))`) and the R80 rejected-shortcut list as part of the statement's context. (ii) The M1 core: the aperiodic four-Kloosterman varying-modulus correlation with its ten-source input-map barrier table attached. These are the project's novel formulations; a clean statement plus barrier certificate is real, publishable-grade mathematics whether or not either is ever proved internally, and it is the correct interface for any future external development (e.g., if bilinear-Kloosterman technology later admits varying modulus with joint symbols, the input map is already written).

**R-5 (scheduling rule). Break the monoculture.** Of every three rounds, at most one core-attack round (the R-2 fork test, or a new-mechanism trial against 80.G that names its false analogue and survives the R80 rejected list); at least one deliverable round (S-A, then the S-F exceptional-set theorem from the existing L^4/AP/LFM machinery with an Ivić-novelty check, then the S-C Li–Yang endpoint-failure certificate); and one audit/hygiene/formulation round (R-4 statements; complete the Xiao source card — its diagonal-scale second/fourth moments for `sum e(h sqrt a)` are potential comparison or counting input for S-F and for the proposed `M9-M2-dual-square-root-spacing-mean-square` node; complete the Li–Yang card; the `R5-Full` reconciliation). The 80/20 evidence rule already permits all of this; what changes is only round allocation.

**R-6 (standing watch, near-zero cost). Literature triggers.** Two families could change the M1 core's status from outside: bilinear forms with Kloosterman sums at varying modulus / joint weights (Blomer–Pascadi program), and mixed character sums modulo prime powers (Cochrane–Granville line). A quarterly existence-and-hypothesis sweep against the frozen input-map interface suffices; no round budget.

**R-7 (hygiene batch, one maintenance pass).** Regenerate `manifests/reading_packet.md` from the active campaign (currently two rounds stale, with superseded target obligations — the exact failure mode of the Round-77A list); refresh the active-directive paragraph of `human/current_directives.md` (it still describes the post-R79 M2 objective, though M2 froze at R80 and effort moved to M1); sync the `best_proof_draft.md` scope line to Round 88; fix the stale header SHA in `state/failure_ledger.md` (header `909b828c…` vs. graph `b279e9e6…`; content is current); add the missing `last_updated_at` on `GC-target`, `M9`, `M9-endpoint-uniformity`. Add a packet-authoring rule: no derivation packet ships with unreconciled displayed constants.

**Calibration.** Expectations are unchanged from the strategy rules' R8, and R80 strengthens them: the endpoint requires a fixed-X non-resonance mechanism that no published method supplies, and one more internal mechanism class (metric parity / half-gap) is now provably unavailable. The realistic near-term wins are: the M1 fork theorem (either branch), the unconditional route exponent, the exceptional-set theorem, the two formalized core statements, and possibly further conductor-range movement if the fork resolves positively. Rounds are successful when they bank one of these — not when they move the endpoint.
