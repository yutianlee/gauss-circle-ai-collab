## Exact determinant-fibre coordinates

In the plus primitive chart

\[
d=\kappa gU,\qquad d'=g(\kappa U+2S),\qquad
m'=\kappa v,\qquad m=\kappa v+2w,
\qquad h=Sv-Uw>0,
\]

put

\[
\Delta_-:=\kappa(U-v)-2w,
\qquad
\Delta_+:=\kappa(U-v)+2S.
\]

Then

\[
g\Delta_-=d-gm,\qquad g\Delta_+=d'-gm',
\]

\[
\Delta_+-\Delta_-=2(S+w)>0,\qquad
2h=v\Delta_+ +U\Delta_- -\kappa(U^2-v^2).
\tag{195.S3}
\]

On \(P_2\), \(|g\Delta_-|\le D_L\) retains the accepted uniform
\(g=O(1)\) bound. Since \(\Delta_+>\Delta_-\), the upper failure is exactly
\(g\Delta_+>D_L\). Resolve it dyadically:

\[
E<g\Delta_+\le2E,\qquad D_L\le E\ll L.
\tag{195.S4}
\]

For fixed \((g,\Delta_-,E)\), (195.S3) is the determinant-fibre relation.
The proposed seam retains every literal endpoint, carry, mask, and phase
value as an actual coefficient vector and studies the joint signed frame in
\((\Delta_+,h)\) before any scalar modulus or orientation norm.

## False controls and stop rule

The mechanism is invalid if it also proves the unsigned, character-erased,
phase-conjugating, adversarial bounded-coefficient, or separately normed
orientation shadow. It is also invalid if it requires post-expansion scalar
masking, \(T=0\) or \(T\ge1\) deletion, \(D>D_L\), another Farey cover, a
proper submask of \(P_2\), or an arbitrary-coefficient replacement.

Stop Round 195 with a rigorous scoped no-go, without an in-round pivot, if:

1. the exact diagonal or repeated-effective-phase family has full positive
   capacity and the actual coefficient structure supplies no signed
   discrepancy;
2. endpoint, carry, birth/death, zero-extension, or mask-commutator vectors
   repay \(Y/(H_B\mathfrak m)\);
3. every exact completion or determinant transposition self-returns to the
   same positive Gram energy; or
4. the claimed gain survives an adversarial or unsigned false control.
