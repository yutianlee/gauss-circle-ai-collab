# Isolated prime arithmetic statement
Let chi4 be the real Dirichlet character modulo4 and r(n)=4 sum_(d|n)chi4(d). Let Psi be real even Schwartz. For U>0 define B_U(R)=sum_(n>=1)r(n)n^(-3/4)Psi(sqrt(n/U))cos(2pi R sqrt(n)-3pi/4). Let B_(T;p|n)(R) denote its subseries indexed by multiples of an odd prime p.
For p=1 mod4, verify or correct r(pn)+1_(p|n)r(n/p)=2r(n) and the consequence B_(T;p|n)(R)=2p^(-3/4)B_(T/p)(sqrt(p)R)-p^(-3/2)B_(T/p^2)(pR).
For p=3 mod4, derive the corresponding relation and test r(p^2 n)=r(n). Check p=2 separately.
For fixed nonnegative w compactly supported on positive reals, transform integral w(R/T)|B_(T/p)(sqrt(p)R)|^12 dR exactly. Can a moment theorem known only on R comparable to U be applied at U=T/p with a constant uniform in growing p? Derive the actual ratio between center radius and U and retain the Jacobian. Discuss what is and is not proved if p is fixed.
Finally restrict an arbitrary completely factorizable coefficient law c(n)=product_p c_p(v_p(n)) to the local split-prime sequence c_p(k)=k+1 (normalization c(1)=1). Define forward/backward local operator H on sequences h(k) by (Hh)(0)=h(1), (Hh)(k)=h(k+1)+h(k-1) for k>=1. Verify Hh=2h for h(k)=k+1. Does a polynomial filter P(H) with P(2)=0 retain this actual local arithmetic channel or annihilate it? This is a local identity question, not an assertion about a norm-bounded global operator or asymptotic wave cancellation.
