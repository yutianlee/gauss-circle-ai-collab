## Mathematical interfaces to test

### 1. Additive near-product geometry

Write \(d'=d+a\), \(m'=m+b\).  Then

\[
 db+ma+ab=r.
\tag{165.S4}
\]

Because all physical factors are \(O(L)\) and \(|r|<L\), nontrivial
solutions lie near the tangent relation \(db+ma\approx-ab\).  A useful
parameterization must be integral, multiplicity controlled, and compatible
with both even and odd complementary factors.  It must not replace the
literal coefficient by a raw solution count.

The primary discovery task should test gcd separation of the linear
Diophantine equation for \(m,m'\), determinant/tangent coordinates,
and any exact partition into genuinely short increments versus long
hyperbola slides.  Every partition must recombine to (165.S1) with the
Fejer weight and aggregate real part intact.

### 2. Character-bearing shifted convolution

\(\chi_4(d')\chi_4(d)\) on the additive relation (165.S3).  Test whether
one may sum a complete residue progression, a primitive gcd class, or a

character by itself is not a saving; the proof must identify an actual
long character sum or an exact sign-reversing pairing that preserves the
product shift and literal profiles.

Möbius opening of squarefreeness or coprimality is allowed only if its
complete signed aggregate and restored lcm scales are retained.  The
Round-162 positive collar is a control, not a substitute.

### 3. Square-root phase and resonant centres

The exact phase is

\[
 J(\sqrt{N+r}-\sqrt N)
 =\frac{Jr}{\sqrt{N+r}+\sqrt N}.
\tag{165.S5}
\]

Test summation in \(r\), in the base row \(N\), and in any integral tangent
parameter.  All derivative estimates must be uniform over the inherited
real centre and must classify near-integral first derivatives, stationary
or transition ranges, and endpoints.  A large real derivative is not
automatically oscillatory on the integer lattice.

The Fejer energy is nonnegative, so only an upper bound for the completed
aggregate real part is needed.  A proof may use cancellation between
different shifts; it need not and should not prove a shiftwise modulus
bound unless that stronger power ledger is independently target-safe.

### 4. Scalar connector versus energy connector
