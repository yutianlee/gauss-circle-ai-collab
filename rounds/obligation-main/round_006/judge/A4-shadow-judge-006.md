## Selected main route

Primary route (unchanged in target, sharpened in scope): the conditional bridge
$$
\text{H1--H3} + \text{H4} + \text{R5-Full} + \text{M9} \Longrightarrow P(X)\ll_\epsilon X^{1/4+\epsilon},
$$
with the live bottleneck `M9-M2`: bound
$$
S_2(D;X)=\sum_{1\le|h|\le H_D}\beta_{h,H_D}\sum_{d\asymp D}w_D(d)\,e\!\left(\tfrac{hX}{4d}\right)\ll_\epsilon X^{1/4+\epsilon},\qquad H_D\asymp DX^{-1/4},
$$
uniformly for $X^{1/4}\le D\le X^{1/2}$, with $\beta_h=\alpha_{h,H_D}C_h$, $C_h=e(h/4)-e(3h/4)=2i\chi_4(h)\mathbf 1_{2\nmid h}$ retained.

The decisive Round-6 finding is negative and re-scopes the route rather than advancing a bound. A4's subcoherence-window analysis (confirmed independently by A1's endpoint calculation and by A2, and re-derived by me) shows that on the coherence window $\delta=X^{1/2}/D$ every fourth-moment tuple has frequency $|\lambda|=|N|/(4\Pi)\le H_D/D\asymp X^{-1/4}$, hence
$$
\delta|\lambda|\ \le\ c_H\,X^{1/4}/D\ \le\ c_H\qquad\text{for all active }D,
$$
so the local averaging multiplier $|\operatorname{sinc}(\pi\lambda\delta)|$ admits no power decay anywhere in the active range (at $D\asymp X^{1/4}$, $\delta|\lambda|$ can be $O(1)$, giving at worst a constant multiplier, while at $D\asymp X^{1/2}$, $\delta|\lambda|=O(X^{-1/4})$ and the sinc multiplier is near $1$). Combined with the drift bound $\sup_I|S_2'|\ll H_D$, i.e. window drift $\ll H_D\delta=X^{1/4}$, the local fourth moment `(LFM)` at threshold $X^{1+\epsilon}$ is equivalent at target precision to the pointwise `M9-M2` bound. Consequence for route selection: `(LFM)` is not a weaker intermediate target; the averaging bridge cannot buy the endpoint. The two viable secondary routes are therefore (a) global $2k$-th moment plus large-value propagation for the sub-endpoint band $D\le X^{1/2-\eta}$, and (b) a signed dual sum / sign-preserving discrepancy (SPD) estimate that keeps $\chi_4(h)$, which for the endpoint block $D\asymp X^{1/2}$ becomes a character-twisted divisor-type exponential sum in Bombieri--Iwaniec / Li--Yang territory and is gated on the Li--Yang source audit.

Backup route this round: A4's signed square-wave/SPD statistic and the graded global near-collision bound, not a black-box Li--Yang import.

## Known gaps

- The central oscillatory sum $S_2(D;X)$ is unbounded at the target scale. The `(LFM)`/AP averaging route is now known to be equivalent to this pointwise bound, so it supplies no reduction.
- The endpoint block $D\asymp X^{1/2}$ is a genuinely polynomial-phase regime: with $d=\lfloor X^{1/2}\rfloor+m$, the phase $X/(4d)$ carries a period-4 linear term (meshing with $\chi_4$), a quadratic term $m^2/(4X^{1/2})$, and non-negligible cubic terms. No elementary mechanism reaches it; it lies outside every fixed moment order under the (unproven) diagonal-floor hypothesis $\tfrac1X\int|S_2|^2\gg D$.
- A4's moment-hierarchy range map depends on the unproven global moment bounds $G_{2k}\ll X^{1+\epsilon}D^k$ and the diagonal-floor lower bound; it is a conditional route map, `diagnostic_only`.
- U-2/SS/anti-paired provide an upper bound for the union of $\{q,q,q',q'\}$ patterns, not a partition; the overlap with denominator-level families (denominator-paired, pair-swapped) is not accounted, and one bookkeeping pass is required before the $D^2X^\epsilon$ budget is certified.
- NF-2 is accepted only if the verbatim algebraic derivation is placed in the lemma bank; the 50000-case randomized check is diagnostic, not proof.
- `H4` remains `source_audit_required`: `sources/vaaler_1985.md` is not yet physically committed with Theorem 6 (2.28), Section 7 (7.1)--(7.3), Theorem 18 (7.13)--(7.17), coefficient sign, Fejer normalization, residual constant, endpoint convention, and the $\Phi$ regularity fact. ## New lemmas to add

L1 (subcoherence-window multiplier, `proved_internal`). For fourth-moment tuples with $|h_i|\le H:=c_HDX^{-1/4}$, $\Pi=d_1d_2d_3d_4\asymp D^4$, $|N|\le 4HD^3$, so $|\lambda|=|N|/(4\Pi)\le H/D\asymp X^{-1/4}$ and $\delta|\lambda|\le c_HX^{1/4}/D\le c_H$ on windows $\delta=X^{1/2}/D$ for every active $D$; hence the coherence-window multiplier admits no power decay. This whole-range statement subsumes A1's local kernel and A1's endpoint-degeneracy (the latter is the $D\asymp X^{1/2}$ extreme, marginal only at $D\asymp X^{1/4}$).

L2 (`(LFM)`-pointwise equivalence, `derived_under_assumptions`).
