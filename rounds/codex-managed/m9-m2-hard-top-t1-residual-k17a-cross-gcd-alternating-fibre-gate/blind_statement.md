# Round 176 statement-only packet

Let \(J=\sqrt X\), \(1\ll L\ll H\le J^{1/2}\),
\(R_0=\lceil L\rceil\), \(0<\gamma<1\), and

\[
 R_{\log}=\min\{R_0-1,\lfloor(\log X)^{100}\rfloor\}.
\]

For squarefree \(N=dm\asymp L^2\), with \(d\) odd, let

\[
 u_L(d,m)=\chi_4(d)\lambda_N(d)e(J\sqrt N),
\]

where \(|\lambda_N(d)|\ll1\) and \(\lambda_N(d)\) retains a normalized
half-open product shell, a squarefree row, a row-dependent neither/both or
no-pair selector, two parity branches, smooth profiles, finitely many hard
point values, endpoints, and zero extension. No regularity of the arithmetic
selector may be assumed.

Open both endpoints at \(N=dm\) and \(N+r=d'm'\). The frozen target is

\[
 \Re\sum_{\substack{R_{\log}<r<R_0,\ 2\mid r\\
 (d'-d)(m'-m)<0,\ (d,d')<\gamma L}}
 \left(1-{r\over R_0}\right)
 u_L(d',m')\overline{u_L(d,m)}
 \ll_{\gamma,\varepsilon}L^2X^\varepsilon.
\tag{176.B1}
\]

The real part is outside the complete variable-shift and incidence sum.
The coefficient-independent incidence capacity is
\(L^3X^\varepsilon\), so a factor \(L\) of signed cancellation is needed.
The omitted sector \(r\le R_{\log}\) is already target-safe.

Starting only from this packet, independently derive a multiplicity-one
parameterization that exposes any exact character oscillation along a
variable-determinant fibre. Test whether that oscillation, kept jointly over
all non-polylogarithmic determinants before every modulus, proves (176.B1),
an owner-complete strict sector, or an exact first no-go. Retain both opposing
orientations, the original divisor-gcd cutoff, every selector and arithmetic
support value, the Fejer factor, the square-root phase, endpoints, and zero
extension.

Required false controls are: constant-amplitude fibres, short fibres,
squarefree Möbius progressions, selected and no-pair rows, arbitrary-support
and dechirped arrays, rowwise summation by parts, rowwise Poisson or
van der Corput followed by absolute dual-mode summation, and canonical
parameterization jumps. A control may falsify a proof mechanism but is not a
lower bound for the literal aggregate.

card, sibling Round-176 report, conductor analysis, or proposed state effect.
Return only a seven-section report with exact statement, derivation, first
