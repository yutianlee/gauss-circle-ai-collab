# Conductor exhaustion-order audit

Campaign: `m9-m1-beta-endpoint-free-axial-limit`  
Role: conductor limit-seam analysis  
Allocation: 100% analytical/algebraic

The accepted finite-box hard-top lemma fixes the first limit. For a complete
smooth numerator (F) on the exact ((\mu,\nu)) rectangle,

\[
 \lim_{a\downarrow0}
 \int_{-V}^{V}\int_{-U}^{U}
 \frac{F(\mu,\nu)}{a+i\mu}\,d\mu\,d\nu
 =\pi\int_{-V}^{V}F(0,\nu)\,d\nu
  -i\int_{-V}^{V}{\rm PV}\!\int_{-U}^{U}
    \frac{F(\mu,\nu)}{\mu}\,d\mu\,d\nu.          \tag{37.E1}
\]

Thus a lawful nested order begins:

1. keep finite (U,V,S) and the fixed-(w) common complement;
2. take the symmetric top (a\downarrow0) limit distributionally in
   (mu), retaining the exact polytope;
3. use finite Cauchy--Green/Stokes identities to recombine any optional
   outside-axis faces, axes, and connectors into the corresponding complete
   positive-line block before estimating a tail;
4. exhaust (U,V) under a stated symmetric rule;
5. take (S>(U+V)/2+2B_0) so beta radial-side cells are already zero;
6. take only the already licensed physical height/spatial inversions, with
   stars produced at this last stage.

On changing to (L=\mu+\nu), the finite section is

\[
 I_{U,V}(L)=[-V,V]\cap[L-U,L+U].                  \tag{37.E2}
\]

At a moving face the sectioned kernel has a logarithmic singularity, but
it is locally (L^p) for every finite (p). Hence a fixed-section
pointwise derivative blowup does not by itself obstruct (37.E1). Conversely,
(37.E1) gives no uniform (V\to\infty) tail theorem for the actual
omega-recombined vector amplitude after the remaining (s,u,j,h,q,x)
operations. Polynomial decay of one isolated profile is insufficient unless
the other factors have a uniform integrable majorant on the same positive
line.

The smallest likely survivor is therefore a complete signed positive-line
height tail, not an individual horizontal side and not the local moving-face
logarithm. A Round-37 promotion requires either an exact cancellation of
that tail or a uniform majorant/BV theorem preserving the actual character,
profiles, floors, and normalization.

No numerical experiment or external theorem was used.
