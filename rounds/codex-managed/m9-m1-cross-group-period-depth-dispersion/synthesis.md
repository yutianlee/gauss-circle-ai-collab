\[

## Masked-period routing and good-prime deletion

The local period must be computed from the complete reciprocal masked
weight. If its exact period modulo \(p^\nu\) is \(p^{\nu-j}\), then its
Fourier transform is supported on \(p^j\mid u\), while the completed
trace descends with the exact factor \(p^{2j}\). This is an algebraic
routing identity; Fejer rescaling prevents it from being an automatic
power gain.

For \(p\geq11\), \(p\nmid K\), and a nonempty mask, reciprocal-period
rigidity with \(a=\min(j,\nu-j)\) gives

\[
 p^a\mid(B-A),\qquad p^a\mid AV,qquad
 p^a\mid AV(V+B).
\]

Writing \(\mathfrak a\) for the product of these good-prime depths, the
remaining directed physical-edge graph has in- and out-degree at most
\(X^\varepsilon M^2/\mathfrak a\). Hence, after the coarse deletion,

\[
 \mathfrak a\geq M^2/\rho_*^2
\]

is also target-safe.
