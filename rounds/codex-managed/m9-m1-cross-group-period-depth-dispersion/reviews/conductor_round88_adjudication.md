# Round 88 conductor adjudication

## Decision

Promote the two scoped cross-group deletions and the exact masked-depth
routing identities. Retain the full Round-88 target, M9-M1, M9-M2, M9,
and the global exponent open.

## Promoted kernel

After the accepted Round-87 same-group package and the once-owned
\(u=0\) diagonal are removed, let \(R_*\) be the first coarse
physical-group coincidence quotient and put

\[
 \rho_*=min\!\left(M,
 \left\lfloor J^{11/30}B^{-2}\right\rfloor\right).
\]

The union \(1<R_*\leq\rho_*\) is target-safe:

\[
 |\mathcal G_{\rm coarse}(D)|
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon
 DB^3\rho_*^2T^4Q^{-5/6}
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon {D\over B}J^{14/5}.
\]

Among the remaining complete fibres, define the good-prime
reciprocal-period conductor \(\mathfrak a\) as in the masked-period
review. The subaggregate

\[
 \mathfrak a\geq M^2/\rho_*^2
\]

is target-safe by the directed-degree estimate. Exact local period
support and the \(p^{2j}\) completed-trace descent are accepted as
routing identities, not as an independent power saving.

## Exact survivor

The first-band hard survivor has

\[
 R_*>\rho_*,\qquad
 \mathfrak a<M^2/\rho_*^2,
\]

and retains all affine/full-phase cancellation, small-prime masked
periods, nonunit-\(K\) and \(2\)-adic conductor drops, projection-only
depth, and aperiodic local traces. It still carries the complete
fourfold stationary symbol, both signs, modulus multiples, Ramanujan
centering, exact deep support, and \(Q^{-5/12}\) row gain.

## Rejected shortcuts

- Numerator coefficients do not determine masked period depth.
- A completed trace descends with \(p^{2j}\), not \(p^j\).
- Sparse local Fourier support does not reduce Fejer mass by itself.
- Coarse divisor-lattice shells do not classify all functional periods.
- Coefficientwise square-root trace bounds fail on exact small-prime
  lower-conductor returns.

## Downstream scope

This is a strict internal reduction of the first M1 conductor band. It
does not close that band uniformly, does not extend the conductor range,
and does not address M2. The global exponent is unchanged.
