# Round 90 conductor normalization review

Let \(L=TQ^{-5/24}\).  The row, ordered-pair, and four-row powers are
respectively

\[
 L,\qquad L^2=T^2Q^{-5/12},\qquad
 L^4=T^4Q^{-5/6}.
\]

The Round-82 triangle capacity and target are

\[
 \mathsf C_{82}=B^3L^2,qquad
 \mathsf T_{82}=J^2/T=J^{7/5}.
\]

For maximum directed degree \(\Delta\), the deep Gram capacity is

\[
 \mathsf C_{\rm deep}(\Delta)=DB^3\Delta L^4.
\]

At full degree \(\Delta=B^2\),

\[
 \mathsf C_{\rm deep}={D\over B}\mathsf C_{82}^2,
 \qquad
 \mathsf T_{\rm deep}={D\over B}\mathsf T_{82}^2.
\]

The Fejer/Cauchy prefactor \(B/D\) therefore gives

\[
 \sqrt{B/D}\,\mathsf C_{\rm deep}^{1/2}=\mathsf C_{82},
 \qquad
 \sqrt{B/D}\,\mathsf T_{\rm deep}^{1/2}=\mathsf T_{82}.
\]

Equivalently,

\[
 \Gamma_{82}=B^3J^{-11/30},\qquad
 \Gamma_{\rm deep}=B^6J^{-11/15}=\Gamma_{82}^2.
\]

At \(B=J^{3/20}\), the two numbers are \(J^{1/12}\) and
\(J^{1/6}\).  They occur at different norm levels.  Treating the
energy-space \(J^{1/6}\) as an additional linear loss would count the
A-process squaring twice.

The \(M\)-normalization is also consistent.  Two normalized ordered
pairs give \(M^{-4}\); the completed convention retains its outer
base-point factor, so

\[
 M^{-5}\mathfrak T_M=M^{-4}\sum_x.
\]

No extra \(M^{-2}\) or \(Q^{-5/12}\) is available.  The normalization
gate passes.
