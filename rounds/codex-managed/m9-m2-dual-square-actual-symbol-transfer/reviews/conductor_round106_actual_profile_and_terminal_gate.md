## Potential separations

The smooth factors in (106.S2) are ratios and admit candidate Mellin
separation, but the sharp maximal condition (106.S4), prior-owner masks
and congruence \(M\equiv0\pmod{dn}\) remain. Any successful use of the
terminal theorem must first prove:

- an exact prefix decomposition of (106.S4) with no power loss;
- uniform BV after all Mellin modes;
- a lawful rescaling or extension of the denominator range;
- exact one-count ownership of the removed square, exact-centre and
  boundary pieces.

Without these lemmas, citing the terminal theorem would be circular or
out of range.
