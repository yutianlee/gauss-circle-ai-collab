# Conductor review: exact owner conjugation and interpolation gate

Campaign: m9-m2-dual-square-actual-symbol-transfer

Round: 106

## Exact discrete statement

Let \(P_E\) be multiplication by one literal owner on the finite
zero-extended \((u,k)\)-array. At the exact Fourier/Poisson stage,

\[
 \widetilde P_E=\mathcal F P_E\mathcal F^{-1}.
\]

Consequently

\[
 \widetilde P_E^2=\widetilde P_E,\qquad
 \widetilde P_E^*=\widetilde P_E,\qquad
 \|\widetilde P_E\|_{2\to2}=\|P_E\|_{2\to2},
\]

and inverse Fourier transform returns the owner exactly. Complements
and disjoint one-count projectors remain complements and disjoint
projectors. A profile multiplier \(M_w\) obeys the same conjugation and
return identity without being idempotent.

The maximal interval already illustrates the issue: its dual action is a
Dirichlet kernel, not the point condition \(b_*\in a+2I\). Sparse square
owners, floors, stars, exact centres, lift supports, reciprocal entry and
exit, hard samples, and prior owner complements likewise become nonlocal
kernels.

## Nonintegral-saddle obstruction

In general

\[
 b_*={4d^2Xn\ell\over r^2}
\]

is not an integer. Therefore evaluating a literal integer owner at
\(b_*\) is not canonical. Two smooth cardinal extensions agreeing on
every integer may differ by

\[
 \sin(\pi v)\psi(v,t,y).
\]

They define the same primal discrete sum but different pointwise values
at a generic stationary \(v_*\). The difference is cancelled only by
the other exact dual integrals. Thus a pointwise owner in a truncated
stationary main term is not an invariant of the original sum.

## Scope of self-return

The exact Fourier conjugation above is an identity and is norm preserving.
It does not prove that a leading stationary truncation is owner
preserving. Such a statement would first have to restore the complete
Fresnel transitions, equality saddles, nonstationary tails, lift and
reciprocal endpoints, maximal endpoints, and every owner Fourier kernel
with a target-sized error. No such theorem is presently accepted.

Accordingly the smallest literal survivor is the nonlocal conjugated
actual-owner kernel acting on the complete-Fresnel dual vector. This is
equivalent in difficulty to the signed nonzero-shift actual Gram; it is
not an extra estimate.

## State recommendation

Promote the exact owner-conjugation/interpolation route obstruction. Do
not call the scalar stationary formula a complete owner-preserving
transform and do not infer an actual lower bound.
