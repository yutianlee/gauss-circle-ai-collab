## 4. Conditional assembly

Let TOP be the canonical hard density-discrepancy theorem and its
accepted top-transform bridge; let BAL and UNBAL be the two displays
above with every literal decoration retained. Then the physical owner
table proves

\[
 \boxed{\mathrm{TOP}+\mathrm{BAL}+\mathrm{UNBAL}
 \Longrightarrow\mathrm{M9\!-\!M2}.}
\]

Only logarithmically many labels remain, and real-\(X\) floors,
endpoint stars, signs, support crossings, and transform errors each
have one owner.

The canonical theorem alone is therefore insufficient.
