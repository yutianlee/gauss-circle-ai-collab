# Statement-only Round 152 problem

Let $N$ be a positive integer, $R\asymp N^{1/4}$, and let
$1\ll M\le R^2$.  Let $W_M$ be a compactly supported smooth function on
positive reals, supported on a fixed dilation of $[M,2M]$, with the
normalized derivative, transition-width, stationary-buffer, and tail
bounds needed for uniform Abel summation and one-dimensional stationary
phase.  The literal boundary convention and zero extension are part of
the data.  Define

$$
 P(W_M)=\sum_{\ell>0,\ \ell\operatorname{odd}}
 \chi_4(\ell)\ell^{-3/4}W_M(\ell)e(\sqrt{N\ell}).
$$

Bounded $M$ and the range $M^{819}\gg R^{1424}$ have separate accepted
owners.  In the remaining growing range, decide whether

$$
 |P(W_M)|\ll_\varepsilon N^\varepsilon
$$

can be proved for the stated actual-profile class.  Derive exact adjacent
odd pairing, one legal differencing step, the split $\ell=ts^2$, exact
square-product resonances, any Mellin or functional-equation transform,
and all endpoint and $R,M$ powers.  If the target is not obtained, state
the first precise character-loss, resonance, transform-self-return,
hypothesis, or power obstruction.  Do not treat an absolute capacity as a
signed lower bound and do not infer any result beyond this scalar.
