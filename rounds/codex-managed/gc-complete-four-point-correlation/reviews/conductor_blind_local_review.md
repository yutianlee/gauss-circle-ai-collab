## 3. Proof and independent checks

The exact mixed second difference has derivative
(3R/8)integral_0^h integral_0^k(t+u+v)^(-5/2), and its next derivative
has coefficient-15R/16. Thus phi'~Lambda/N and phi''<<Lambda/N^2 on
the entire support. These formulas remain valid at h=k.

Partition into O(1+Lambda) consecutive real cells of length at most ell.
Each reset primitive contributes its right-end boundary term as well as
its integral against the derivative of W e(phi). Both together cost
N^eta ell^nu per cell, because ell*Lambda/N<=1 and ell/N<=1.
All interior boundary terms must be retained. This gives exactly
N^(eta+nu)(1+Lambda)^(1-nu). A partition having arbitrarily many tiny
cells cannot be substituted with the same bound.

One integration by parts gives the complete smooth mean integral bound
T^epsilon N/(1+Lambda); summing h,k costs at most two harmonic logarithms
and yields NT^(-1+6b). No small products or repeated shifts are deleted.
The exact ceilings give H=L-1~N^(1/2)T^-2b and J=K-1~NT^-4b even
when they stay equal to1. Their largest product has qHJ~T^(1-6b),
q=T/N^(3/2). A positive proportion of shift pairs lies at that product
scale, so its use in the discrepancy term introduces no hidden power loss.

At N=T^(1-2b), the discrepancy exponent is
1-6b+4b nu+(1-2b)eta. Comparing it to4b gives the stated criterion.
