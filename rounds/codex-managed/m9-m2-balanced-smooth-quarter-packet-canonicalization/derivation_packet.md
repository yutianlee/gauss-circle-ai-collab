## Accepted starting point

parents: the hard density-discrepancy energy, the balanced smooth signed
packet, and the unbalanced smooth three-quarter packet. This round treats
only the balanced smooth parent. It is a deliverable/canonicalization
round, not a new speculative core attack.

After exact-square, \(R^{-1}\)-near-square, and
\((h,k)\ge L^{1/2}\) owners, the accepted balanced reduction has

\[
 \mathcal T_{\mathrm{small}}^+
 ={1\over2i}\sum_{G<L^{1/2}}G\mathcal Q_G(R),
\tag{112.1}
\]

\[

 \ll_\varepsilon L^{3/2}X^\varepsilon.

- Cauchy in the wrong variable erases \(\chi_4\).
- A symmetric complementary-divisor pairing does not cancel the
  \(m\equiv1\pmod4\) sector and is unavailable for even products.
- Exact squares, one squarefree ray, and large gcd are already target-safe
  and are not the residual.
- The hard endpoint profile is not covered by smooth gcd Poisson.

  \(X^{1/12}\) worst deficit and do not prove (112.2).
- Arbitrary or phase-conjugating coefficients are controls, not lower
  bounds for the actual packet.
