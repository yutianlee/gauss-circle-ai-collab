# Round 80 synthesis

## Objective and outcome

Round 80 asked whether the residual nonsquare strict-metric blocks

\[
 AJD^3\gg L^3,
 \qquad 0<\|\Lambda/k\|\asymp R^{-1},
\]

could be closed by nearest-integer quotient parity, a period-one metric
Fourier gap, or a signed primitive-ray fiber argument while retaining the
complete Round-77 coefficient.

They cannot be closed by those mechanisms.  All three independent tasks
found the same exact carrier cancellation.  This is promoted as a route
obstruction; no estimate or hard subrange is promoted.

## Exact result

For \(\theta=\Lambda/k\), the complete coefficient factors as

\[
 \mathfrak B^\circ(g)=e(g\theta/2)\mathfrak C^\circ(g),
\]

where

\[
 \mathfrak C^\circ(g)
 =g\int A^\circ(gu)
 e\!\left(g\left[ku-J(\sqrt b-\sqrt a)\sqrt u\right]\right)du.
\]

Thus, if \(\theta=\ell+\eta\),

\[
 (-1)^{q+\ell}\mathfrak B^\circ(g)e(-g\eta/2)
 =(-1)^q\mathfrak C^\circ(g).
\]

The quotient sign \((-1)^{p/k}=(-1)^\ell\) cancels pointwise.  The same
carrier shifts the apparent half-integral Fourier frequencies back to
integers, including the mean \(r=0\).  Hence the density term survives.

With \(h=ga\), \(s=gb\), and \(x=gu\),

\[
 \mathfrak C^\circ(g)
 =\int_{s/4}^{h}A^\circ_{h,s}(x)
 e\!\left(kx-J(\sqrt s-\sqrt h)\sqrt x\right)dx,
\]

and \((-1)^q=\chi_4(h)\chi_4(s)\).  The full zero mode is therefore
the original residual transposed two-character energy under adjoint
Poisson.  The transformed object is a literal self-return.

## Capacity and survivor

The best accepted positive/Abel ledger on one hard block remains

\[
 L^2X^\varepsilon
 \sqrt{AJD^3/L^3},
\]

which is too large whenever the hard ratio is unbounded.  No new
polynomial range is obtained.

The smallest surviving object is the complete one-count cross-row kernel
whose metric window is decomposed into its mean and nonzero integer modes.
Both must be estimated jointly.  Its only explicit sign is the original
\(\chi_4(h)\chi_4(s)\); near-square Pell rays show that primitive
\(q\)-parity need not alternate.

cancellation and metric-parity self-return.  Add it as evidence and a
scope barrier to the top-M2 signed cone.  Reject all claims that quotient
parity, a half-frequency gap, discrepancy alone, transferred variation,

Keep the signed cone, transposed-energy estimate, \(M9\!-!M2\),
