 \mathcal F_{M,H_0}[A]

## Exact lattice algebra and the selector correction

The phase product defect is

$$
 \mathcal N=p(k+h)-qk,
\qquad
 \Theta=\sqrt M\frac{\mathcal N}
 {\sqrt{p(k+h)}+\sqrt{qk}}.
\tag{124.R7}
$$

On fixed active interiors, \(\mathcal N=0\) if and only if
\(p=q,h=0\).  It is not the numerator of the alias gradient.  The latter
is

$$
 \mathcal G=pk-q(k+h)=\mathcal N-(p+q)h,
$$

and

$$
 \partial_k\Theta
 =\frac{\sqrt M\,\mathcal G}
 {2\sqrt{k(k+h)}\{\sqrt{pk}+\sqrt{q(k+h)}\}}
 =M\left(\frac1{r_*}-\frac1{s_*}\right).
\tag{124.R8}
$$

For an integer \(k\)-dual label \(j\), the continuous physical defect is

$$
 E_*(j)=M(s_*-r_*)-jr_*s_*
       =r_*s_*\{\partial_k\Theta-j\}.
\tag{124.R9}
$$

Hence every exact \(k\)-saddle lies on \(E_*=0\), even when
\(\mathcal N\ne0\).  The live physical \(E\ne0\) condition is a coupled,
discontinuous collar in \((r,s)\); inserting it before two rowwise Poisson
formulae is invalid.  Formula (124.R5), not a transported projector, is the
accepted connector.

## Principal joint return and capacity scope

On a fixed smooth \((k,h)\)-interior,

$$
 \det\nabla^2_{k,h}\Theta
 =-\frac{M\sqrt{pq}}
 {16(k+h)^{3/2}k^{3/2}}<0.
\tag{124.R10}
$$

For the Fourier phase \(\Theta-jk-dh\), the stationary equations give

$$
 r_*=M/d,qquad s_*=M/(d-j),qquad
 k_*=\frac{Mq}{4(d-j)^2},qquad
 h_*=\frac M4\left(\frac p{d^2}-\frac q{(d-j)^2}\right),
\tag{124.R11}
$$

and the Legendre phase is

$$
 \Theta-jk-dh=jk+dh
 =\frac M4\left(\frac p d-\frac q{d-j}\right).
\tag{124.R12}
$$

This is the primal reciprocal phase at the ideal selectors.  The Hessian
factor cancels the \(k\)-powers in the first stationary amplitude and
leaves \(4C_H(H-|h_*|)/(pq)\) after the character coefficient.  The later
operation therefore returns the primal phase at principal-symbol level;
Hessian rank alone creates no saving.

The nominal fixed-interior capacities are \(D^2\) for all \((p,q)\) and
\(D^2/L\) after either retaining only the formal \(p=q\) part or granting
square-root cancellation across the \(L^2\) character pairs.  Since

$$
 \frac{D^2}{L\sqrt X}\to\infty,
\tag{124.R13}
$$

these diagnose the failure of triangle, coefficient-blind energy, and
random-sign credit.  They are not signed lower bounds, and
\(p=q,h\ne0\) is not a termwise modulus-square diagonal.  No theorem here
rules out every possible joint norm.

The complete \((k,h)\) transform is not promoted.  It still needs the
triangular Fejer cusp, \(|h|=H_0\) faces, literal \(k\)-entries and exits,
stationary-boundary transitions, and all nonstationary modules.  The
strict-interior phase return is enough only to park the proposal that
joint Hessian nondegeneracy or product-defect equality itself supplies the
missing power.
