# Round 78 synthesis

Round 78 closes the algebraically structured square/common-squarefree
subfamily of the accepted top-\(M2\) primitive-ray survivor.  The exact
parametrisation is

\[
 a=s^2,\qquad b=t^2,\qquad t=s+2u,\qquad
 (s,u)=1,\qquad 1\leq u<s/2,
\]

and for an odd lift \(g=2n+1\) the two reciprocal phases combine to
\(e(-gXu^2/k)\) on
\[
 \frac{Ju}{s}<k<\frac{2Ju}{t}.
\]

The decisive new local theorem is sampled total \(k\)-variation for the
complete smooth-collar integral:

\[
 \sup_k|\mathfrak B^\circ_{s^2,t^2,k}(g)|
 +\operatorname{Var}_k\mathfrak B^\circ_{s^2,t^2,k}(g)
 \ll_\varepsilon
 X^\varepsilon\sqrt{\frac{gt^2}{K}},
 \qquad K=\frac{Ju}{s}.
\]

It follows from the exact quadratic Gaussian multiplier with the literal
\(W\)-profiles, \(q_X\), normalized \(\eta/\Phi\) factor, and both fixed
physical collars.  A pointwise logarithmic \(k\)-derivative bound is
false at a collar crossing, but each smooth collar is traversed only once
and contributes one endpoint-sized variation packet.

The outer reciprocal phase has
\[
 \left|\frac{d^2}{dk^2}\left(-\frac{gXu^2}{k}\right)\right|
 \asymp\frac{gt^2}{K}\ll1.
\]
Weighted van der Corput gives \(O_\varepsilon((L+1)X^\varepsilon)\)
for each fixed \((s,u,g)\).  Since the number of actual square lifts is
\(O(L\log(2+L))\), the exact signed square contribution satisfies
\[
 |\mathcal S_L^\square(X)|
 \ll_\varepsilon L^2X^\varepsilon.
\]
All exact and metric reciprocal resonances are included.

The corresponding absolute Abel majorant is sharply too large:
\[
 \mathcal M_L^\square(X)
 \asymp_{X^\varepsilon}\sqrt J\,L^{3/2}
\]
for the standard fixed support with a positive terminal interior box.
In particular, a fixed populated \((9,11)\) ray along fourth powers
already gives \(\gg X^{1/4}\) at fixed \(L\).  Thus absolute raywise
Abel summation is rejected; its failure does not obstruct the signed
theorem.
