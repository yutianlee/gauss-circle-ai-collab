## Decision

Promote the unconditional one-third theorem and its two supporting
lemmas. Repair and promote the R5 nodes. Keep every one-quarter target
open.

The accepted mathematical content is:

\[
 \mathcal M_1(D;X),\mathcal M_2(D;X)
 \ll_\varepsilon X^{1/3+\varepsilon}
\]

for every active denominator shell, and

\[
 P(X)\ll_\varepsilon X^{1/3+\varepsilon}
\]

uniformly for real \(X\). The first conclusion is a weaker fallback
envelope than M9, whose statement still asks for exponent \(1/4\).

The exponent \(1/3\) is exact for the accepted direct menu. The witness
\((\delta,\ell)=(1/2,1/6)\) has menu exponents

\[
 1/3,\qquad \max\{1/3,1/6,0\},\qquad 1/2,\qquad

\]

directly from H4, the exact profile partition, and the elementary divisor
bound. R5-Full may depend on that reconciliation, but the reconciliation
must not depend on R5-Full.

No promotion is made to M9-M1, M9-M2, M9, endpoint uniformity at exponent
\(1/4\), the conditional one-quarter bridge, or GC-target. The canonical
M1 Gram core and M2 signed cross-row core remain the analytic barriers to
