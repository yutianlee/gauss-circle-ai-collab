# Statement-only Round 148 problem

Let \(R=X^{1/4}\), \(N=\lfloor X\rfloor\), \(DE\asymp M\le R^2\),
and

$$
Q\asymp D\sqrt{N/M},\qquad D\le\sqrt M,\qquad Q\le R^2\ll N.
$$

An exact one-sided transform of a squarefree, coprime, parity- and
character-weighted cone sum produces a signed reciprocal phase
\(e(Nd/q)\). Before imposing squarefreeness, the elementary divisor
parameterization \(j=N-aq\) proves

$$
\sum_{q\asymp Q}\left|\sum_{d\asymp D}e(Nd/q)\right|
\ll_\varepsilon (Q+D)(NQ)^\varepsilon .
$$

The required transformed scale is \(RD X^\varepsilon\). Expanding
\(\mu^2(d)=\sum_{a^2\mid d}\mu(a)\) and taking triangle gives only
\(QD^{1/2}+D\).

Independently determine the strongest true coefficient-sensitive
statement that can replace this failed triangle placement. First derive
an exact admissible signed model retaining the outer \(q\)-amplitude,
Mobius signs, coprimality, parity, \(\chi_4\), cone/profile/prefix, and
all boundary terms. Then prove a target estimate, prove a strict
target-safe range with the remainder explicitly owned, or establish a
rigorous method no-go or countermodel.

Do not assume the squarefree absolute \(Q+D\) analogue. Do not use
arbitrary favorable \(q\)-signs, random-sign heuristics, or support
disjointness. Audit \(q\mid N\), small \(j\), \(D=1\), balanced and
unbalanced aspects, clipped prefixes, and every \(M\)-range.
