# Round 157 barrier packet

## Accepted inputs

1. The nearest-cell selected scalar and exact ambient theta completion
   are equivalent up to the accepted Round-154 target-safe error.
2. Complete half-period theta-frequency resummation is an exact
   inverse-Gauss return to the quotient selector.
3. The complete \(v=0\) row is
   \(O_{\varepsilon,A}(M^{-1/4}X^\varepsilon)\) throughout the frozen
   outer-defect range.
4. The literal zero coefficient has the accepted signed-block BV norm,
   and all primitive, induced, conductor-one, odd-prime-power, and
   two-adic zero-row factors are closed.
5. Sampled Parseval keeps every fold modulo \(2N/d\). A proper
   frequency cutoff becomes a translated chirp and remains coupled.

## Frozen open scalar

For

\[
 q=4N,\qquad c=4N/d,\qquad H=c/2,\qquad
 M^{3/4}(\log(2X))^A<V\le\sqrt{NM},
\]

the open matrix is

\[
 -\frac{i(1+i)}{2Nq}
 \sum_{V<|j|\le2V}
 \sum_{\substack{d\mid N\\d\ \mathrm{odd}}}
 \chi_4(d)d\sqrt c
 \sum_{\substack{v\bmod H\\v\ne0}}
 \widehat B_j(2dv)K(-v^2,-j;c).
\tag{157.B1}
\]

With \(G_N(t)=\mathbf{1}_{N\mid t}\chi_4(t/N)\),
\(A_j=\widehat B_j(0)\), and
\(B_j^\circ(x)=B_j(x)-A_j/(4N)\), the first exact test is

\[
 \mathcal T_{\ne0,U}(V)=
 \sum_{V<|j|\le2V}\sum_{x\bmod4N}
 B_j^\circ(x)G_N(x^2-j).
\tag{157.B2}
\]

## Barriers that may not be recycled as gains

- complete \(v\)-resummation;
- the already closed zero row;
- termwise DFI;
- sampled Parseval with only physical diagonals;
- separated coefficient surrogates;
- bare flat-\(j\) reciprocal-arc localization;
- fixed-\(v\) interval bounds followed by absolute \(v\)-summation;
- one-dimensional Fourier \(L^1\) bounds that restore an \(H\) loss;
- modulus-average theorems at one fixed \(4N/d\); and
- the hypothetical \(V\le M^{3/2}\) square-root capacity without a
  literal signed theorem.

## Required power ledger

Every candidate must restore the selector factor, Fourier factor,
\(d\sqrt c\), all odd \(d\), every nonzero representative and fold, both
defect signs, the physical support size \(K=\sqrt{NM}\), the literal
profile and transition norms, hard endpoints, and the external
\(B_{1,U}(1)\) seam. A bound on a rectangular discrepancy, a spectral
norm, or a theorem right side is only an upper capacity until this ledger
is printed.

## Downstream quarantine

No Round-157 statement transfers to \(D>1\), \(L>1\), generic \(t=1\),
original \(t\ge2\), the cross owner, another M1 or M2 component, endpoint
uniformity, M9, the bridge, the quarter theorem, or a global exponent
without a separate proof.
