# Round218 isolated packet-coherence control

Read only protocol.md, your already permitted isolated task context,
and this new statement. Do not read any claimant, arithmetic application,
current report, control, strategy, graph, source or synthesis. The result
is an abstract lemma, with no discrete application supplied.

Fix an even integer p>=2 and a nonnegative smooth compactly supported
weight w which equals1 on an open interval J. Choose a compact interval
J' strictly inside J, with positive length. Let T tend to infinity,
L=L(T)>=1, and let A_T be a finite labeled set of cardinality
M_T comparable to T^2. Each label a has a real center rho_a in T J'.
Centers can repeat with arbitrary multiplicities.

Let H_T be real functions converging in L^p(R) to a real nonzero
H in L^p(R). For every label a, let c_a be positive with
c T^(-1/2)<=c_a<=C T^(-1/2), and let

 F_a(R)=c_a H_T(L(R-rho_a))+E_a(R),
 sup_a ||E_a||_(L^p(dR))=o(T^(-1/2)L^(-1/p)).

The constants and the little-o are uniform in the label a. No spacing
or maximum multiplicity bound is assumed. Define

 C_p(T,L)=sum_(ordered a_1,...,a_p in A_T)
                |integral w(R/T) product_j F_(a_j)(R) dR|.

Prove or refute the proposed lower bound

 C_p(T,L)>=c' T^(1+p/2)L^(-p)

for all sufficiently large T. The lower bound concerns the sum of
moduli AFTER each full radius integral. It is not a bound for the
modulus of the sum or for an arbitrary signed sum of tuples.

Explain all quantifiers and constants, ordering and repeated centers,
why even p matters, whether smoothness/oddness/pointwise convergence
of H_T is needed, and the role of the global Lp error and the plateau
margin. Control translated profiles, endpoints and L(T) allowed to grow
arbitrarily fast. Do not assume uniform pointwise smallness of E_a.

reviews/isolated_packet_coherence_rederivation.md in the218 round folder;
