# Conductor audit of the blind sixteen-term formula

Campaign: `m9-m1-beta-complete-axial-connector-ledger`  
Role: conductor claimant-seam review  
Allocation: 100% analytical/algebraic

## 1. Abstract algebra

The blind report defines \(F_j=L_j+B_j\), with
\(B_j=H_{j,+}-H_{j,-}\), and obtains

\[
 R_uR_v[\Theta Q]
 =F_uF_v+F_uP_v+P_uF_v+P_uP_v
 +\frac12F_uA_v+\frac12P_uA_v
 +\frac12A_uF_v+\frac12A_uP_v
 +\frac14A_uA_v,                                   \tag{34.B1}
\]

where the first connectors carry \(\psi'Q\) and the last carries
\(\psi''Q\). Expanding \(F_uF_v\) and the other \(F\)-terms gives the
claimed sixteen terms. The coefficients, first-connector signs, and mixed
\(+1/4\) sign agree with the conductor calculations.

For transverse separately meromorphic poles, finite Fubini and iterated
Laurent coefficients validate order reversal. The corner convention is
sequential: the second transfer of the first axis residue extracts the
corner once.

## 2. Project-specific overreach

The abstract result does not by itself license the report's notation

\[
 Q_{\rm ef}=Q_{\rm term}^{R_1}
 +Q_{\rm side}^{\rm ren}+Q_{\rm ar}^{\rm res}.     \tag{34.B2}
\]

The accepted endpoint and arithmetic estimates begin only after physical
profile limits, while renormalized-side removal uses a nested \(S\)-limit.
The blind permitted packet does not prove that outside \(u,v\) transfers
commute with those limits. Therefore (34.B2) is at most a finite algebraic
complement before the modules are removed; it is not yet an accepted
post-module kernel.

Furthermore, on an arithmetic stratum described by \(A=0\), the assertion
that the pulled-back beta coordinate is identically zero is valid for the
moving arithmetic residue itself, but subsequent outside-axis shifts may
shear its finite domain and create boundary terms. Connector derivatives
vanish tangentially only after the residue is parameterized consistently.

## 3. Verdict

The sixteen-term product-Cauchy--Green identity is a strong promotable
finite lemma. The actual post-module application must remain open pending:

1. a precise finite pre-limit complement definition;
2. a proof that endpoint/profile and nested-side limits commute with the
   outside-axis transfers, or an exact order that avoids requiring such
   commutation;
3. a collision ledger for axial versus artificial/arithmetic poles.

No estimate or computation is used.
