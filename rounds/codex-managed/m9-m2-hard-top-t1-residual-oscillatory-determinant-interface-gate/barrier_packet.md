# Round 167 barrier packet

## Frozen target

Prove

\[
 \Re\mathfrak C_{R_0,2,{\rm opp},g<\gamma L}^{\rm rem}
 \ll_{\gamma,\varepsilon}L^2X^\varepsilon,
 \qquad R_0=\lceil L\rceil,
\tag{167.G1}
\]

by one literal selector-aware oscillatory variable-determinant interface, or
close at its first exact source-class or restored-power no-go. Preserve
(d'm'-dm=r), all even (r<R_0), and one outer real part.

## Accepted input

- the complete Round-164 residual coefficient and endpoint-safe Fejer
  identity;
- the Round-165 absolute-site parity reduction, complete monotone tangent
  sector, high-divisor-gcd bound, dual-gcd character ledger, phase ledger,
  and variable-scale alternative;
- the exact opened-incidence positive capacity
  (L^3X^\varepsilon) for the remaining minimal sector;
- the exact matrix skeleton
  (\det\bigl(\begin{smallmatrix}d'&d\\m&m'\end{smallmatrix}\bigr)=r);
- compatibility of the preliminary determinant, two-adic gcd, and fixed
  (\chi_4(d')\chi_4(d)) seams after (r=hk),
  (k=2^{v_2(r)}); and
- the source-audit ordering: coefficient/automorphy first, then phase
  seminorm, variable-(r) aggregation, main terms/correlation norms, and
  endpoints/completion.

## Known source failures to confront

- No admissible automorphic or single smooth representation of the actual
  selector-dependent multiplier has been proved.
- Conditional smoothing of the square-root phase requires
  (\delta^{-1}\gtrsim1+Jr/L), and the hidden polynomial loss is not
  target-safe.
- A fixed-(r) application followed by triangle inequality loses the signed
  shift aggregate.
- The principal main term, (\mathcal K_+), residual source norms,
  two-adic/Möbius sums, cells, sharp hyperbolic cutoff, hard crossings,
  endpoints, and completion are unpriced.
- The 2025 Part-I kernel theorem is non-oscillatory; its general bound leaves
  two positive selector/phase autocorrelations, while its determinant
  corollary restores invariance and smoothness.

## Rejected restarts

- arbitrary-array or coefficient-uniform positive bounds;
- treating an aligned array as a literal K17a counterexample without an
  incidence lift;
- Cauchy transfer from an unpartitioned correlation to a selected signed
  incidence sector;
- absolute values inside the (r), determinant, divisor, selector, parity,
  cell, or endpoint sums;
- fixed-shift estimates summed by triangle inequality;
- average-centre, almost-all, spectral mean-square, or positive
  autocorrelation substitutions for the fixed-centre signed target;
- importing a determinant, Kloosterman, shifted-divisor, or automorphic
  theorem by structural analogy;
- erasing the principal main term or boundary/completion cost;
- using a large real phase derivative as modulo-one separation; and
- pivoting inside the round to the maximal Fejer scale or another proof
  owner.

## Promotion and stop rules

A proof must preserve exact multiplicity and every selector, squarefree,
parity, gcd, profile, phase, endpoint, and real-part feature, and must pay all
source terms by (O_{\gamma,\varepsilon}(L^2X^\varepsilon)). A no-go must
identify the first exact failed hypothesis or fixed positive power loss and
remain local to the tested placement.
