## 3. Proof or derivation

### 3.1 Import test

Each source was tested in the following order.

1. **Current theorem status.**  Resolve the latest arXiv version or the
   official journal record; a withdrawn result is stopped here.
2. **Coefficient identity.**  Exhibit a coefficientwise equality from the
   physical atom (198.8), with (198.3)--(198.7) evaluated first, to the
   source coefficient.  Structural resemblance is insufficient.
3. **Norm location.**  Keep the single outer real part and ask whether the
   theorem permits the modulus, height, orientation, frequency, and row
   sums to occur before a positive norm.
4. **Endpoint and smoothing.**  Preserve the actual hard samples, stars,
   ties, half weights, sharp faces, carries, branch births/deaths, and
   zero extensions.  No arbitrary smooth majorant may replace a live
   literal face without pricing its boundary.
5. **Parameter range.**  Verify modulus factorization, primitivity,
   interval length, transversality, shift size, derivative, support, and
   family-average hypotheses.
6. **Power restoration.**  Only after Steps 1--5 survive, multiply back
   every project normalization, row count, conductor lift, packet count,
   and outer restoration.
7. **Owner implication.**  Check that the resulting assertion targets the
   same graph node and the same lawful route.

The no-match lemma follows because every candidate fails at Steps 2--5,
or, for the few formal scalar maps, fails the restored power at Step 6.

### 3.2 Exact P2 boundary/sign/gcd test

The three residual pieces fail the available source interfaces for
different literal reasons.

- On \(P_{\partial\mathrm{lit}}\), the allocation swap crosses a sharp
  code boundary.  Neither exponent-pair Abel transfer nor smooth shifted-
  convolution theory has uniform variation control for every code in
  (198.5).  Demeter--Wu requires transverse smooth caps, while (198.11)
  is an aligned crossing.  Positive completion therefore leaves
  \(D_LL^2\), not \(L^2\).
- On \(P_{s\mathrm f}\), the lower character multiplier that made the
  common-cell swap odd is absent.  Erasing or averaging the character
  changes a difference into a sum.  Complete Kloosterman-product theorems
  allow fixed invariant weights, but do not accept this moving physical
  character-failure mask together with arbitrary conductors and endpoints.
- On \(P_{g\mathrm f}\), \((m,\beta)>1\) changes the conductor and the
  live corner set.  Fixed-prime-power, fixed-factor, or factorable-modulus
  theorems cannot delete the changed-gcd corners.  Pascadi's favorable
  \(p^2/pq\) factor is not a uniform substitute for the physical moving
  gcd.

The complete four-corner cross-coprime rectangle is not a repair: it
forces \(\kappa=1\), while the live packet requires
\(2\le\kappa<D_L\) in the intended application, and its simultaneous
swap has sign \(+1\).  These statements explain why the closest source
shapes do not import.  They do not prove that any of the residual pieces
has positive mass or violates (198.1).

## 4. First doubtful or unproved step
