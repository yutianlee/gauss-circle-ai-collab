## Newly relevant circle records

1. Peng Gao, arXiv:2604.23918v1, Theorems 1.1--1.2, proves saddle-point
   asymptotics for the $r(n)$-weighted count restricted to integers whose
   prime factors are at most $y$. This is friability, not the project's
   smooth physical M1 block, and it gives no unrestricted pointwise error
   estimate. See `sources/gao_2026_smooth_gauss.md`.
2. Youness Lamzouri, arXiv:2605.21476v1, Theorem 1.1, proves a sharp
   signed omega result for the circle discrepancy. The sectorial
   Gamma-kernel resonance theorem is an existence/lower-bound method over
   a long interval, not a uniform fixed-$X$ upper estimate for a literal
   packet. See `sources/lamzouri_2026_circle_omega.md`.
3. Benoit Cloitre, arXiv:2607.20960v1, Theorem 1.1 and its converse
   exponent implications, transfers the circle discrepancy to an
   odd-index Fibonacci fractional-part sum. It is an equivalence, not an
   improved discrepancy estimate. See
   `sources/cloitre_2026_gauss_equivalence.md`.

## Result

2026-08-31**. This is not a claim that no useful theorem exists anywhere.
No source supplies the factor $D_L$ on the aligned
$P_{\partial{\rm lit}}$ face, removes the open-packet multiplier
$\min(Y,D_L)/(H_B\mathfrak m\kappa)$ before positivity, or closes any
complete M1/M2 parent. Therefore the literature check licenses no proof
