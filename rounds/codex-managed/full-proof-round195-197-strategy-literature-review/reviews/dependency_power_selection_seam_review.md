### 3.4 Adjudication of the selection disagreement

The blind recommendation has one genuine strength: \(P_{\partial\mathrm{lit}}\) is the first place where the Round-197 common-cell mechanism provably loses its smooth/BV interface, and its \(D_L\) missing factor is explicit. It is therefore the right first falsifier for any proposed Round-199 mechanism.

It is nevertheless the wrong theorem exit for four reasons.

1. **It is another strict submask of the same open operator.** Rounds 195 and 197 already gained exact strict sectors. A boundary-only success would leave \(P_{s\mathrm f}\dot\cup P_{g\mathrm f}\) unpriced and would repeat, rather than end, the subdivision pattern.
2. **The three failures are the exact complement of one accepted code.** They are not three arbitrary owners: sharp-code failure, sign failure, and gcd failure exhaust the failure of the accepted lower-swap mask inside \(P_2\). Their disjoint sum is therefore a single exact linear operator and the natural completion kernel.
3. **Joint cancellation may be essential.** Taking a modulus on \(P_{\partial\mathrm{lit}}\) before restoring \(P_{s\mathrm f}\) and \(P_{g\mathrm f}\) may destroy a lawful cancellation among actual face jumps, character failures, and recomputed-gcd allocations. The full theorem is weaker than three separate component bounds.
4. **The complete remainder has a mechanical graph effect.** Success closes the remaining \(P_2\) sector. Boundary-only success creates another subordinate strict-sector node but closes no named existing remainder. The protocol's smallest-kernel rule and the strategy's anti-subdivision rule agree once “kernel” is interpreted as smallest completion kernel rather than smallest atom.

The apparent breadth of (R199) is controlled by the no-go exit. Round 199 need not force an unproductive proof attempt through all three channels after the aligned face falsifies a proposed mechanism. The aligned face is tested first; an exact self-return there closes the Round as a mechanism no-go, with no analytic pivot. Conversely, if the face test passes, the theorem exit still requires the sign- and gcd-failure channels and the exact outer assembly.

This resolves the disagreement without voting: retain the blind report's first stress test, but adopt the full-graph report's complete theorem boundary.
