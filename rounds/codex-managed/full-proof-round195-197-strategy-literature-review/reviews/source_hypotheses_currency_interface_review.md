### 3.3 Literal P2 seam

The three residual pieces fail for distinct reasons.

- \(P_{\partial\mathrm{lit}}\) is a discontinuous aligned face.  The
  relation

  \[
   \left(\frac{g\alpha}{m}-g\right)
   \left(\frac{gm}{\alpha}-g\right)
   =-\frac{g^2(\alpha-m)^2}{\alpha m}<0
  \]

  permits all \(O(LD_L)\) lower-close pairs to cross a ratio face.
  Model-phase/BV transfer and smooth shifted-correlation theorems do not
  control all literal branch jumps, endpoints, and zero extensions.
  Positive completion therefore retains \(D_LL^2\), not \(L^2\).

- \(P_{s\mathrm f}\) is exactly where the character multiplier needed for
  the common-cell lower difference fails.  Erasing the character changes
  a difference to a sum.  No audited complete-sum theorem accepts this
  moving character-failure mask together with the physical endpoints.

- \(P_{g\mathrm f}\) changes the gcd, conductor, and live corner set.
  Fixed prime-power or prescribed-factorization theorems cannot delete
  the changed-gcd corners or replace the moving physical conductor by one
  favorable modulus.

The Milićević--Robinson--Shupe alignment phenomenon is a guardrail here,
not a repair.  Its theorem is complete at one prime power, while the
project face is incomplete, moving-modulus, and hard-masked.  The
four-corner project rectangle also forces physical \(\kappa=1\), so it
does not turn the source theorem into a map for
\(2\le\kappa<D_L\).
