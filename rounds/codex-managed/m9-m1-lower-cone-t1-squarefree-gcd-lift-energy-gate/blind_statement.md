# Statement-only Round 149 problem

Let \(R=X^{1/4}\), \(N=\lfloor X\rfloor\), \(DE\asymp M\le R^2\),
\(D\le\sqrt M\), and \(Q=2\sqrt{ND/E}\). For each squarefree
\(d\asymp D\), put \(d_{\mathrm o}=d/(d,2)\), and let
\(\mathcal P(d)\) be a finite set of odd squarefree
pairs ((\alpha,b)) with (b\mid d), and put
(\ell=[\alpha^2,b]). Assume membership depends on the pair only
through (d), (ell), and the condition that the original progression
(e=\ell n) meets the specified clipped amplitude.

Consider the exact finite scalar

$$
\begin{aligned}
 \mathcal T={}&\sum_d\mu^2(d)\frac Dd
 \sum_{(\alpha,b)\in\mathcal P(d)}
 \mu(\alpha)\mu(b)\frac{\chi_4(\ell)}\ell\\
 &\qquad\times\sum_{\substack{q>0\\q\ {\rm odd}}}
 \chi_4(q)W_d(\ell/q)e(Nd\ell/q),
\end{aligned}
$$

where (W_d(\ell/q)) is smooth, supported on (q\asymp\ell Q),
and includes the actual radial, cone, and prefix profile. The required
bound is

$$
 |\mathcal T|\ll_\varepsilon RD X^\varepsilon.
$$

Independently determine whether summing all pairs with the same
(ell), and then all gcd lifts with the same reduced fraction
(ell/q=L/q_0), produces a square-summable coefficient and a
target-sized joint (d)-energy. Derive every compressed coefficient
exactly, including the odd-prime cases \(p\mid d_{\mathrm o}\) and
\(p\nmid d\), the absent \(p=2\) lcm factor, and the finite
nonempty-progression cutoff.

If (G(d)) denotes the exactly compressed row, test the sufficient
energy estimate

$$
 \sum_{d\asymp D}\mu^2(d)|G(d)|^2
 \ll_\varepsilon R^2D X^\varepsilon.
$$

Price its literal diagonal, distinct reduced fractions, exact
(N)-dependent alignments, near collisions, common divisors,
imprimitive denominators, (D=1), (L=1), (q_0\mid N), all aspect
ratios, and every clipped prefix. Prove the target, a strict range with
its complement owned, or the first rigorous method-specific no-go.

Do not replace the actual coefficient by arbitrary bounded signs, take
modulus before gcd-lift compression, assume a squarefree (Q+D) bound,
or interpret an adverse upper capacity as a lower bound for the signed
scalar.
