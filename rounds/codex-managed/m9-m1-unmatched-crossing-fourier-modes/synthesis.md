decomposition. Its difference factor is real arithmetic information, but
after the exact wavelet averaging it localizes modes at
(k\asymp J/T=X^{\nu/2}) rather than producing an additional small
factor. An exponent pair ((\kappa,\lambda)) therefore costs
(J^\lambda(J/T)^\kappa).

At \(\nu=2/5\), the TTY pair costs exponent (0.40273\ldots), and the
audited classical pair ((2/7,4/7)) costs (12/35\). Both lose even to
the original absolute product bound (X^{3/10+\varepsilon}). The Fejer
residual is target-safe for (K\ge X^{1/4}), but supplies no cancellation.

The first viable input is a joint (j,k) signed theorem. Its diagonal is
already of exact target size (X^{1/4}), so essentially lossless
off-diagonal control is required. No exponent or radial interval changes.
