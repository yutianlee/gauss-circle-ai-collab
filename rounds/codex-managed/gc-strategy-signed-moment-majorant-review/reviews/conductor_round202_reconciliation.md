Cauchy gives T^(3/2) close-pair mass. Removing the full exact mass
costs O(1), by the accepted absolute theorem of Round201. Multiplying
by T and a positive real Fourier weight near zero gives T^(5/2).

This argument uses actual r_2 weights. The open-lower/closed-upper
annular count is a subset of the closed positive band, so inclusive
endpoints and possible integer jumps cannot reduce its lower bound.
Ordered pairs of six-tuples are precisely ordered twelve-tuples;
there is no extra factorial. All exact relations, including
nonpairings, are deleted by an absolute bound uniform in the cutoff.
Schwartz decay guarantees convergence of the infinite majorant at
each fixed T; its uniform lower bound is supplied by a finite band.

The real part of w-hat is positive only on the small chosen interval.
The proof does not discard complementary terms of a signed sum to
infer a lower bound for that sum. The continuum control verifies that

smaller than every fixed negative power. This is a scope control,
not a replacement theorem for the arithmetic coefficients. The general
