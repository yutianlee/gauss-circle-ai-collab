# Round 142 statement-only problem: rational additive modes of the nonresonant cone

Let

\[
R=X^{1/4},\qquad y=\lfloor\sqrt X\rfloor,\qquad
N=\lfloor X\rfloor=y^2+q_X,\qquad0\le q_X\le2y.
\]

Define the exact floor-free cone coefficient

\[
C(m)=
\sum_{\substack{hr=m,\ r\ \mathrm{odd}\\r>4h}}\chi_4(r).
\tag{B142.1}
\]

Let \(V_{\rm low}\) be the fixed smooth compactly supported profile,
equal to one near zero.  On the disjoint powers-of-two blocks

\[
\mathcal I_M=[M,2M)\cap[1,C_VN/R^2],
\]

put

\[
k_m=\left\lfloor\sqrt{Nm}+{1\over2}\right\rfloor,
\qquad j_m=k_m^2-Nm.
\tag{B142.2}
\]

The frozen scalar is

\[
\mathfrak T_N^{\rm nr}
=\sum_M\sum_{\substack{m\in\mathcal I_M\\|j_m|>\sqrt M}}
m^{-3/4}V_{\rm low}(R^2m/N)
C(m)e(\sqrt{Nm}).
\tag{B142.3}
\]

The target is

\[
\boxed{\mathfrak T_N^{\rm nr}\ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon}
\tag{B142.4}
\]

uniformly for every real \(X\).  The exact floor correction, exact
radicals, and all nonzero cells \(0<|j_m|\le\sqrt M\) have already
been proved target-safe; they may not be reintroduced as an unpriced
owner.

The following exact coefficient fact is available:

\[
\sum_{m\le M}C(m)e(m/4)
={i\pi\over8}M+O(M^{1/2}).
\tag{B142.5}
\]

For a reduced rational \(\alpha=a/q\), the finite additive transform is

\[
S_C(M;a/q)=\sum_{m\le M}C(m)e(am/q)
=\sum_{4h^2<M}
\sum_{\substack{4h<r\le M/h\\r\ \mathrm{odd}}}
\chi_4(r)e(ahr/q).
\tag{B142.6}
\]

Determine the full rational additive spectrum of (B142.1), including
whether (B142.5) belongs to an infinite hierarchy of linear main modes.
Any claimed formula must give:

1. the exact condition on \(q\) for a nonzero row mean;
2. every residue class, sign, and main constant;
3. both finite cone endpoints and their errors;
4. a stated range of uniformity when \(q\) grows with \(M\);
5. the order and reconstruction error of any finite or infinite
   rational-mode subtraction.

Then determine whether those modes can be assembled along the local
derivative slopes of \(m\mapsto\sqrt{Nm}\) so that every extracted
rationally shifted square-root branch is target-safe and the residual
coefficient has the additive partial-sum control needed for (B142.4).
Define every major arc, width, overlap multiplicity, endpoint, dyadic
weight, and full \(R\)- and \(q\)-power.

The following distinctions are mandatory.

- A fixed-\(q\) asymptotic is not a theorem uniform over the
  denominators met by the varying phase slope.
- The accepted condition \(|j_m|>\sqrt M\) concerns phase values, not
  derivative distance from a rational.  No implication between them
  may be assumed.
- Subtracting one periodic mode is not a strict reduction unless its
  nonlinear square-root twist is target-safe and every other mode and
  reconstruction error is owned.
- A rational coefficient main term, raw mode capacity, positive
  energy, or radial cosine estimate is neither a bound nor a lower
  bound for the signed fixed-centre complex scalar.
- A Poisson, Voronoi, Mellin, or stationary transform that returns the
  reciprocal cone is bookkeeping, not a gain.
- The conclusion concerns only (B142.3).  It proves no square identity,
  lower GAR, blockwise M1 or M2 theorem, endpoint theorem, M9, global
  quarter theorem, or exponent improvement without each separate
  implication.

Prove (B142.4), reduce it to a strictly smaller owner-complete
rational-spectrum survivor with its complement target-safe, or prove
the first exact denominator-uniformity, major-arc accumulation,
periodic-branch, reconstruction, source-hypothesis, or self-return
obstruction.
