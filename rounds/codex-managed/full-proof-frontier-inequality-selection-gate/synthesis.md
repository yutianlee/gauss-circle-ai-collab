## Hard TOP

Support, displayed phase, and pointwise coefficient magnitude do not imply
the target.  For arbitrary row supports \(S_m\), phases \(\phi_{m,h}\),
and a unit direction \(c_h\), exact phase alignment gives

\[
 \sup_{|a_{m,h}|\le1}
 \sum_m\left|\sum_{h\in S_m}a_{m,h}e(\phi_{m,h})c_h\right|^2
 =\sum_m|S_m|^2.
\]

After all \(hm=\square\) entries are removed, a hard-cone rectangle still
has energy \(\asymp L^3\) in this coefficient class.  Product-fibre
averaging is genuinely noninvertible but fixes the aligned space
\(F(m,h)=f(mh)\); a high-pass discards that space and needs a full-capacity
remainder.  This is a method obstruction only.  The literal endpoint
coefficient may have extra structure, but none was available at the tested
interface.  Hard TOP remains open and was not selected.

## Lower GAR

The uncentered square function is false for the actual survivor because
\(\mathcal I_k\mathscr V_\delta\) contains the drift \(kc_y\); on a block
\(K\asymp y\) its energy is \(\gg y^3\).  Centering by \(kc_y\) is
mandatory and is harmless only in the original signed wavelet, whose full
first moment vanishes.

The centered estimate

\[
 \sum_{k\in\mathcal K(K)}|S^\circ(k)|^2
 \ll K(R^2+K)X^\varepsilon
\]

would imply the lower target, but it is a stronger separated \(k\)-norm.
Its exact reduced-Farey expansion has a target-safe diagonal and a
prescribed-centre nonzero-determinant off-diagonal, yet coefficient-blind
capacity is still \(y^3\) at \(K=y\), against the requested \(y^2\).
The transform provides no gain and violates the frozen selection rule.
Lower GAR remains open and was not selected.

\[
