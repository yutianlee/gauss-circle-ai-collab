## Accepted exact normalization

Write (X=N+\vartheta), (L_c=[c,4]), and periodize the offset Fourier
transform before unfolding its two aliases.  On a Farey cell of order
(J),

\[
 -\frac1{c(c+c_-)}\leq\beta<\frac1{c(c+c_+)},
 \qquad c+c_\pm>J.
\]

After two-dimensional residue Poisson summation, scaling

\[
 x=Ju,\quad y=Jv,\quad \tau=\beta J^2,
 \quad \Lambda=J/c,
\]

and then writing (	au=Lambda z), the exact phase is

\[
 \Lambda\{z(uv-1)-\rho u-(c/L_c)\sigma v\},
 \qquad
 -\frac{J}{c+c_-}\leq z<\frac{J}{c+c_+}.
\]

The (J^2) spatial Jacobian cancels the (J^{-2}) beta Jacobian.
Every compatible nonaxial saddle is nondegenerate, and the sharp
three-variable stationary factor is

\[
 \left(\frac cJ\right)^{1/2}|\rho\sigma|^{-1/4}.
\]

The neighbor-dependent incomplete-Fresnel factor is retained at saddle
entry and exit and is uniformly bounded.  Large dual aliases are
summable by integration by parts in (u) or (v); the two surviving
axes have no triple critical point and admit the same compact partition.

The complete local table is retained.  For odd (c), the local factor
is (2ic\chi_4(c\sigma)e_c(\lambda_c\rho\sigma\bar b)), with
(sigma) odd.  For (c\equiv2\pmod4), it is
(2c\chi_4(s_0)e_{2c}(\sigma s_0)), with (\rho,sigma) odd.  For
(4\mid c), it is (c\chi_4(s_0)e_c(\sigma s_0)), with (\rho)
odd.  Here (s_0\equiv-\rho\bar b\pmod c).  The only axes are odd
(c,\rho=0) and (4\mid c,sigma=0); the double zero vanishes.

Consequently the exact local coefficient has size

\[
 \frac{T}{\sqrt{cJ}}
\]

up to fixed symbols and the harmless local constants.  There are
(O(C)) moduli, (O(C/T)) endpoint numerators, and a fixed finite

\[

 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon\frac{C^{3/2}}{\sqrt J},

(C\leq J^{2/3}).

## Upper-conductor survivor

\[
