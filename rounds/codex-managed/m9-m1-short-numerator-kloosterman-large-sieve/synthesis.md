# Round 71 synthesis: three-variable Farey stationarity closes the low conductors

The order-(J) Farey representation has more cancellation than the
schematic short-numerator family shows.  On a cell (a/c+\beta), scale

\[
 x=Ju,\qquad y=Jv,\qquad \tau=\beta J^2,
 \qquad \Lambda=J/c.
\]

After the local Poisson factor (1/c), the exact beta--space phase is

\[
 \Lambda\{z(uv-1)-\rho u-(c/[c,4])\sigma v\},
 \qquad \tau=\Lambda z.
\]

Its compatible nonaxial critical point is nondegenerate.  The
(d\tau=Lambda,dz) measure and three-dimensional stationary phase
combine to give

\[
 \Lambda\cdot\Lambda^{-3/2}
 =\left(\frac cJ\right)^{1/2}.
\]

Thus each actual local incidence has size

\[
 \frac{T}{\sqrt{cJ}},
\]

not merely the outer (T/c).  The proof retains both offset aliases,
the exact real center, the sharp neighbor-dependent Farey interval,
incomplete-Fresnel entry/exit, all odd/even local factors, both surviving
axes, and a summable dual tail.

On (c\asymp C), there are (O(C)) moduli, (O(C/T)) contributing
endpoint numerators, and a fixed finite compatible dual family.  Hence

\[
 \mathcal D_C
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon\frac{C^{3/2}}{\sqrt J}.
\]

It follows that every fixed-interior conductor block

\[
 T\leq C\leq J^{2/3}
\]

is (J^{1/2}X^\varepsilon)-safe.  At the benchmark this closes
(X^{3/10}\lesssim C\leq X^{1/3}).

For odd (c), the remaining interior stationary phase is

\[
 e_{4b}(\rho\sigma\bar c)

\]

The range (J^{2/3}<C\leq J) remains open.  Completing the short
numerator preserves the dispersion diagonal.  Poisson summation in
(c) exposes complete Kloosterman sums with a square-root chirp, but
the next (B)-process returns exactly to the reciprocal-square phase.
Current incomplete-large-sieve, bilinear-fraction, and Kuznetsov
theorems fail the actual linear-twist, coupled-weight, level-four, or

Therefore Round 71 promotes a genuine low-conductor estimate and
narrows the next arithmetic target to the upper conductor.  It does not
