## 2. Exact statement and hypotheses

\[
 U=mq>4H_B,\qquad q>H_B,\qquad (a,q)=1,

\[
 j_q(a,v)=|a\bar v_q|_q,

 T=\min\!\left\{\frac{q-1}{2},

\]

For every admissible `a` and every dyadic band `J\le j_q(a,v)<2J` lying in the fast range `j_q(a,v)>T`, prove, uniformly in all admissible parameters,

\[
 \boxed{
 \sum_{\substack{\omega,v\ {\rm literal}\\
                  J\le j_q(a,v)<2J}}
 \mathsf V\!\left(W_{\kappa,u,mq,v,\omega}^{\sigma}\right)
 \ll_{B,\varepsilon}
 \frac{H_Bm\kappa uJ}{q}X^\varepsilon.}
 \tag{R191-FB}

Here “literal” means that the two orientations, all heights, affine sites, selectors, arithmetic deletions, profiles, endpoints, phases, signs, and zero extensions remain in their original coupled form under the single outer real part. The estimate must use the actual coefficient structure, including the exact lift

\[
 c_{mq}(ma)=m^{-1}c_q(a),\qquad |ma|_{mq}=m|a|_q,
\]

and may not replace the literal coefficients or `\chi_4` structure by absolute values or arbitrary signs.

The current proved comparison bound is

\[
 \sum \mathsf V(W_{\kappa,u,mq,v,\omega}^{\sigma})
 \ll_\varepsilon \frac{Y\kappa uJ}{q}X^\varepsilon,
\]

so the exact missing saving is

\[
 D=\frac{Y}{H_Bm}>1,
\]

and is `Y/H_B` on the primitive slice `m=1`.

**Promotion gate.** The gate for this objective is a complete analytic proof of (R191-FB), uniform over every listed literal variable and endpoint, followed by valid summation over admissible `a` and dyadic `J` without losing `D`. The strongest warranted conclusion is then only: the complete fast complement, hence the exact original-\(t=1\) residual after the stated connectors, is closed. No direct-M1 parent is promoted from this result alone.

**Genuinely new seam.** The proof search should target a **signed orientation-paired projective-difference lemma**: keep the single outer real part intact, couple the two orientations before applying variation, and combine the projective permutation `v\mapsto a\bar v_q` with the exact `m^{-1}` lift weight. **Stop rule.** Stop the Round-191 route immediately upon any one of the following:

1. the orientation pairing leaves an uncancelled boundary or deletion term of order `Y\kappa uJ/q` on the primitive `m=1` slice;
2. the argument takes an absolute value before the literal orientation/projective coupling and thereby also proves the unsigned or adversarial-sign analogue;
3. the proposed projective-difference bound implies uniform polylogarithmic centered-kernel prefix control for the known bad prime slope;
4. summing the candidate band estimate over `a`, `J`, endpoints, or zero extensions recreates a factor comparable to `D`;
5. the proof needs a source theorem or an unstated connector.

Any such outcome should be recorded as a no-go for this seam, not repaired by enlarging the round objective.
