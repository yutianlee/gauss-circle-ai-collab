### 3.1 Audit procedure

status; exact theorem and invoked definitions; pointwise versus averaged

### 3.2 MRS power restoration and false control

For \(r=\lceil k/2\rceil\), MRS Theorem 1.1 states

\[
 S(\boldsymbol a;p^n)
 \ll_k p^{n-(n-\Delta^*(\boldsymbol a)-1)/r+1}.
\]

At generic alignment \(\Delta^*=0\), with \(q=p^n\), this is exactly

\[
 p^{n-(n-1)/r+1}=q^{1-1/r}p^{1+1/r}.
\]

At \(\Delta^*=n\), the bound permits full size, and the paper's Example 1
exhibits full-size behavior.  For \(k=2\), Remark 4 identifies the
correlation with a Ramanujan sum and its high-alignment vanishing threshold.
Thus “complete products always save a pure power of the full modulus” is a
false paraphrase.  Moreover, no Round-189 fixed shift tuple or complete
residue variable exists, so even the generic formula has no lawful project
map.

The earlier K17a diagnostics remain informative but nonimportable: a
hypothetical generic \(k=2\) placement leaves \(Lp^2\), and \(k=4\) leaves
\(Lq^{1/2}p^{3/2}\), not the \(L\) target.  Residual \(p\)-powers may not be
discarded.
