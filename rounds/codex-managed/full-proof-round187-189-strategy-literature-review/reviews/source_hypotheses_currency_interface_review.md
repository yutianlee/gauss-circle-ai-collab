For the live Round-189 seam, the calculation is mechanical.  A \(J\)-band
has phase increment of size \(J/q\), so Abel costs \(q/J\).  The target
coefficient variation
\(H_Bm\kappa u(J/q)\) would then give \(H_Bm\kappa u\), and the exact lift
factor \(m^{-1}\) gives the allowed \(H_B\kappa u\).  The proved variation
\(Y\kappa u(J/q)\) instead gives \(Y\kappa u\), and after the same lift it
gives \((Y/m)\kappa u\).  Hence the missing factor is

\[
 \frac{(Y/m)\kappa u}{H_B\kappa u}=\frac{Y}{H_Bm}.
\]

Shen's Theorems 2--3 resemble the individual inverse phase but do not
accept this joint coefficient.  Shen's Theorem 4 moves to a positive
modulus moment, which destroys the signs needed before the single real
part.  MRS concerns a different complete prime-power Kloosterman-product
object.  Its formula

\[
 S(\boldsymbol a;p^n)
 \ll_k p^{n-(n-\Delta^*(\boldsymbol a)-1)/r+1},
 \qquad r=\lceil k/2\rceil,
\]

becomes \(q^{1-1/r}p^{1+1/r}\) only under generic alignment
\(\Delta^*=0\), while maximal alignment permits full size.  Thus neither
source reaches the stage at which a lawful fast-band power comparison can
even begin.

leaves \(Lq^{699/700}\); K26 capacity \(L^2\) versus target \(L\) misses
\(L\); BAL \(L^4\) versus \(L^3\) misses \(L\); smooth M1
\(X^{1/3}\) versus \(X^{1/4}\) misses \(X^{1/12}\); and the graded lane
