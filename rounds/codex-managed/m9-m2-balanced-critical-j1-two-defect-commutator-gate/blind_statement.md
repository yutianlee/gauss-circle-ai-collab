# Round 171 statement-only packet

Let \(X\) be real, \(L\asymp X^{1/6}\), and let \(B\) be one fixed
persistent critical \(j=1\) balanced block. Let

\[
 a(h,k)=\chi_4(h)\eta\!\left({(h,k)\over\sqrt L/2}\right)A_B(h,k)
\]

be the literal finite block coefficient, including its low-gcd weight,
slanted symbol, floors, stars, support crossings, signs, aliases, and endpoint
conventions. For a primed copy set

\[
 \Delta=h'k'-hk,\qquad \rho=hk'-h'k,
 \qquad \mathrm{df}=\{|\Delta|>L,\ |\rho|>L\}.
\]

The frozen target is

\[
 \left|\sum_{\mathrm{df}}a(h,k)\overline{a(h',k')}
 \left[e\!\left(\sqrt X(\sqrt{hk}-\sqrt{h'k'})\right)-1\right]\right|
 \ll_\varepsilon L^3X^\varepsilon.
 \tag{171.B1}
\]

Its direct positive capacity is \(L^4X^\varepsilon\). Exactly one factor
\(L\) must be saved before applying a positive norm.

Independently set \(h'=h+p\), \(k'=k+q\) and verify the exact coordinate
domain, inverse, multiplicity, and

\[
 \Delta+\rho=q(2h+p),\qquad
 \Delta-\rho=p(2k+q).
 \tag{171.B2}
\]

Determine whether the complete zero-subtracted scalar admits an exact
two-direction discrete commutator or summation-by-parts identity that yields
the target. On character support \(p=2s\) and
\(\chi_4(h)\chi_4(h+p)=(-1)^s\), so there is no character cancellation
inside a fixed-\(p\) fibre. The sectors \(p=0\) and \(q=0\) remain and may
satisfy both far gates.

A successful derivation must state both difference operators, every gate and
boundary correction, the exact complement, and a complete power ledger. Test
it with constant character product, erased gcd/slanted structures, and
arbitrary phase-adapted coefficients. If it survives these controls, it has
not isolated the required literal cancellation.

Prove the target, prove a complete nontrivial strict sector, or give the first
exact identity/capacity no-go. Do not infer anything about other balanced
labels or a global theorem.
