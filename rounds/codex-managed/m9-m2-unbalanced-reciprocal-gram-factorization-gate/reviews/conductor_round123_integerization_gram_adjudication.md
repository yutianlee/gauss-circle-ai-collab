## Accepted theorem package

Let

\[
 D=X^\delta,\quad L=X^\ell,\quad R=X/D,\quad K=XL/D^2,
\]

in the strict flat-smooth UNBAL region, and keep every sampled amplitude
at the original real \(X\). For any integer \(M\asymp X\),

\[
 |\mathscr R_X-\mathscr R_M^\sharp|
 \ll_\varepsilon (1+|M-X|)X^\varepsilon.
\tag{123.R1}
\]

The proof differentiates the physical product kernel. Its factor
\(L/D\) is cancelled by a divisor layer cake of effective length \(D/L\).
The \(d=0\) and negative-product branches are rapidly decaying. A direct
reciprocal-phase triangle costs \((1+|M-X|)K\) and is not the proof.

Consequently both \(M=\lfloor X\rfloor\) and an odd multiple
\(M=2^tM_0\), \(2^t\asymp X^{1/4}\), are lawful; the latter consumes the
full scalar target allowance and supplies no automatic near-alias gain.

For the integerized row, the unshifted weighted Cauchy Gram is a one-way
sufficient norm. On a nondegenerate interior its diagonal is
\(R>X^{1/2}\), so it is strictly stronger than the scalar target.

For the unique nearest integer alias \(j\), put

\[
 E=M(s-r)-jrs,\qquad u=M-jr,\qquad v=M+js.
\]

Then

\[
 M(1/r-1/s)=j+E/(rs),\qquad uv-M^2=jE,
\tag{123.R2}
\]

and, for \(M=2^tM_0\) with \(M_0\) odd,

\[
 \chi_4(r)\chi_4(s)
 =(-1)^{(jrs+E)/2^{t+1}}.
\tag{123.R3}
\]

There is no half-integer alias tie. For \(j\ne0\), the factor map is
invertible after the exact congruences, supports, orientations, and
profiles are retained. It degenerates at \(j=0\). All nonzero exact
aliases \(E=0\) together are \(O_\varepsilon(X^\varepsilon)\), while
smooth summation restricts the remaining effective defects to
\(|E|\ll X^{1+\rho}/L\).

For the shifted block square, define

\[
 A_{r,n}^{(H)}
 =\sum_{a=0}^{H-1}{q_{r,n+a}\over n+a}e(Ma/r),
\]

and let \(\Gamma_{M,H}(r,s)\) be its exact ordered-pair kernel with
prefactor \((J+H-1)/H^2\), \(J\asymp K\). Smooth zero extension and Abel
summation give

\[
 |\Gamma_{M,H}(r,s)|
 \ll_A \psi_H(M/r)\psi_H(M/s)
 \left(1+K\|M(1/r-1/s)\|\right)^{-A},
\tag{123.R4}
\]

where

\[
 \psi_H(x)=\min(1,(H\|x\|)^{-1}).
\]

The product layer cake proves

\[
 \sum_{r\asymp R}\psi_H(M/r)^2
 \ll_\varepsilon (1+R/H)X^\varepsilon.
\tag{123.R5}
\]

On the whole nearest-alias sector \(j=0\), write \(s-r=2m\). Then
\(K|M(1/r-1/s)|\asymp L|m|\), and (123.R4)--(123.R5) give

\[
 \sum_{j(r,s)=0}|\Gamma_{M,H}(r,s)|
 \ll_\varepsilon (1+R/H)X^\varepsilon.
\tag{123.R6}
\]

At

\[
 H_0=\left\lceil X^{1/2}/D\right\rceil\leq K,
\]

the full \(j=0\) sector, including every shifted self-correlation and
off-diagonal zero alias, is \(O_\varepsilon(X^{1/2+\varepsilon})\).

Hence, for every fixed \(\eta>0\), a violation

\[
 |\mathscr R_X|\geq X^{1/4+\eta}
\]

forces a positive-real contribution of size
\(\gg X^{1/2+2\eta}\) in the exact sector

\[
 j\ne0,\qquad E\ne0,\qquad
 |E|\leq X^{1+\eta/10}/L,
\tag{123.R7}
\]

with both \(\psi_{H_0}\) selectors, all shifted profiles and endpoints,
both alias orientations, and every character sign retained. This is a
fixed-power inverse theorem, not a threshold-level estimate.

## First remaining gap

At \(H=H_0\), prove

\[
 \sum_{\substack{r,s\ {\rm odd}\\
 j(r,s)\ne0,\ E(r,s)\ne0\\
 |E(r,s)|\leq X^{1+\rho}/L}}
 \chi_4(r)\chi_4(s)\Gamma_{M,H_0}(r,s)
 \ll_\varepsilon X^{1/2+\varepsilon}.
\tag{123.R8}
\]

Factor coordinates preserve this sum exactly. The two-adic sign is the
original quarter character in those coordinates. Poisson summation in the
transverse variable activates modes \(m-\sigma/4\asymp L\), and the
opposite character channel obeys the derivative self-return

\[
 \partial_r\Phi+m
 =-{Mh+\nu(s^2-r^2)\over r^2}.
\tag{123.R9}
\]

For a nonempty alias range, an allowed \(|h|<H_0\) makes (123.R9) vanish
at an interior point, with second derivative \(\asymp L|j|/X\). Thus a
uniform fixed-alias first-derivative bound is false. A continuation needs
a genuinely joint stationary-lattice inequality in the mode, shift,
alias, and reciprocal variables.
