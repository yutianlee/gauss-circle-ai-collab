# Blind statement: post-inner outer bilinear gate

Let \(R\asymp LD\) outer labels \(r\) carry coefficients \(A_r\) with

\[
 \sup_r|A_r|\ll L^{-1},\qquad
 \sum_r|A_r|^2\ll D/L.
\]

For each \(r\), let

\[
 F_r=\sum_{p\in P_r}\sum_{\rho\mid a_r+p}
      \sum_{\eta}S_{r,p,\rho,\eta},
 \qquad |P_r|\ll L,
\]

where each \(S\) is an exact finite Stieltjes-threshold reciprocal sum
and has the already proved bound \(|S|\ll K/L\).  All support lengths,
characters, congruences, and phase normalizations supplied below are part
of the class; arbitrary replacement matrices are not.

The scalar object is

\[
 \mathcal O=\sum_r A_r e(\gamma_r)F_r.
\]

The pointwise outer triangle gives \(|\mathcal O|\ll DK\).  At the
critical scales

\[
 D=Y^{1/2},\qquad L=Y^{1/6},\qquad K=Y^{11/48+o(1)},
\]

so this is \(Y^{35/48+o(1)}\), while the target is \(D\).  A sufficient
positive outer-energy assertion would be

\[
 \sum_r|F_r|^2\ll LD\,Y^\varepsilon,
\]

but it may be strictly stronger than the scalar target.

Determine the exact dual/Gram of this assertion for the supplied literal
kernel.  Either prove a target or strict-saving scalar inequality, give a
minimal additional actual-family hypothesis, or construct a support- and
energy-matched phase-aligned countermodel.  Test product-window resonances
and the unit-Hessian two-variable transform.  Do not infer a Gauss-circle
exponent, M9 statement, or quarter theorem.
