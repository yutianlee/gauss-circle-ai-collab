For a physical M1 block put (a=\delta-\ell).  The elementary direct
capacity is

\[
 c_0(\delta,\ell)=
 \min\left(a,{1-a\over2}\right),
\]

because the companion second-derivative exponent
((3\delta-1-\ell)/2) is no larger than ((1-a)/2) on the active
triangle.  Add the audited TTY row before minimizing.

first smooth family (D_1\asymp\lfloor\sqrt X\rfloor/2), choose an actual
frequency shell (L\asymp X^{1/6}).  Both approach (a=1/3), where

\[
 a={1-a\over2}={1\over3}.
\]

The TTY exponent is (770/1923>1/3) at the limiting point.  These are
candidate asymptotic witnesses that each direct parent retains one-third
capacity.  The review must verify literal ownership, floors, available
frequency labels, and absence of an accepted adjacent-profile
cancellation before this chart is promoted.

On the hard cone the accepted physical normalization is

\[
 X^{1/4}L^{-3/2}\mathcal T^{M1}_L.
\]

Thus coefficient-blind normalized capacity (L^2), against the normalized
target (L^{3/2}), gives physical capacity

\[
 X^{1/4}L^{-3/2}L^2=X^{1/4}L^{1/2}\asymp X^{1/3}.
\]

\[
 {X^{1/3}\over X^{1/4}}=X^{1/12}=L^{1/2}.
\]

This equality of deficits is a strategy diagnostic only.  It does not
identify the two sums coefficientwise and does not authorize combining
their profiles.
