# Round 79 synthesis

Round 79 proves the sharp positive incidence theorem for the residual
nonsquare top-\(M2\) reciprocal resonances. In the exact half-angle
coordinates

\[
 m=\frac{a+b}{2},\qquad q=\frac{b-a}{2},\qquad
 u=\frac{q}{m+\sqrt{m^2-q^2}},
\]

the phase center and literal reciprocal interval are

\[
 \Lambda=Xqu,\qquad
 \frac{Ju}{1-u}<k<\frac{2Ju}{1+u}.
\]

On a dyadic \((A,D,K,G,R)\)-block, the metric window is
\(|\Lambda-\ell k|\ll k/R\). Setting \(p=\ell k\) leaves
\(O(1+K/R)\) possible integers per ray, each with only
\(X^\varepsilon\) divisor factorizations. Therefore

\[
 \mathcal I_{\rm ns}\ll_\varepsilon
 X^\varepsilon AD\min\!\left(K,1+\frac KR\right)
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon\frac{ADK}{R}.
\]

The complete Round-77 coefficient gives

\[
 \mathcal A_{\rm ns}\ll_\varepsilon
 X^\varepsilon A\sqrt G\sqrt J D^{3/2}.
\]

This closes positively every block with \(AJD^3\ll L^3\).

Exact nonsquare centers are more rigid. Rational ratios of two
quadratic centers force the same primitive ray; hence at fixed \(X\)
at most one nonsquare ray is exactly resonant. Its divisor modes have
complete absolute contribution \(O_\varepsilon(LX^\varepsilon)\).

The factor-\(R\) saving is sharp. Averaging \(X\) on a uniformly
populated inner cone produces the ordinary \(1/R\) metric population,
even after deleting exact equalities and square rays. Thus no
classification of every metric incidence as exceptional algebraic
structure is possible, and the baseline remains too large on
\(AJD^3\gg L^3\).

The next exact survivor is the complete-coefficient signed
strict-metric sum on those hard blocks. Its density and centered
discrepancy must be treated together outside absolute values. Round 79
does not close the signed top cone, \(M9\!-\!M2\), \(M9\!-\!M1\),
\(M9\), endpoint uniformity, or the global exponent.
