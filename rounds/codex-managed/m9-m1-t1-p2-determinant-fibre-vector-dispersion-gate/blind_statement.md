# Isolated Round-195 statement: the full \(P_2\) determinant fibre

This packet is self-contained. It states a proposed theorem and the exact
controls to test; it does not assert that the theorem is true.

Fix \(X\ge2\), a literal hard-M1 shell \(L\ge2\),
\(Q=\lfloor(\log(2X))^B\rfloor\), \(R_0=\lceil L\rceil\), and
\(D_L=\lceil\sqrt L\rceil\). A physical opposing atom has

\[
N=dm,\qquad N+r=d'm',\qquad d,d'\ {\rm odd},\qquad
0<r<R_0,\quad 2\mid r,
\]

with \(d,m,d',m'\asymp L\). Put \(g=(d,d')\). Every atom carries its
actual residual selector, squarefree and coprimality predicates, profile,
floor, star, half weight, hard sample, crossing, endpoint trace, Fejer
factor, square-root phase, orientation, affine site, conjugation, and zero
extension. These data are coefficients of the actual operator, not arbitrary
bounded weights.

The live spectral packet has

\[
U=\mathfrak m q>4Q,\qquad q>Q,\qquad
\mathfrak m|a|_q>Q,\qquad Q\mathfrak m<Y,
\]

\[
T=\min\left\{\frac{U-1}{2},
\left\lfloor\frac{Q\mathfrak m U}{Y}\right\rfloor\right\},
\qquad
A=\min\{U-1,\lfloor Q^{C_0}\rfloor\}.
\]

At \(T=0\), no Farey row is removed. At \(T\ge1\), every retained core row
satisfies

\[
|c\beta-d_0\rho|>T
\quad(1\le c\le A,\ 0\le d_0\le c,\ (c,d_0)=1),\qquad
|\rho|\ge(A+1)(T+1).
\]

The physical mask

\[
P_2=\mathbf1_{\{|d-gm|\le D_L\}}
    \mathbf1_{\{|d'-gm'|>D_L\}}
\]

is imposed before Fourier expansion and height differencing, and the exact
core operator is rerun on \(P_2W\). Thus all physical-mask commutators,
affine births/deaths, carries, endpoints, phases, and zero extensions remain
inside the new core.

In the plus primitive chart

\[
d=\kappa gU,\quad d'=g(\kappa U+2S),\quad
m'=\kappa v,\quad m=\kappa v+2w,\quad h=Sv-Uw>0,
\]

define

\[
\Delta_-=\kappa(U-v)-2w,\qquad
\Delta_+=\kappa(U-v)+2S.
\]

Then exactly

\[
g\Delta_-=d-gm,\qquad g\Delta_+=d'-gm',
\]

\[
\Delta_+-\Delta_-=2(S+w)>0,\qquad
2h=v\Delta_+ +U\Delta_- -\kappa(U^2-v^2).
\tag{I195.1}
\]

On \(P_2\), \(g=O(1)\), \(|g\Delta_-|\le D_L\), and
\(g\Delta_+>D_L\). The minus orientation has the corresponding signed
primitive chart and must remain in the same final aggregate.

For one fixed inherited packet, let
\(\mathscr R_{{\rm core},{\rm fix}}^\sigma(P_2W)\) denote the complete
complex core sum with both orientations, both frequency signs, the full
anchor Fourier sum, and all literal coefficients above. Its positive atom
capacity is \(O(Y\kappa uX^\varepsilon)\). The proposed complete theorem is

\[
\boxed{
|\mathscr R_{{\rm core},{\rm fix}}^\sigma(P_2W)|
\ll_{B,C_0,\varepsilon}Q\mathfrak m\kappa uX^\varepsilon.}
\tag{I195.2}
\]

It is required uniformly in every packet and both \(T\)-branches, followed
by

\[
\boxed{
|\mathscr R_{{\rm core},Y,Q}^\sigma(P_2W)|
\ll_{B,C_0,\varepsilon}L^2X^\varepsilon.}
\tag{I195.3}
\]

The missing worst-case factor is \(Y/(Q\mathfrak m)\). A stronger inequality
contracting each packet relative to its exact positive mass is optional and
is not equivalent to (I195.2).

Independently determine whether (I195.1) supports a coefficient-retaining
determinant-fibre or \(TT^*\) theorem proving (I195.2), or whether its exact
diagonal, repeated effective phases, endpoint/carry/birth/death vectors, or
mask commutators force a rigorous method obstruction. Any proposed proof
must fail on the unsigned, character-erased, adversarial phase-aligned,
arbitrary bounded-coefficient, and separately normed orientation controls.
It may not discard \(T=0\), the strict \(T\ge1\) branch, or a proper part of
\(P_2\), and may not widen \(D_L\).

A rigorous no-go is a successful result. A strict target-safe submask is not
completion of \(P_2\) unless its exact complement is retained. No conclusion
may be promoted to \(P_1\), other original-\(t\) incidences, an M1 or M2
parent, endpoint uniformity, a bridge, the Gauss-circle theorem, or an
