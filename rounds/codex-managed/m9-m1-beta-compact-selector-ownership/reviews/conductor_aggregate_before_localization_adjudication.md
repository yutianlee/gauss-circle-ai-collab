## Decision

The requested rowwise physical selector map is not the correct interface.
At fixed finite contours the sixteen product Cauchy--Green terms are one
Stokes representation of a common operator.  They are not sixteen
separately limiting physical amplitudes.

Let \(\mathcal C_1,\ldots,\mathcal C_{16}\) denote the cells in the
accepted two-axis identity, including the positive mixed cell

\[
 \mathcal C_{16}[\psi Q]
 =\frac14 A_uA_v[\psi''(\beta)Q].
\]

Then, at every fixed finite box and with the common collision convention,

\[
 \sum_{k=1}^{16}\mathcal C_k[\psi Q]
 =R_uR_v[\psi(\beta)Q].                                  \tag{44.C1}
\]

The accepted endpoint-free construction takes the physical height limit
only after (44.C1) has returned the complete cell complex to its original
positive-line representative.  It supplies no limit for a single
\(\mathcal C_k\).  Therefore a row label such as \(L_uL_v\), \(B_uP_v\),
or \(A_uA_v\) cannot serve as an ownership projector.

## Lawful alpha split

On the right side of (44.C1), and still at finite height, linearity gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
R_uR_v[\psi Q]
={}&R_uR_v[\psi\chi_0 Q]\\
 &+R_uR_v[\psi(1-\chi_0)Q].                       \tag{44.C2}
\end{aligned}
\]

This is the correct central/large-alpha split.  If either term is
transferred again, derivatives of \(\chi_0\) and the complete beta
connector ledger reappear.  Adding the two transferred terms cancels the
\(\chi_0'\) and \(\chi_0''\) contributions but leaves
\(A_uA_v[\psi''Q]/4\), exactly as required by (44.C1).  Thus the mixed
cell is absorbed by whole-vector Stokes recombination; it is never zero.
