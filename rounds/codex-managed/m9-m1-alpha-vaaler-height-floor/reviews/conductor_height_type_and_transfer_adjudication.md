# Conductor adjudication: Vaaler height type and transfer scope

## Outcome

All three Round-52 derivations agree on the central type correction and on
the exact adjacent-height algebra.  The floor

\[
 H_j=\lfloor D_jX^{-1/4}\rfloor
\]

is an external integer parameter when the Mellin contours move.  Therefore

\[
 \partial_\mu H_j=\partial_\nu H_j
 =\partial_\alpha H_j=\partial_\beta H_j=0,
\]

and no (H_j)-floor delta belongs in the alpha Cauchy--Green incidence
ledger.  The factor ((H_j+1)^v) is instead an ordinary entire multiplier
in (v).

For

\[
 a_H(h)=\mathbf1_{1\le h\le H}\Phi\!\left(\frac h{H+1}\right),
\]

the exact increment (a_{H+1}-a_H) is positive, contains the full new
frequency (h=H+1), and satisfies

\[
 \|a_{H+1}-a_H\|_{\ell^1}=\frac12,
 \qquad
 \sum_h\frac{|a_{H+1}(h)-a_H(h)|}{h}\asymp\frac1{H+1}.
\]

The discovery report proves the sharper pointwise estimate and complete
power-weighted trichotomy.  The blind and hostile reports independently
prove the range needed for graph promotion.  The right-endpoint taper is
quadratic, but the old frequencies collectively contribute the remaining
order-one adjacent mass.

## Transfer obstruction

The unit-height lemma is not an actual dyadic-scale lemma.  Exact dyadic
scaling gives

\[
 H_{j+1}=\left\lfloor\frac{H_j}{2}\right\rfloor,
\]

and monotonicity plus Vaaler complementarity yield

\[
 \|a_K-a_H\|_{\ell^1}=\frac{|K-H|}{2}.
\]

Thus one actual dyadic height change has coefficient-only unweighted
capacity comparable to (H_j), and its (1/h)-weighted capacity is
comparable to one.  Moreover the complete physical term also changes the
denominator profile, its support and star, (D_j^u), and the finite-height
factor ((H_j+1)^v).  On a high (v)-line the latter can turn a formally
small adjacent physical coefficient change into full (H) or (log H)
capacity.  These are genuine mathematical obstructions to importing the
unit-step estimate into the alpha rectangle.

## Decision

Promote the typed discrete-height lemma and the actual-scale transfer
no-go.  Reject the identification of the floor with a moving alpha seam
and reject the use of the (O(H^{-1})) unit increment as an actual dyadic
block estimate.  Keep the alpha transition and all downstream obligations
open.

The next lawful object is the complete coupled dyadic difference on a
single common positive-line antecedent.  It must include the two Vaaler
heights, denominator profiles, supports and equality stars, scale powers,
radial data, and the external normalization exactly once.  A useful next
lemma must prove a signed partial-sum estimate for that complete family or
a sharp no-go; coefficient-only differencing is no longer admissible.
