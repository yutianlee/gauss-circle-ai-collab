# Conductor candidate: metaplectic two-character core

## 1. Candidate exact identity

On the literal residual hard block, define for \(r\ne0\)

\[
 \alpha_{r,g,k}={rX\over2gk}
\]

and the oscillatory spectral kernel

\[
 d\mu_{r,g,k,x}(\tau)
 ={e(\operatorname {sgn}(r)/8)\over\sqrt{2|\alpha_{r,g,k}|}}
 e\!\left(-{(J\sqrt x-\tau)^2\over4\alpha_{r,g,k}}\right)d\tau.
\]

For the density put \(d\mu_{0,g,k,x}=\delta_{J\sqrt x}\). Then exactly

\[
\begin{aligned}
 &W_R(\Lambda_q/k)
 e\!\left(-J(\sqrt s-\sqrt h)\sqrt x\right)\\
 &\quad=\int_{\mathbb R}e(\tau\sqrt h)e(-\tau\sqrt s)
 \left(\mu_Rd\mu_{0,g,k,x}
 +\sum_{r\ne0}\widehat W_R(r)d\mu_{r,g,k,x}\right).
\end{aligned}
\]

The equality is an oscillatory/distributional identity and must be
regularized symmetrically before sums and integrals are interchanged.

## 2. Candidate transformed object

After inserting the exact identity into the canonical block, the radical
phase and the two characters factor as

\[
 \chi_4(h)e(\tau\sqrt h)\;\chi_4(s)e(-\tau\sqrt s).
\]

The conjecturally useful form is a finite sum or an integrable direct
integral of bilinear pairings

\[
 \int \Theta_{\chi_4}[v_{\zeta,1}](\tau)
 \overline{\Theta_{\chi_4}[v_{\zeta,2}](\tau)}
 \,d\nu_\zeta(\tau),
\]

where \(\zeta\) records \(g,k,x,r\), the primitive ray, the Fejer shift,
and the exact owner. This display is a target representation, not an
accepted separation theorem: the projective norm of the moving symbol
must be proved.

## 3. Quantitative gate

For the fixed-\(a\) row, the exact target is

\[
 \mathcal G_H^{\rm act}
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon{H^2E_0\over\rho},
 \qquad E_0\asymp LJD^2,\qquad
 \rho={AJD^3\over L^3}.
\]

Consequently any vector-valued theta/Hankel inequality must save
\(\rho^{-1}\) at energy level after the complete projective norm and all
dyadic sums. Square-root cancellation in only one of the two character
rows, a generic large-sieve factor, or the unitarity of the Gaussian
operator is insufficient unless its exact scale ledger reaches this
bound.

## 4. Candidate advantage over prior routes

The identity is exact before scalar stationary phase and therefore does
not point-evaluate the nonintegral saddle \(b_*\). It also retains the
metric density as an explicit atom in the same spectral family as every
discrepancy chirp. It attacks the two \(\chi_4\)-rows jointly rather than
applying a second scalar \(q\)-process.

These properties distinguish it from the determinant-only, adjacent-
\(q\), dual-square, parity and discrepancy-only routes already proved to
self-return.

## 5. Principal risks

The full actual symbol may have large separation rank in \(h,s\), and
the oscillatory chirp measure has no useful total variation norm. Sharp
owner complements may be harmless under a uniform operator theorem, or
they may destroy every automorphic hypothesis. A half-integral-weight
formula may transform each \(\Theta_{\chi_4}\) back to a longer dual row,
restoring exactly the original capacity. Any of these, if proved with the
literal symbol, is a successful no-go result.

## 6. Minimal promotable outcomes

Promote only one of:

1. the complete fixed-\(a\) Gram;
2. a disjoint hard parameter range with an explicit \(\rho^{-c}\) gain;
3. the exact metaplectic factorization plus a rigorous equal-capacity
   obstruction and the smallest surviving coupled symbol.

Do not promote a formal theta notation, a source analogy, or a transform
identity without the owner, error, and capacity ledgers.
