## Scope of the candidate completion

This review concerns the oriented linear block, not the fixed-\(a\)
positive Gram.  The accepted Round-77 ledger bounds the original
Poisson zero and positive modes, equality modes, wrong-sign
nonstationary tails, endpoint samples, and fixed physical collars by
\(O_\varepsilon(L^2X^\varepsilon)\).  The later square, exact-centre,
and positive-safe owners have the same linear target.  If these owners
are reinserted once, the residual stationary \(k\)-interval can be
replaced by one fixed dyadic \(k\asymp K\) lattice, with the reinserted
terms subtracted after the completed block is estimated.

This is a candidate use of the accepted one-count ledger.  It must be
checked against the exact dyadic owner table before promotion.  It is
not valid inside \(\mathcal G_H^{\rm act}\) without additional cross-
energy estimates.

## Base-variable scaling of the exact chirp

Put \(x=gu\) and set \(\lambda=\tau\sqrt g\).  For one nonzero metric
mode,

\[
 \alpha={rX\over2gk},\qquad
 J\sqrt x-\tau={gJ\sqrt u-\lambda\over\sqrt g}.
\]

The exact Gaussian measure becomes

\[
\begin{aligned}
 &{e(\operatorname {sgn}(r)/8)\over\sqrt{2|\alpha|}}
 e\!\left(-{(J\sqrt x-\tau)^2\over4\alpha}\right)d\tau\\
 &\qquad
 =e(\operatorname {sgn}(r)/8)
 \sqrt{k\over |r|X}\,
 e\!\left(-{k(gJ\sqrt u-\lambda)^2\over2rX}\right)d\lambda.
\end{aligned}
\]

The density mode is the atom \(\delta_{gJ\sqrt u}\) in the
\(\lambda\)-variable.  Thus the scalar prefactor of the base-variable
chirp is independent of \(g\); the lift moves only its centre.

## Exact product structure after completion

The accepted collared amplitude has

\[
 A^\circ_{ga,gb}(gu)
 =g^{-3}L^3u^{-3/2}E_a(g)\overline{E_b(g)}
 P_a(u)\overline{P_b(u)}
 \rho\!\left({g(a-u)\over M}\right)
 \rho\!\left({g(u-b/4)\over M}\right),
\]

with \(P_a(u)=a^{-3/4}W(\sqrt{q_Xa/(4u)})\).  After
\(dx=g\,du\), every factor depending on \(a\) and every factor
depending on \(b\) is separated at fixed
\((g,k,u,\lambda,r)\).  The radical phase is

\[
 e(\lambda\sqrt a)e(-\lambda\sqrt b).
\]

The smooth block condition \(b-a\asymp D\) also separates exactly:
for a fixed compactly supported smooth \(\psi\),

\[
 \psi\!\left({b-a\over D}\right)
 =\int_{\mathbb R}\widehat\psi(\xi)
   e(-\xi a/D)e(\xi b/D)\,d\xi,
 \qquad
 \lVert\widehat\psi\rVert_1\ll_\psi1.
\]

Primitivity has the exact Möbius expansion

\[
 \mathbf 1_{(a,b)=1}=\sum_{d\mid a,\ d\mid b}\mu(d).
\]

Here the actual factors \(a^{-3/4}b^{-3/4}\) contribute
\(d^{-3/2}\) after \(a=da_1\), \(b=db_1\).  Hence the Möbius
separation has absolutely summable scalar cost
\(\sum_{d\ {\rm odd}}d^{-3/2}<\infty\); it is not being bounded by the
false unweighted projective norm \(\sum_{d\le A}1\).

Subject to the linear owner-completion check, the completed block is
therefore an exact coherent integral of products

\[
 \Theta_{\zeta,1}(\lambda)
 \overline{\Theta_{\zeta,2}(\lambda)},
\]

where each \(\Theta_{\zeta,i}\) is a one-variable
\(\chi_4\)-weighted square-root sum and \(\zeta\) contains
\((d,g,k,u,\xi,r)\).  Density and all \(r\ne0\) modes remain one
coefficient; no absolute sum over \(r\) or \(\lambda\) is licensed.

## What this does and does not remove

The completion removes, at linear level, the two formal separation
objections caused by the sharp stationary ratio interval and the prior-
owner masks.  It does not estimate the resulting coherent integral.
The chirp has infinite \(\lambda\)-support and constant modulus, the
separate character B-processes have equal squared mass, and their
Gaussian recombination is another metaplectic chirp.  Thus neither
ordinary Plancherel nor separate row bounds provide the missing
\(\rho^{-1/2}\) linear gain.

The first possible new theorem is a direct, outside-absolute estimate
for this completed coherent two-character integral.  A theorem stated
only for compact spectral weights, independently chosen row
coefficients, or a positive \(L^2\) measure does not apply.  No
\(M9\!-\!M2\) or exponent consequence is claimed here.
