Promote a scoped exact functional-calculus obstruction and finite
triangular-separation lemma.  Do not promote the fixed-\(a\) Gram, a
hard-cone subrange, the proposed theta pairing as an automorphic object,
or any downstream estimate.

The exact kernel is stronger and simpler than the original candidate.

\[
 K_\alpha(u)
 ={e(\operatorname {sgn}(\alpha)/8)\over\sqrt{2|\alpha|}}
 e(-u^2/(4\alpha)),
 \qquad T_0=I.
\]

Then

\[
 \widehat{T_\alpha f}(y)=e(\alpha y^2)\widehat f(y)
\]

and the complete metric-mode sum is exactly

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \sum_{r\in\mathbb Z}\widehat W_R(r)T_{rc}
 =\mathcal F^{-1}M_{W_R(cy^2)}\mathcal F.
 \quad}
\]

The \(r=0\) metric density is the identity component.  This formula
retains all cross-mode terms and reconstructs
\(W_R(\Lambda/k)\) at \(y=\sqrt s-\sqrt h\).  Plancherel gives

\[
 \left\|\sum_r\widehat W_R(r)T_{rc}f\right\|_2^2
 =\int |W_R(cy^2)|^2|\widehat f(y)|^2\,dy,
\]

so the operator has exactly the original metric-window capacity.  Its
norm is \(\lVert W_R\rVert_\infty\), independent of \(\rho\).
Consequently the visible Fresnel factor, modewise unitarity, Gaussian
inversion, or separate Plancherel estimates cannot supply the missing
\(\rho^{-1/2}\) linear gain.

\[
 \sum_{a,q}(-1)^qF_a(q),
 \qquad
 (-1)^q=\chi_4(a)\chi_4(a+2q).

\[
 (-1)^v

in the signed linear block, but the primitive character has constant

At fixed \(z=k/J\), the reciprocal fibre is exactly

\[
 (1-z)^{-1}<\sqrt{b/a}<1+2z.
\]

triangular mask.  Ranking the two ordered sets and applying finite
Fourier inversion on a cyclic group of size \(>2N\) gives an exact
separated expansion whose coefficient \(\ell^1\)-mass is
\(O(\log(2+N))\).  Hence the sharp band has no positive-power
algebraic separation loss.  Its continuous Mellin transform still has
a principal-value \(1/\xi\) tail, and the finite rank factors depend
jaggedly on \(k\), ties, and endpoints.  The lemma therefore does not
