## 3. Exact metaplectic identity

\[

 \sum_{r\ne0}\widehat W_R(r)e(r\Lambda_q/k).

\[

 ={rX\over2gk}(\sqrt s-\sqrt h)^2,

\[
 e(\alpha y^2)

\[
\begin{aligned}

\[

spectral measure in \(\tau\). This identity is exact before stationary
