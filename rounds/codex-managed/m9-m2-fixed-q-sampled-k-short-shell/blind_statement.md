# Round 104 statement-only packet

Campaign: `m9-m2-fixed-q-sampled-k-short-shell`

This packet is self-contained. Do not read the proof graph, proof draft,
strategy files, Round-104 claimant reports, or earlier derivations beyond
the exact statements below.

Let \(J=\sqrt X\), \(1\le L\le J^{1/2}\), and let

\[
 a<b<4a,\qquad a,b\ \text{odd},\qquad (a,b)=1,
 \qquad b=a+2q.
\]

On a dyadic block assume

\[
 a\asymp b\asymp A,\qquad q\asymp D,\qquad
 K\asymp {JD\over A},\qquad G\asymp {L\over A},
\]

and retain only the residual hard range

\[
 \rho={AJD^3\over L^3}>1.
\]

Put

\[
 \delta_{a,q}=\sqrt{a+2q}-\sqrt a,\qquad
 \Lambda_{a,q}={X\delta_{a,q}^2\over2},
\]

\[
 I_{a,q}=\left({J\delta_{a,q}\over2\sqrt a},
                 {J\delta_{a,q}\over\sqrt{a+2q}}\right).
\]

For each odd lift \(g\asymp G\), the literal complete centered integral
is

\[
 \mathfrak B^\circ_{a,q,k}(g)
 =g\int_{(a+2q)/4}^{a}A^\circ_{ga,g(a+2q)}(gu)
 e\!\left(gk\left(\sqrt u-{J\delta_{a,q}\over2k}\right)^2\right)du.
\]

The full coefficient \(F_a(q)\) sums this integral over the exact open
integer interval \(k\in I_{a,q}\), the finite odd lift set, every metric
density and discrepancy mode, primitive and prior-owner masks, profiles,
floors, stars, orientations, fixed physical collars, entry/exit and zero
extension. No arbitrary bounded coefficient may replace this symbol.

The exact carrier is

\[
 e\!\left(-{g\Lambda_{a,q}\over2k}\right).
\]

For \(1\le H\le D\), define

\[
 \mathcal G_H^{\rm act}
 =\sum_{a,n}\left|\sum_{0\le h<H}(-1)^hF_a(n+h)\right|^2.
\]

The required hard-block target is

\[
 \mathcal G_H^{\rm act}
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon
 {H^2L^4\over AD}.
\]

Independently derive the sharp uniform consequence of the complete
centered integral in the sampled reciprocal variable \(k\). In
particular, determine whether

\[
 |F_a(q)|\ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon {L^2\over A}
\]

is valid uniformly in \(q\asymp D\), what it implies for
\(\sum_{a,q}|F_a(q)|^2\) and \(\mathcal G_H^{\rm act}\), and the largest
range of \(D\) that follows without any signed \(q\)-correlation.

Test empty fibres, endpoint equality, fixed collars, \(G\asymp1\), Pell
or near-square rows, fourth powers, density modes, an arbitrary
phase-conjugated coefficient, and the distinction between fixed or
polylogarithmic \(D\) and polynomial \(D\).
