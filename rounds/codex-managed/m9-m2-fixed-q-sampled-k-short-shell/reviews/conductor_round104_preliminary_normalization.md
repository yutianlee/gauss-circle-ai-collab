## Exact algebra

For \(b=a+2q\),

\[
 (\sqrt b-\sqrt a)^2
 ={2q^2\over a+q+\sqrt{a(a+2q)}},
\]

so \(\Lambda_q\asymp J^2D^2/A\) and
\(I_{a,q}\) lies at \(k\asymp JD/A\). The centered saddle maps the two
literal reciprocal endpoints to \(a\) and \(b/4\).

With \(G=L/A\),

\[
 {J\delta_q\sqrt G\over K^{3/2}}
 =\sqrt{AL\over JD}
\]

up to fixed cone constants. The physical collar resolution is
\(JD/(AL)\ge1\) on every nonempty row because \(A\ll L\) and
\(L\le J^{1/2}\).

## Reciprocal phase

The complete mode phase satisfies

\[
 f_{\nu,g}''(k)\asymp {|2\nu-g|A^2\over JD}.
\]

Multiplying the standard second-derivative bound by the proposed actual
variation scale gives

\[
 \sqrt{ALn}+\sqrt{L/A}\,n^{-1/2},
\]

with no residual \(D\)-power. The metric moments then give \(L+1\) per
lift and \(L^2/A\) per row.

## Gram powers

There are \(O(AD)\) rows, so the row theorem gives
\(E_{A,D}\ll X^\varepsilon DL^4/A\). Direct Cauchy in an \(H\)-window
counts each row \(H\) times after the initial Cauchy factor \(H\), hence

\[
 \mathcal G_H^{\rm act}
 \ll X^\varepsilon H^2DL^4/A.
\]

Against the target \(X^\varepsilon H^2L^4/(AD)\), the exact route loss
is \(D^2\), independent of \(H\). Fixed or polylogarithmic \(D\) is safe;
a polynomial \(D\)-range is not.

The remaining doubtful seam is not the power ledger. It is the uniform
actual-symbol proof of sampled-\(k\) variation and the complete owner
ledger as \(q\) varies. Those are assigned to the independent reports.
