# Conductor scope: localization versus global side deletion

Campaign: `m9-m1-beta-axial-side-exhaustion`  
Role: conductor module-order audit  
Allocation: 100% analytical/algebraic

Round 33 admits two exact organizational orders:

1. keep the global three-mask finite package, recombine its endpoint
   prefixes, and invoke the unmasked boundary modules; or
2. collapse the global endpoint package before transition localization.

The accepted Round-21 lemma already deletes the **unmasked** renormalized
radial sides under nested exhaustion. The Round-35 issue is whether later
beta localization and two-axis displacement generate a missing image of
that deleted side.

At every finite stage satisfying

\[
 S>\frac{U+V}{2}+2B_0,
\]

the beta mask and all its derivatives vanish on the radial-side cell.
Therefore the beta-localized image of that side is exactly zero. This
shows that the diagram commutes in the beta component:

\[
\begin{array}{ccc}
\text{finite unmasked }R_M\text{ side}
 &\xrightarrow{\text{nested limit}}&0\\
\downarrow\Theta_\beta,\,\mathsf X_{uv}&&\downarrow\\
0&\xrightarrow{}&0.
\end{array}
\]

No claim is needed that each of the other two hierarchical masks vanishes;
their sum with the beta mask reconstructs the unmasked module already
handled by Round 21. Nor is this an interchange of a nonuniform integral
with a limit: the lower-left term is identically zero at every sufficiently
large finite stage.

This closes only the radial-side component of the beta commutator. The
endpoint and \(R_1\) arithmetic modules are not supported at
\(t=\pm S\), so the same support argument does not touch their physical
profile limits.
