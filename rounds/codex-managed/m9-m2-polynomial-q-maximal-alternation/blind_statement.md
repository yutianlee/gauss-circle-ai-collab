\[
 b=a+2q,\qquad a\asymp b\asymp A,\qquad q\asymp D,\qquad
 a,b\ \mathrm{odd},\qquad (a,q)=1,

\[
 K\asymp {JD\over A},\qquad G\asymp {L\over A},\qquad
 \rho={AJD^3\over L^3}>1.
\]

For each permitted \(a,q\), \(F_a(q)\) is the exact zero-extended actual
row coefficient. It retains the moving open reciprocal interval, finite
odd lifts, primitive and prior-owner masks, actual profiles, floors,
stars, physical collars, both orientations, saddle entry and exit, the
complete punctured metric density and every discrepancy mode.

The accepted fixed-row theorem is

\[
 |F_a(q)|\ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon {L^2\over A}.
\]

Define the maximal alternating row

\[
 \mathcal M_a(D)
 =\sup_{I\subset [D,2D)}
 \left|\sum_{q\in I\cap\mathbb Z}(-1)^qF_a(q)\right|.
\]

The proposed theorem is

\[
 \mathcal M_a(D)
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon {L^2\over A}. \tag{105.B1}
\]

If (105.B1) holds, taking \(H\asymp D\) gives

\[
 \mathcal G_H^{\rm act}
 =\sum_{a,n}\left|\sum_{0\le h<H}(-1)^hF_a(n+h)\right|^2
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon {DL^4\over A},
\]

which is the canonical target
\(X^\varepsilon H^2L^4/(AD)\) at \(H\asymp D\).
