# Barrier packet: maximal residual parity-Fejer dyadic-frequency gate

## Frozen power ledger

- Support interval length: \(M_L\asymp L^2\).
- Accepted coefficient energy: \(D_L\ll_\varepsilon L^2X^\varepsilon\).
- Coefficient-uniform maximal Fejer capacity: \(M_LD_L\asymp
  L^4X^\varepsilon\).
- K26 and each sufficient dyadic-link budget: \(L^3X^\varepsilon\).
- Missing saving at the top scale: one factor \(L\).
- Even shifts below \(R_0=\lceil L\rceil\) cost the full permitted
  \(L^3X^\varepsilon\) once; there is no second spare short-shift payment.

## Exact controls

1. Keep the exact even-shift projector
   \(K_R^{(2)}=(F_R(\theta)+F_R(\theta+1/2))/2\), including both peaks.
2. Use full-line zero extension.  Do not evaluate a square root off the
   positive coefficient support.
3. The dyadic difference has zero diagonal; any restored diagonal is an
   artifact that must be removed or charged.
4. The Haar identity is exact only for a doubling link.  Use the exact Fejer
   difference for the final truncated link.
5. Preserve the single aggregate real part through the \((r,N)\) sum and,
   if transformed, through the common frequency and dual-mode assembly.
6. Retain selected and no-pair rows, squarefree and coprimality holes, both
   complementary parities, all profiles and point values, hard endpoints,
   floors, stars, crossings, and zero-extension jumps.

## False implications

- A phase-aligned or dechirped array has \(L^4\) capacity, so no arbitrary-
  coefficient theorem is available.
- Large real derivatives do not imply distance from integers.
- Character constancy on an even cofactor-gcd row is not cancellation.
- Positivity of Haar detail gives no saving without an actual-symbol lower
  bound.
- Global Parseval or Fejer-kernel supremum gives only \(RD_L\).
- Absolute summation after character Poisson restores the accepted moving
  product collar and does not prove K26.
- K26 proves at most the complete residual \(t=1\) scalar; it does not prove
  the remaining \(t=1\) channels or any parent theorem.

## Hard stop

Stop at the first exact loss of a literal coefficient component, parity or
endpoint term, common-frequency coupling, one-real-part structure, or the
factor-\(L\) budget.  Stop also if the transform self-returns to K17a, the
fixed-shift/absolute-dual placements, or the Round-169 product collar without
a new signed connector.  Do not pivot inside Round 172.
