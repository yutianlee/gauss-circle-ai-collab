# Conductor review: first unmapped ownership stratum

Campaign: `m9-m1-beta-double-bounded-explicit-validation`

## Exact gap

For the central beta cell

\[
c_{\rm db}(\mu,\nu)=\psi(\beta)\chi_0(\alpha),
\qquad
\partial_\mu\beta=\partial_\nu\beta=-\frac12,
\quad
\partial_\mu\alpha=\partial_\nu\alpha=\frac12,
\]

one has

\[
\partial_\mu\partial_\nu c_{\rm db}
=\frac14\{\psi''\chi_0-2\psi'\chi_0'+\psi\chi_0''\}.
\]

The accepted product Cauchy--Green ledger therefore contains the mixed
connector

\[
\frac14A_uA_v[
\{\psi''\chi_0-2\psi'\chi_0'+\psi\chi_0''\}Q_T].
\]

After adding the large-alpha complement, the \(\chi_0'\) and
\(\chi_0''\) parts cancel, but the beta connector

\[
\frac14A_uA_v[\psi''(\beta)Q_T]
\]

survives. The Round-43 packet allows a connector derivative to be hidden
inside an unnamed \(M_\tau\), but it neither supplies the corresponding
\(\tau\) nor proves that this stratum is routed to a previously accepted
module. Hence the identity between the actual compact remainder and
\(\mathfrak T\) is unproved at its first nontrivial mixed stratum.

## Required finite certificate

The next packet must expand the accepted product transfer and give, for
each row:

1. operator stratum and orientation;
2. coefficient \(1,1/2\), or \(1/4\) and residual contour measure;
3. terminal survivor versus routed module;
4. central/large-alpha recombination status;
5. exact \(\tau\), singular or smooth profile, and \(j\)-selector;
6. floors, stars, collision convention, and unique corner ownership;
7. artificial/axis/endpoint exclusion certificate;
8. unique owner of the external \(X^{1/4}\) factor.

Aggregate three-mask routing proves the global boundary-module identity;
it does not by itself supply this cellwise table. Conversely, the local
analytic estimate applies once the table is supplied, but cannot prove the
identity.
