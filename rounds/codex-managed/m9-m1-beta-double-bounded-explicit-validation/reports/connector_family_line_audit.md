# Round 43 connector-family line audit

## 1. Result

**No-go result: the displayed class is analytically plausible, but the actual-family equality is not proved by the permitted artifacts.**  The phase/profile, hard-top split, compact gamma multiplier, and terminal contour normalization in the packet agree with the accepted local product-cell formulas.  What is missing is the advertised term-by-term map from the accepted finite product Cauchy--Green vector to the abstract set \(\mathfrak T\).  In particular, no permitted artifact names the \(\tau\) representing each surviving beta connector, gives its coefficient and measure, or identifies the exact accepted module that removes each axis, connector-axis, collision, and corner term.

The first exact unmapped term is already the mixed compact connector obtained from the last term of the accepted two-axis ledger.  Before the internal alpha partition is recombined, its central share is

\[
 \frac14 A_uA_v\!\left[
 \{\psi''(\beta)\chi _0(\alpha)
   -2\psi'(\beta)\chi _0'(\alpha)
   +\psi(\beta)\chi _0''(\alpha)\}
 Q_T\right].                                             \tag{43.L1}
\]

The \(\chi _0'\) and \(\chi _0''\) pieces cancel against the large-alpha complement, leaving the beta mixed connector \(\frac14A_uA_v[\psi''(\beta)Q_T]\).  The packet says this cancellation occurs and says a fixed connector derivative may be hidden in \(M_\tau\), but supplies neither an ownership convention for the compact restriction of the surviving \(\psi''/4\) term nor a corresponding \(\tau\).  Thus (43.L1) is generically coverable by the proposed class, but it is not actually mapped into it.

## 3. Proof and derivation

In the beta-slab variables used by the accepted tail theorem,

\[
 \partial_\mu\beta=\partial_\nu\beta=-\frac12,
 \qquad
 \partial_\mu\alpha=\partial_\nu\alpha=\frac12.
\]

Consequently, for \(c_{\rm db}=\psi(\beta)\chi _0(\alpha)\),

\[
 \partial_\mu\partial_\nu c_{\rm db}
 =\frac14\{\psi''\chi _0-2\psi'\chi _0'+\psi\chi _0''\}.
                                                               \tag{43.L4}
\]

The accepted Round-34 product identity contains

\[
\begin{aligned}
R_uR_v[\psi Q_T]={}&F_uF_v[\psi Q_T]+F_uP_v[\psi Q_T]
 +P_uF_v[\psi Q_T]+P_uP_v[\psi Q_T]\\
&+\frac12F_uA_v[\psi'Q_T]+\frac12P_uA_v[\psi'Q_T]
 +\frac12A_uF_v[\psi'Q_T]+\frac12A_uP_v[\psi'Q_T]\\
&+\frac14A_uA_v[\psi''Q_T].                         \tag{43.L5}
\end{aligned}
\]

Expanding the two \(F\)'s gives sixteen actual strata.  Equation (43.L4) shows exactly how the central-alpha version of the final term arises.  Adding the complementary cutoff \(\psi(\beta)(1-\chi _0(\alpha))\) cancels every \(\chi _0'\) and \(\chi _0''\) term, as asserted in the packet, but does not delete the beta connector \(\psi''/4\).  A later compact restriction therefore needs a declared ownership rule and a named multiplier.

Round 36 cannot supply that missing row.  Its identity cancels connector terms only after summing the **three global masks**, returns the aggregate physical module to its original positive line, and expressly does not prove individual masked or cellwise physical limits.  Round 38 proves existence of the endpoint-free signed Cauchy limit, including the artificial residue, axes, and corner, but leaves its constants unrestricted in \(X\) and gives no finite multiplier table.  Round 41 starts only **after** the routing and explicitly leaves the character, floors, stars, coefficient monomial, radial factor, and external normalization outside its local product kernel.  Round 42 then records precisely that the finite connector/module and \(j\)-scale assembly was not certified.

There are genuine passes.  The packet's identity

\[
 p_{j,x}(\nu)=e^{i\gamma_{j,x}\nu}\widehat\phi(b+i\nu),
 \qquad
 \gamma_{j,x}=\log\frac{2\sqrt X(H_j+1)}{D_j\sqrt x},
\]

is the accepted Round-41 profile.  Its singular share is confined to the \(j=0\), \(u^{-1}\) hard top, while the regular top remainder and interior \(\widehat W_j\)'s are smooth ordinary-\(\mu\) shares.  The packet's outer \((2\pi)^{-2}\) measure, with the extra \((2\pi)^{-1}\) in a smooth functional, also has the right terminal three-contour shape.  These facts show that a completed table could land in the proposed analytic class; they do not construct the table.

The floors and active endpoint block are likewise only stated, not mapped.  The permitted actual-vector formulas require every \(D_j,H_j+1,W_j\), floor, and star to remain attached, whereas \(\mathcal A_{\rm db}\) displays no row recording which physical equality star survives or which routed module owns it.  An abstract promise that exact stars are retained cannot verify one-count ownership.

## 4. First doubtful or unproved step

The first unproved step is the sentence in the packet that the actual compact remainder is "exactly this family."  At the first nontrivial mixed-connector seam, that sentence would require an explicit declaration such as

\[
 \tau=\tau_{A_uA_v},\qquad
 M_{\tau_{A_uA_v}}supset \frac14\psi''(\beta)\chi _0(\alpha),
                                                               \tag{43.L6}
\]

together with the actual pushforward of \(A_uA_v[Q_T]\), its sign and measure, and a proof that the \(\chi _0'\), \(\chi _0''\) shares were cancelled before the compact restriction without being counted in the already closed large-alpha package.  No such declaration appears.  If instead this term is claimed to be routed away, the packet must name the accepted target-safe module and show that it removes this beta terminal connector rather than only the globally recombined endpoint/arithmetic package.  Neither alternative is documented.

## 5. Required control test and outcome

The required control was a symbolic sixteen-row ownership trace, with no numerical experiment.

- **Profile and raw phase:** pass.  The packet reproduces the exact Round-41 modulation and \(L,\beta\) phases.
- **Hard top versus ordinary \(\mu\):** pass at the level of types.  Only \(j=0\), \(u^{-1}\) receives \(\mathsf P\); regular top and interior profiles receive \(\mathsf S\).
- **Internal alpha connector cancellation:** pass only for the sum of central and complementary alpha masks, by (43.L4); compact ownership after that cancellation is unspecified.
- **Beta connector and mixed connector trace:** fail.  No \(\tau\) is supplied for the four \(\psi'/2\) groups or the \(\psi''/4\) group in (43.L5).
- **Axes, connector axes, collision, and corner one-count:** fail as an audit.  The packet declares them excluded, but gives no row-to-module certificate.  The aggregate Round-36 identity is insufficient for a termwise beta exclusion.
- **Active \(j\) endpoint block, floors, and stars:** fail as a mapping test.  Their preservation is asserted but their exact rows and equality conventions are not displayed.
- **Measures and external factor:** pass conditionally for full-dimensional terminal product rows; untestable for excluded lower-dimensional rows until those rows are identified.  The external \(X^{1/4}\) factor is not duplicated in the displayed amplitude.

Overall outcome: **the family-class test passes, the actual-family equality test fails for missing data.**
