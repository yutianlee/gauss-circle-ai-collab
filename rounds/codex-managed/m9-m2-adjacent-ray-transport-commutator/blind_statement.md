# Round 111 blind statement

Let (Xge2), (J=sqrt X), and (1le Lle J^{1/2}). For odd
(a) and integer (q), define a finite zero-extended actual row

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 F_{L,a}(q)={}&1_{q\ge1}1_{(a,a+2q)=1}
 \sum_{\substack{g\ \mathrm{odd}\\ga,g(a+2q)\in\mathscr H_L}}
 \sum_{m=\lceil g(a+2q)/4\rceil}^{ga}
 A_L(ga,g(a+2q),m)\\
 &\quad\times
 e\!\left(J(\sqrt{ga}-\sqrt{g(a+2q)})\sqrt m\right),
 \end{aligned}
\tag{B111.1}
\]

where (mathscr H_L) is finite and half-open and (A_L) is the literal
actual coefficient, including its inherited floors, stars, half weights,
collars, entry/exit data, profiles, and zero extensions. No regularity of
(A_L) in (q) may be assumed unless derived from the statement.

The exact signed survivor is

\[
 \mathfrak C_L^{\mathrm{off}}
 =\sum_{a\ \mathrm{odd}}\sum_{q\in\mathbb Z}(-1)^qF_{L,a}(q).
\tag{B111.2}
\]

Derive the exact parity high-pass identities for a finite zero-extended
sequence, distinguish signed commutator, total adjacent variation, maximal
partial sums, row energy, and Gram norms, and give the weakest exact
commutator estimate that implies

\[
 \Re\mathfrak C_L^{\mathrm{off}}
 \ll_\varepsilon L^2X^\varepsilon.
\tag{B111.3}
\]

deaths, and arbitrary coefficient countermodels. Do not consult the proof
