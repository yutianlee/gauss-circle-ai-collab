# Round 169 barrier packet

## Accepted starting facts

1. The complete literal \(t=1\) scalar and all endpoint conventions are
   fixed in the accepted Round-168 kernel.
2. Its intact series factors as
   \(D=L(s_1,\chi_4)\zeta(s_2)G\), with \(G\) holomorphic for
   \(\Re s_1,\Re s_2>1/2\).
3. The exact cardinal representation has one target-safe zeta residue and
   leaves the signed two-height integral \(\mathcal I_\eta\).
4. On a favorable recombined smooth/BV model, the radial band has length
   \(JL\), transform scale \(L^{2\eta}\sqrt{L/J}\), and the two named
   absolute placements miss the target by \(\sqrt J\).
5. Every fixed-\(B\) polylogarithmic-\(L\) full scalar is target-safe; for
   fixed \(\kappa\), so is its exact residual complement.
6. Deliberately reopening the squarefree/coprime projector and using
   positive physical Poisson gives the accepted Round-162 product collar
   with capacity \(\sqrt{JL}\).  No factorwise-FE or AFE equivalence to
   that collar is accepted.

  no-go.

## Exact controls for a joint transform

### Residual Euler coefficients

For odd \(p\),

\[
 G_p=1-x_p^2-y_p^2-x_py_p+x_p^2y_p+x_py_p^2.
\]

Any coefficient expansion must retain the signs, \(\chi_4\)-powers,
joint prime support, the \(p=2\) factor \(1-2^{-2s_2}\), and the
weighted \(\ell^1\) cost as \(\eta\downarrow0\).  Treating \(G\) as a
bounded scalar after a functional equation is not an exact coefficient
argument.

### Functional-equation ledger

The completed zeta and odd real character functions have different gamma
parities and conductors.  Record their root numbers, gamma quotients,
poles, dual contour directions, and all residues.  An approximate
functional equation must state both pieces, lengths, weights, truncation,
and remainders for the actual independent heights.

### Literal weight

The exact cardinal transform is a sum of \(O(L^2)\) unit cells.  A global
smooth/BV model is only a control until a target-safe equality with the
literal array is proved.  Every real-centre floor, star, half-open shell,
cone edge, even-second-leg branch, profile transition, and zero value must
survive the joint transform or appear in a priced correction.

### Signed phase and power

The required gain is \(\sqrt J\) over the optimistic absolute capacity.
The gain must persist after summing every \(G\)-coefficient block, dual
length, gamma piece, modulus, spectrum, exceptional term, main term,
boundary, and completion error.  Taking absolute values over any of these
interfaces may spend the entire gain.

### Self-return

If the joint transform reconstructs the original coefficient series,
the Round-162 positive collar, or another representation with the same
\(\sqrt J\) deficit, state the coefficientwise map and the exact first
unpaid factor.  Structural analogy is not a self-return theorem.

## False controls

1. Replace \(\chi_4\) by \(1\).
2. Replace all arithmetic coefficients by phase-aligned unit weights.
3. Drop \(G\) or keep only its constant coefficient.
4. Restrict to \(t_1=t_2\).
5. Apply the favorable smooth stationary norm to one cardinal cell.
6. Sum transformed coefficient blocks absolutely.
7. Keep only a diagonal, zero-frequency, or principal spectral term.

An argument that also proves one of these controls must identify the extra
actual arithmetic input before promotion.

accepted XOR connector, its exact residual.  Other few-point channels and
collars, hard TOP, BAL, UNBAL, M9--M2, both direct M1 parents, GAR,
endpoint uniformity, M9, both bridges, the quarter theorem, and both
