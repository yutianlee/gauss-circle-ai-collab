## 1. Result

The literal all-inclusive kernel target is type-wrong. Radial horizontal
sides and explicit endpoint images are contour functionals, not pointwise
terminal \((L,\nu)\) symbols, and their raw hard-endpoint capacity cannot
satisfy a uniform \(\lambda^{-2}/\lambda^{-3}\) bound.

The correct architecture is:

1. use the accepted arbitrary-order radial subtraction to remove only the
   renormalized sides;
2. collapse the explicit endpoint and arithmetic pieces by the accepted
   finite Cauchy identity and route them to the already-proved endpoint and
   residue modules;
3. define a side-collapsed, endpoint-free terminal beta kernel;
4. extract the exact finite \(v=0\) vector residue and connectors;
5. prove the Round-31 weighted symbol bound only for the remaining terminal
   kernel.

The common omega combination equals \(R_1\) identically under identical
ownership, so rho Taylor and cutoff derivatives cancel to all orders. The
first surviving interface is a mask-compatible endpoint/axial ownership
identity, followed by the weighted terminal bound.

## 2. Exact statement and hypotheses

Let the accepted finite endpoint identity be

\[
 T_\xi+S_\xi+P_\xi=D_\xi-A_\xi,                   \tag{32.D1}
\]

and retain the separately proved lower endpoint, upper physical endpoint,
and recombined \(R_1\) arithmetic residue modules. Let
\(\mathcal V_{\rm nonend}\) be the beta vertical plus connector after
these modules are removed once in the accepted physical-limit order.

With identical masks, domains, stars, and one combined top regularization,
define conditionally

\[
 K_{\rm red}(L,\nu)=\operatorname {Reg}_{\mu=0}
 \Big[\mathcal V_{\rm nonend}
 \{\omega G+(1-\omega)R_1-\omega E_1\}\Big]_{\mu=L-\nu}.       \tag{32.D2}
\]

After an exact finite \(v\)-contour displacement extracts the height
residue and all matching side/arithmetic/corner shares, define
\(K_{\rm red}^\circ\) by removing that polar share. The desired terminal
bound is

\[
 |K_{\rm red}^\circ|\ll X^\varepsilon\lambda^{-2}w(\nu),
 \qquad |\partial_LK_{\rm red}^\circ|
 \ll X^\varepsilon\lambda^{-3}w(\nu),              \tag{32.D3}
\]

with \(w\in L^1\) and polylogarithmic norm.

## 3. Proof or derivation

For every compatible derivative,

\[
 \omega G+(1-\omega)R_1-\omega E_1=R_1,
 \qquad D^k(\text{left side})=D^kR_1,               \tag{32.D4}
\]

because \(G-E_1-R_1=0\). Hence all rho Laurent/Taylor defects and all
cutoff derivatives vanish when ownership is common.

Radial sides cannot enter (32.D3). Before endpoint subtraction the hard
side begins at \(S^{-1}\), while the left-edge degree-two factor has
capacity \(S^{1-2\ell}\), \(\ell<0\), giving growing capacity
\(S^{-2\ell}\). Arbitrary-order subtraction makes the remainder side
summable, but leaves an explicit endpoint operator. Identity (32.D1)
collapses that operator to the original endpoint and arithmetic modules;
reinserting it into (32.D2) would double count it.

Likewise the polar height norm is only logarithmic before differentiation,
whereas its absolute \(\nu\)-derivative costs \(b^{-1}\). The exact finite
\(v\)-residue must therefore be extracted, including terminal, radial-side,
arithmetic, top, and joint-corner shares, before (32.D3) is asked.

If (32.D3) holds, the Round-31 finite-section lemma gives terminal BV
\(O(X^\varepsilon\lambda^{-2})\); the accepted numerator then preserves
local \(q^{-2}\).

## 4. First doubtful or unproved step

The first missing exact identity is mask-compatible endpoint and axial
ownership. The finite endpoint Cauchy theorem and physical endpoint bounds
exist, but the packet does not yet prove that each beta mask and connector
share collapses to those same modules without a leftover boundary term.
Similarly, local subtraction of \((b+i\nu)^{-1}\) is not the full finite
\(v=0\) vector residue identity.

After this definition seam, (32.D3) is unproved for the axial-subtracted
terminal remainder. Full scale and external normalization sums remain
downstream.
