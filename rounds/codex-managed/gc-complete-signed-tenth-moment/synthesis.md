## What the arithmetic attack established

O(T^(7+epsilon)N^-1/2), below the target by T^(18/65). In a zero relation
of ten reduced rationals, a prime's maximal denominator valuation cannot
occur once. Thus the product of their denominators is squarefull. There
are O(R^(5+epsilon)) possible denominator tuples with maximum comparable
to R; choosing nine numerators determines the tenth, and their actual
coefficient weights cost only H/R. This includes both rational arms,
nonpairing equalities, the varying width and the moving prefix.

The original-root exact tenth mass is also harmless:
O(T N^(-5/2+epsilon)). The isolated rederivation counted every squarefree
class, not only pairings, and supplied an explicit displaced signed
control. These exact estimates do not bound the nonzero frequencies.

The actual rational nonzero near-frequency termwise integral majorant is

exceeds the target by T^(242/65). On the original frequency side, the
corresponding lower bound is N^2 and exceeds the physical target at
every fixed interior exponent alpha>166/325. Neither is a signed-moment
lower bound, nor an obstruction to cancellation after signed aggregation.

The proposed numerator-character orthogonality fails even on actual
same-denominator fibres: the positive-frequency coefficients have the
same character phase. An explicit nonpairing relation at q=3^k confirms
this. Other denominators and the other arm may still cancel; no global
impossibility is inferred.
