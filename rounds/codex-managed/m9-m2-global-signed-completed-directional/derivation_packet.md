\[
 \mathcal E_L^{\mathrm{top}}

\tag{110.1}

\[

\tag{110.2}

## 2. Immediate algebraic candidate

Define

\[
 \mathfrak C_L^{\mathrm{comp}}
 =\sum_B\mathfrak Q_B^{\mathrm{comp}}.
\tag{110.3}
\]

Then (110.1)--(110.2) formally give

\[
 \mathcal E_L^{\mathrm{top}}
 =\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{\mathrm{owned},L}
  +2\Re\mathfrak C_L^{\mathrm{comp}},
 \qquad
 |\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{\mathrm{owned},L}|
 \ll_\varepsilon L^2X^\varepsilon.
\tag{110.4}
\]

The conductor candidate is that the exact remaining hard-top theorem is
the one-sided scalar estimate

\[
 \boxed{\quad
 \Re\mathfrak C_L^{\mathrm{comp}}
 \ll_\varepsilon L^2X^\varepsilon.
 \quad}
\tag{110.5}
\]

The modulus bound is sufficient but stronger. The blockwise
outside-absolute estimate is stronger still. A positive Gram estimate
is not automatically equivalent.

\tag{110.6}
