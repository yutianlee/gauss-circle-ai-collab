# Conductor candidate: global completed scalar is the minimal hard-top target

From the accepted physical energy identity and Round-109 owner
completion,

\[
 \mathcal E_L^{\mathrm{top}}
 =\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{\mathrm{owned},L}
  +2\Re\mathfrak C_L^{\mathrm{comp}},
 \qquad
 |\widetilde{\mathcal E}_{\mathrm{owned},L}|
 \ll_\varepsilon L^2X^\varepsilon.
\]

Therefore the hard-top energy theorem is equivalent, up to
target-sized terms, to the one-sided estimate

\[
 \Re\mathfrak C_L^{\mathrm{comp}}
 \ll_\varepsilon L^2X^\varepsilon.
\]

The candidate must be certified only after the complete block sum is
written on one common finite atom space. The modulus estimate is a
convenient stronger target; blockwise absolute values and the fixed-a
Gram are not algebraically necessary.

The intended mathematical opportunity is that
\(\mathfrak C_L^{\mathrm{comp}}\) still contains the two primitive
characters, whereas the positive Gram cancels them. No cancellation
claim is made here.
