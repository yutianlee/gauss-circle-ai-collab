# Statement-only packet: global signed completed directional interface

Let \(L\ge1\). Suppose a finite real energy has the exact decomposition

\[
 E_L=E_{\mathrm{safe},L}
     +2\Re\sum_{B\in\mathcal B_L}Q_B^{\mathrm{res}},
 \qquad
 |E_{\mathrm{safe},L}|\le C L^2X^\varepsilon.
\tag{B110.1}
\]

For every finite block \(B\), suppose

\[
 Q_B^{\mathrm{res}}
 =Q_B^{\mathrm{comp}}-\sum_{\nu\in\mathcal O_B}O_{B,\nu},
 \qquad
 \sum_{B,\nu}|O_{B,\nu}|
 \le C L^2X^\varepsilon.
\tag{B110.2}
\]

Every set is finite. One representative orientation is used, followed
by the single outer \(2\Re\) in (B110.1). The completed blocks retain the
same one-count partitions and zero extensions.

Independently determine:

1. the weakest scalar norm or one-sided estimate on
   \(C_L^{\mathrm{comp}}=\sum_BQ_B^{\mathrm{comp}}\) that is sufficient
   and, up to target-sized terms, necessary for \(E_L\ll L^2X^\varepsilon\);
2. whether blockwise absolute estimates or a Gram estimate are required;
3. finite countermodels to every invalid norm upgrade;
4. the exact hypotheses needed to exchange, merge, or remove the finite
   block partition.

Do not use any Gauss-circle history, candidate formula, or sibling
report. Treat all coefficients as complex unless the displayed
real-part identity forces otherwise.
