## 3. Proof and hostile derivation

### 3.1 Abel sign, endpoint normalization, and projective reindexing

For any finitely supported $W$, zero extension gives

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \sum_h\Delta^-W(h)z^h
 &=\sum_hW(h)z^h-\sum_hW(h-1)z^h\\
 &=(1-z)\sum_hW(h)z^h.
 \end{aligned}
\tag{191.H14}
\]

Thus (191.H11) is exactly the original height sum, including the birth
at its first site and the death one step after its last site. There is
no endpoint remainder and no cancellation supplied by Abel summation.

For $1\leq j\leq(q-1)/2$,

\[
 4{j\over q}\leq |1-e(j/q)|\leq2\pi {j\over q}.
\tag{191.H15}
\]

Consequently, on $J\leq j<2J$,

\[
 {q\over4\pi J}\leq |1-z|^{-1}\leq {q\over4J}.
\tag{191.H16}
\]

Both orientations have the same normalization.

The map $v\bmod q\mapsto b=a\bar v_q\bmod q$ is a bijection on units,
but only the bucketed reindexing

\[
 \sum_{\substack{v\ {\rm literal}\\b_v\in\mathcal B_J}}F(v,b_v)
 =\sum_{b\in\mathcal B_J\cap\mathbb U(q)}
   \sum_{\substack{v\ {\rm literal}\\
                    v\equiv a\bar b_q\pmod q}}F(v,b)
\tag{191.H17}
\]

is exact. The inner coefficient still depends on the actual integer
$v$, its affine sites, masks, and endpoints. Since the literal
$v$-range has length $O(u)$ and $q\mid u$, each class occurs
$O(u/q+1)=O(u/q)$ times, with no stray additive term. There are
$O(J)$ signed slope classes. This proves (191.H13), but not a common or
free height weight.

\tag{191.H18}

\[

\tag{191.H19}

 \ell_+(h)={S_{0,+}(h)-\rho-S_{0,+}(h-1)\over U},\qquad

\tag{191.H20}

These are integers in $\{-1,0,1\}$, and

\[
 \begin{array}{ll}

### 3.3 Proof of the signed-inverse strict sector

The map

\[
 v\bmod U\longmapsto\rho_U(v)\bmod U
\tag{191.H29}
\]

is a bijection on $\mathbb U(U)$. For
$0\leq R\leq(U-1)/2$, the number of unit classes with
$1\leq|\rho_U(v)|\leq R$ is exactly

\[
 2\sum_{\substack{1\leq r\leq R\\(r,U)=1}}1\leq2R.
\tag{191.H30}
\]

The literal $v$-range has length $O(u)$. Since $U\mid u$, a class
modulo $U$ occurs $O(u/U+1)=O(u/U)$ times. Literal masks only delete.
Therefore

\[
 \#\{v:1\leq|\rho_U(v)|\leq R_\rho\}
 \ll {uR_\rho\over U}\leq {Qmu\over Y}.
\tag{191.H31}
\]

This includes the floor-zero and saturated cases. At saturation every
unit $v$-class is included and the complementary packet is empty for
that lift. Intersecting with the projectively fast predicate or a
$J$-band only decreases the count.

There are $O(Y)$ heights, $O(1+\kappa)=O(\kappa)$ affine sites per
oriented row, and $O_\eta(X^\eta)$ per atom. Hence

\[
 |\mathscr J_{\rho\text{-small}}(a)|
 \ll_\eta {Qmu\over Y}\,Y\kappa X^\eta
 \ll_\eta Qm\kappa uX^\eta,
\tag{191.H32}
\]

where (191.H14) permits use of either the original sum or its jump form.
No orientation pairing or BV estimate is used.

Restoring the outer coefficient gives

\[
 {1\over m}|c_q(a)|\,Qm\kappa u
 =Q\kappa u|c_q(a)|.
\tag{191.H33}
\]

Since

\[
 \sum_{a\in\mathbb U(q)}|c_q(a)|\ll\log(2q),\qquad
 \sum_{mq\mid u}1=\tau_3(u),
\tag{191.H34}
\]

the full ledger is

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 QX^\eta\sum_{\kappa\ll L}\sum_{u\asymp L/\kappa}
 \kappa u\,\tau_3(u)\log(2u)

\tag{191.H35}

Dyadic $Y$- and projective-band counts add fixed logarithms. Equation
(191.H35) uses $L\ll X^{1/4}$ and a fresh epsilon budget. It absorbs no

### 3.4 Full anchor parity and the fast-projector self-return

Before Fourier expansion, $U$ odd gives

\[
 (-1)^{S_{0,\omega}(h)}(-1)^t=(-1)^{S_{h,t,\omega}}.
\tag{191.H36}
\]

Under (191.H18) or (191.H19), the old and transported site parities
differ by the constant

\[
 {(-1)^{S_{h,t,\omega}}\over
  (-1)^{S_{h-1,t+\ell_\omega(h),\omega}}}
 =(-1)^\rho.
\tag{191.H37}
\]

Equivalently, the canonical anchor contributes
$(-1)^{\rho+\ell_\omega(h)}$, the affine-index change contributes
$(-1)^{\ell_\omega(h)}$, and the carries cancel because $U-1$ is even.

The constant law does not hold for an individual retained Fourier mode.
Since $\rho\equiv\bar v\pmod U$, hence modulo $q$, the old/new
mode-and-affine factor is

\[
 (-1)^{\ell_\omega(h)}e(\epsilon_\omega k\rho/U)
 =(-1)^{\ell_\omega(h)}e(\epsilon_\omega a\rho/q).
\tag{191.H38}
\]

It depends on the carry and mode. The constant $(-1)^\rho$ is
reconstructed only after all Fourier modes are recombined. Multiplication
by the carry sequence mixes Fourier modes, so the
high-mode/conductor/lift/projective projector has a nonzero commutator
with that multiplication. For $U=p$ prime, a single retained near-half

\[
 (-1)^{\ell(h)}e((p-1)\rho/(2p)),
\tag{191.H39}
\]

not $(-1)^\rho$. Recombining omitted modes restores (191.H37) but
returns to the complete Round-185 high-height packet of positive
capacity $YL^2X^\varepsilon$. Even in a fictional
transport-invariant coefficient model, (191.H37) is constant $+1$ for
even $\rho$, so bare parity has no uniform height cancellation.

### 3.6 Operator capacity and rejected transform routes

Let $\mathcal R$ be any finite set of oriented projective rows and let
row $r$ have $n_r$ consecutive heights. As a functional on
zero-extended arrays with $\|W_r\|_\infty\leq1$, (191.H14) gives

\[
 \left\|
 \sum_{r\in\mathcal R}{1\over1-z_r}
 \sum_h\Delta^-W_r(h)z_r^h
 \right\|_{\ell^\infty\to\mathbb C}
 =\sum_{r\in\mathcal R}n_r.
\tag{191.H46}
\]

The upper bound is triangle inequality. Equality is attained by

\[
 W_r(h)=\overline{z_r}^{\,h}
\tag{191.H47}
\]

on the row interval and zero outside. Every row then contributes the
positive real number $n_r$, and both orientations may be included in
the same construction. Boundary jumps are exactly those required by
zero extension. If the exact square-root phase is inserted as a
prescribed unit-modulus atom multiplier, a bounded array may conjugate
that multiplier too. Thus support, boundedness, displayed phases, and
the word coboundary cannot prove a coefficient-uniform saving.

For $M_J\ll uJ/q$ rows of length $O(Y)$, the capacity is

\[
 \kappa YM_JX^\eta
 \ll \kappa Y{uJ\over q}X^\eta.
\tag{191.H48}
\]

At maximal permitted multiplicity this exceeds (191.H12) by $D_J$.
The separately absolutized variation route is weaker:

\[
 \mathsf V(W_r)\leq2\sum_h|W_r(h)|
 \ll_\eta Y\kappa X^\eta,
\tag{191.H49}
\]

and therefore

\[
 |\mathscr J_{a,J}|
 \ll_\eta {q\over J}{uJ\over q}Y\kappa X^\eta
 \ll_\eta Y\kappa uX^\eta.
\tag{191.H50}
\]

This exceeds the target by the full $D$.

The same capacity rejects the proposed false routes:

1. **Separable/free weights.** For $M$ distinct slopes, the matrix
   $z_b^{-h}$ on consecutive heights is Vandermonde of rank
   $\min(M,Y)$ and gives value $MY$ in (191.H46). Bounded rank, a
   common height weight, or a low-cost separated expansion is an extra
   hypothesis. Expansion by height slices followed by positive norms
   returns (191.H48), deficit $D_J$.

2. **Projective neighbors.** Where $b,b+1$ are units,

   \[
   v_b\equiv a\bar b_q\pmod q,\qquad
   v_{b+1}-v_b\equiv-{a\over b(b+1)}\pmod q.
   \tag{191.H51}
   \]

   Neighboring $b$'s need not be neighboring integer values of $v$,
   affine rows, endpoints, or masks. There is no identity
   $G_b=H_{b+1}-H_b$. For (191.H47),
   $G_b=(1-z_b)Y$, so $(1-z_b)^{-1}G_b=Y$ is constant and reinforcing
   across $b$. The deficit is $D_J$.

3. **Completion, large sieve, and alias energy.** The row-wise
   modulation operator has $\ell^2$ norm $\sqrt Y$. Globally its
   coefficient vector has norm $\sqrt{YM_J}$, while a bounded array may
   have norm $\kappa\sqrt{YM_J}$. Cauchy gives
   $\kappa YM_J$, with equality for (191.H47). A large sieve requires a
   common coefficient sequence; here the coefficient is a matrix indexed
   by the frequency row. Repeated $v$'s in one residue class are aliases,
   not new orthogonal frequencies. Positive completion and alias norms
   retain deficit $D_J$, or $D$ after positive variation.

4. **Transport-only cancellation.** An unrestricted lattice transport is
   exact, but a common transported atom contributes

   \[
   (-1)^t\{B_{\omega,h}(t)
   -(-1)^{\ell_\omega(h)}
    B_{\omega,h-1}(t+\ell_\omega(h))\}.
   \tag{191.H52}
   \]

   Equations (191.H26), (191.H28), and (191.H42) show that the terms
   are not equal. Births and deaths occur whenever transport leaves a
   literal support. Taking positive norms gives (191.H50), deficit $D$.
   Restoring all modes to use (191.H37) is a self-return.

5. **Phase-only cancellation.** A theorem uniform over bounded
   amplitudes multiplying the exact inverse and square-root phases is
   falsified by conjugating those phases as in (191.H47). Its band
   deficit is $D_J$, reaching $D$ for $J\asymp q$. A signed literal
   phase correlation could still be true, but it is a new theorem;
   neither small variation nor nonstationarity is established.

6. **Positive divisor/sieve opening.** Opening
   $\mathbf1_{(U,h)=1}$ contains the $d=1$ progression of length $Y$;
   small divisor progressions can have linearly many mask changes.
   Squarefree and allocation-coprimality openings add congruence labels
   while the selected-prime mask, profiles, endpoints, and phase remain
   moving. Positive recombination gives (191.H50), deficit $D$. No
   hidden Bombieri--Vinogradov or free-weight hypothesis is available.

These controls refute only the indicated theorem classes. They are not
lower bounds for the literal packet.

### 3.7 Every literal jump source

Let
$\widetilde B_{\omega,h}(t)=\mathbf1_{I_{\omega,h}}(t)B_{\omega,h}(t)$
include all zero extensions. Before product opening, the exact affine

\[
\begin{aligned}
 W_\omega(h)-W_\omega(h-1)
 ={}&\sum_{t\in I_h\cap I_{h-1}}(-1)^t
       \{B_{\omega,h}(t)-B_{\omega,h-1}(t)\}\\
 &+\sum_{t\in I_h\setminus I_{h-1}}(-1)^tB_{\omega,h}(t)
 -\sum_{t\in I_{h-1}\setminus I_h}(-1)^tB_{\omega,h-1}(t),
\end{aligned}
\tag{191.H53}
\]

or (191.H52) on a transported common range. For an ordered list of
literal fields $f_1,\ldots,f_s$,

\[
 \prod_{r=1}^sf_r(h)-\prod_{r=1}^sf_r(h-1)
 =\sum_{j=1}^s\{f_j(h)-f_j(h-1)\}
   \prod_{r<j}f_r(h)\prod_{r>j}f_r(h-1)
\tag{191.H54}
\]

assigns every common-range change once. Applied literally, these
identities include:

- the dyadic height indicator, carrier cutoff
  $0<2\kappa gh<R_0$, and terminal zero extension;
- canonical-anchor carries, affine positivity, and all site births and
  deaths;
- $(U,h)=1$, squarefree endpoint masks, allocation coprimalities, and
  the moving residual selected-prime mask;
- both endpoint coefficients, shell and cone predicates, profiles,
  floors, stars, half-weights, hard samples, real-$X$ crossings, and
  endpoint inclusions;
- the Fejer factor $1-2\kappa gh/R_0$, conjugation/order, all signs, and
  both orientations; and
- the actual square-root phase (191.H40).

Two isolated pieces are positively target-safe. The dyadic and terminal
zero extensions and the single carrier-cutoff death have
$O(\kappa)$ jump mass per row. The Fejer scalar has step
$2\kappa g/R_0$; because a nonempty $h\asymp Y$ block has
$2\kappa gY<R_0$, its isolated common-range variation is

\[
 Y\cdot O(\kappa)\cdot{2\kappa g\over R_0}X^\eta
 \ll\kappa X^\eta
\tag{191.H55}
