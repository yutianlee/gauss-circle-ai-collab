## Accepted result

The fixed interior reciprocal cone has the exact short-product form

\[
 \sum_{n\ge1}A_{X,\Xi}(n)
 K\!\left(\frac{n-X}{2\sqrt{X/N}}\right)+O_B(X^{-B}),
 \qquad
 A_{X,\Xi}(n)=\sum_{j\mid n}\chi_4(j)\Xi(j/\sqrt X),
\]

with \(K=\widehat g\) and \(g(t)=\mathbf1_{t>0}V(t^2)/t\).
All moments of \(K\) vanish. More strongly, its samples on the shifted
integer lattice annihilate every polynomial exactly once the dilation is
larger than the support radius of \(g\).

This does not annihilate the arithmetic coefficient or exact products.

absolute bound is \(X^\varepsilon\sqrt{X/N}\), leaving the factor
\(X^{1/4-\nu/2}\), exactly the accepted \(H/L\) near-product deficit.
