## Decision

Promote three narrowly scoped results:

1. the frozen exact M2 second moment;
2. the exact moving M1/M2 main-block moment under the accepted profiles,
   floors, and hard prefix;
3. the assembled real-variable theorem

   \[
   \int_Y^{2Y}|P(t)|^2dt\ll_\varepsilon Y^{3/2+\varepsilon},
   \]

   and its density-one \(1/4+\eta\) consequence.

Mark the Xiao source audit complete without an implication edge. Reject
arbitrary moving weights, naive floor-by-floor freezing, zero-margin
density saving, and all pointwise/canonical-core implications.

## Proof status after the round

The accepted uniform theorem remains

\[
 P(X)\ll_\varepsilon X^{1/3+\varepsilon}.
\]

There is now a separate unconditional metric theorem: for every fixed
\(\eta>0\), outside a set of measure
\(O_\varepsilon(Y^{1-2\eta+\varepsilon})\) in \([Y,2Y]\),

\[
 |P(t)|\leq Y^{1/4+\eta}.
\]

This is genuine exponent progress for almost every real \(t\), but no
improvement of the global pointwise exponent. The next interface is
exceptional-set removal: a local large-value or short-interval theorem
that can control a prescribed point without assuming the open canonical
M1/M2 estimates.
