# Conductor candidate: frozen M2 second moment

For the data in the Round-93 packet, group \((h,d)\) by the reduced fraction
\(a/b=h/d\).  Since \(kb\asymp D\),

\[
 |A_{a,b}|\le {1\over\pi|a|}
 \sum_{k\asymp D/b}{1\over k}\ll {1\over|a|}.
\]

Also \(b\ll D\) and \(1\le|a|\ll Hb/D\), so

\[
 \sum_{a,b}|A_{a,b}|^2
 \ll\sum_{b\ll D}\sum_{a\ne0}{1\over a^2}\ll D.
\]

Distinct \(a/(4b)\) are \(\gg D^{-2}\)-separated.  The continuous
large-sieve inequality therefore predicts

\[
 \int_I|S(t)|^2dt\ll(V+D^2)D.
\]

Chebyshev gives the packet's exceptional-set bounds.  This candidate is
deliberately frozen.  The actual moving-\(H(t)\) transfer and its endpoint
cost are the hostile seam, and no pointwise conclusion is claimed.
