strip-edge connectors. Every outside \(u\)-, \(v\)-, and \(s\)-side

\[

\]

after the hard top contributes its single inverse-height factor. This
grows in the admissible range; absolute integration does not close the

The standalone inverse Mellin kernel is

\[
 \frac1{2\pi i}\int X_\zeta(r)Y^{-r}\,dr=2\cos(2\pi Y)

in its fundamental strip and by continuation against permitted compact
profiles. At integral \(Y\), the high unsigned cosine sum is coherent.
Any coefficientwise cancellation theorem must pass this control. A
transition mask convolves the pure cosine kernel, so coherence disproves
the naive coefficientwise route but is not by itself a lower bound for
the fully coupled signed operator.

There is no ordinary simultaneous Dirichlet chamber with
\(\Re A>1\) and \(\Re(1-B)>0\) when \(\Re z>0\). Eta or pole-subtracted
representations retain a high Mellin factor or reconstruct the unsigned
dyadic tower; they do not automatically yield decay.
