## Cellwise graph theorem

For a tensor of selected complete reduction cells, define

\[
 \mathfrak b_\sigma=
 \prod_{p^\nu\in S}{p^2\over\eta_{\omega_p}}.
\]

Both physical directed degrees satisfy

\[
 \Delta_\sigma\leq {M^2\over\mathfrak b_\sigma}.
\]

Consequently every tensor with

\[
 \mathfrak b_\sigma\geq M^2/\rho_*^2
\]

is target-safe.  A union must pay
(M^2\sum_\sigma\mathfrak b_\sigma^{-1}).  The conditional
(M=3^\nu), ([1:2:1]), (M/3\leq\rho_*<M) family is a nonempty newly
owned example.

## Capacity obstruction and exact survivor

The full nonunit union can have degree (q^2).  Exact (q=4,8,9)
controls show that lower period and large completed traces coexist with
high physical degree.  At the top conductor,

\[
 M^2\asymp J^{3/10},\qquad \rho_*^2=J^{2/15},
\]

so the worst residual retains the factor (J^{1/6}) above the target
degree.  The unresolved operator consists of the residual cells failing
the conductor threshold together with affine-only, projection-only, and
aperiodic factors, with the complete fourfold symbol and
(Q^{-5/12}) row gain retained.
