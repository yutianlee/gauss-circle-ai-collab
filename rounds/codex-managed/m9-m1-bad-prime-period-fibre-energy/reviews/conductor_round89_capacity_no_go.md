# Round 89 conductor review: capacity and no-go

For a physical edge package of in/out degree (Delta), the inherited
bound is

\[
 \operatorname{Cap}_{M1}(\mathcal E)
 =DB^3\Delta T^4Q^{-5/6}.
\]

Relative to the target, its normalized capacity gap is

\[
 \Gamma_{M1}(\mathcal E)=\Delta B^4J^{-11/15}.
\]

At (B=J^{3/20}), a full-degree factor has
(Delta\asymp M^2\asymp J^{3/10}), whence

\[
 \Gamma_{M1}\asymp J^{1/6}.
\]

The hostile (q=8) cell has exact lower-period structure while retaining
full physical degree, so this deficit survives the bad-prime
classification.  The (q=4,q=8,q=9) controls also show that local trace
depth is not a coefficientwise square-root estimate.

This is a route-specific capacity theorem, not a lower bound for the full
signed operator.  It terminates open-ended local period peeling: the next
step must use a signed actual-symbol interaction or prove a formal
self-return/barrier theorem.
