# Conductor candidate: bad-prime cellwise period graph

reduction cell at each selected prime-power factor.  For a unit local
phase, the reciprocal mask is periodic precisely when its complete-mask
derivative vanishes on every allowed residue.  At (p=2,3,5,7), the
successful normalized reductions are exactly the table in the
constructive report.  The five transverse (p=3) rays and four
transverse (p=5) rays have exact reciprocal depth one for every lift.

For a selected cell (omega=[a:b:v]), put (eta_omega=1) when both
(a,b) are nonzero or for the zero cell, and otherwise put
(eta_omega=p-1).  If

\[
 \mathfrak b_sigma=prod_{p^\nu\in S}{p^2\over\eta_{omega_p}},
\]

then fixing a source and fixing a target separately gives

\[
 \deg^+(\mathcal E_sigma),\deg^-(\mathcal E_sigma)
 \leq {M^2\over\mathfrak b_sigma}.
\]

Therefore the complete-fibre tensor is target-safe whenever

\[
 \mathfrak b_sigma\geq M^2/\rho_*^2.
\]

\[
 \sum_{\sigma\in\Sigma}\mathfrak b_sigma^{-1}
 \leq {\rho_*^2\over M^2};
\]

no union gain may be inferred merely from period depth.  The conditional
power-of-three example (M=3^\nu), ([a:b:v]=[1:2:1]),
(M/3\leq\rho_*<M) is a nonempty newly owned package.

period formulas are accepted only as exact routing/lower-depth
