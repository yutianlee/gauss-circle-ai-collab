# 3. Proof or derivation

First work at fixed finite contours.  By H1 and H2, summing all hierarchical masks and all sixteen strata cancels every mask-derivative contribution and gives the ordinary unmasked finite Cauchy identity.  Thus there is only one common antecedent.  No row of the sixteen-stratum table is separately declared to be a physical amplitude.

Now perform only the routed operations frozen in the packet.  H3 kills every transferred renormalized radial side.  H4 transfers the endpoint and recombined arithmetic terms only after full aggregation.  H5 gives the signed Plemelj limit and the actual global positive-b tail.  H6 keeps every exceptional share at its one-count owner.  Finally, H7 gives the exact germ cancellation

\[
 R_{\mathrm{germ}}
 :=\omega G+(1-\omega)R_1-\omega E_1-R_1=0.
 \tag{3.1}
\]

Therefore the routed physical object is precisely \(\mathcal B_+\); no cutoff-derivative, connector, radial-side, or germ correction remains to be appended to it.

By H8, the alpha selectors are applied only now, to this single returned object.  Multiplying H9 by the complete finite vector componentwise gives (2.1).  In particular, the only possible terminal partition residual is

\[
 R_\beta
 :=\mathcal B_+-P_0\mathcal B_+-\sum_kP_k\mathcal B_+
                  -\sum_jQ_j\mathcal B_+
 =\left(1-P_0-\sum_kP_k-\sum_jQ_j\right)\mathcal B_+=0.
 \tag{3.2}
\]

This equation also gives the omission and double-count controls.  The compact, middle, and inner/outer selectors exhaust unity exactly once.  The two oriented artificial-residue shares and the exceptional interfaces in H6 are owned components of the finite vector under this componentwise partition; their separate mention records their bounds and owners, not a second addition to (2.1).  H14 rules out a second copy of any floor, star, equality condition, \(\chi_4\), collision convention, contour factor, radial coefficient, or endpoint coefficient.

The target estimates now enter only after the exact algebra.  H10--H13 control, respectively, the compact cells, both oriented artificial-residue components, the full middle group, and all inner/outer nonsaddle cells.  Since the terminal selector families are finite, summing their accepted estimates preserves the same \(O_\varepsilon\) exponent.  Linearity of the finite sum and of \(\operatorname{Re}\) then gives (2.2), with H15 applied once to the assembled normalized amplitude.

Neither the lemma statement, its hypotheses, the coefficient, nor the kernel-identification formula appears in the accepted-input sections.

The packet supplies neither \(\Delta_{W3}=0\) nor a separate estimate for \(\Delta_{W3}\).
