## 4. First doubtful or unproved step

There is no unproved beta step inside the scoped W1--W4 chain.  The first
unproved implication is the next one: W4 does **not** by itself prove
`M9-M1-swept-transition-horizontal-operator`.  The accepted partial
functional-equation reduction has a second, alpha-bounded zeta-high trace,
whereas W4 expressly excludes it.  Thus the current `implies` entry from
`M9-M1-beta-transition-connector-reduction` to the swept operator cannot be
used as a direct promotion license; it must mean only that W4 supplies the
beta branch, or it should be replaced by an explicit open alpha-branch
dependency.

Two nearby strengthenings are also unproved and must not be smuggled into
the promoted wording: termwise physical limits of individual beta-masked
sixteen-stratum rows, and an absolute physical-height norm for the
unintegrated hard-top diagonal.  Neither is needed for W1--W4.
