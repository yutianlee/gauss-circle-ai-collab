\[

 =-{i\over2h}\sum_{a\bmod4h}\chi_4(a)e(an/(4h)).

\[

\[

The coefficient has critical size \(R^{-1/2}\).

## 4. Why the short window remains open

A length-\(R\) primal window produces \(\asymp R\) dual integers for each
of \(\asymp R\) values of \(h\).  The raw strip therefore has capacity
\(R^2\); after the stationary factor this is \(R^{3/2}\), whereas the
target is \(\sqrt R\).  A signed raw \(O_\varepsilon(RX^\varepsilon)\)
theorem is still required.

After the shear \(r=4Kh+s\), the reciprocal phase has
\(\det\nabla^2=-X^2/r^4\asymp-1\) and two opposite order-one eigenvalues.
The lattice phase \(uv\) proves that determinant alone supplies no
cancellation.  The accepted terminal M1 theorem also does not immediately
apply to the short window because its stationary selector is an
\(h\)-dependent radial strip, not a separable coefficient.
