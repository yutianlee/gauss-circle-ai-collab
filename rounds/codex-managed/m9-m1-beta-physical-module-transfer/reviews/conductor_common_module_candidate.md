# Conductor common finite module candidate

Campaign: `m9-m1-beta-physical-module-transfer`  
Role: conductor synthesis-in-progress  
Allocation: 100% analytical/algebraic

Define at fixed finite heights, with every scale/profile factor retained,

\[
 \mathfrak M_{\rm fin}
 :=\sum_{\xi\in\{1,N_X\}}\mathfrak D_{\xi;U,V,S}
   +\mathfrak R^{\rm ar}_{U,V}[R_1].               \tag{36.M1}
\]

The unmasked finite endpoint identities give

\[
 \mathfrak T_\xi+\mathfrak S_\xi+\mathfrak P_\xi
 =\mathfrak D_\xi-\mathfrak A_\xi,
\qquad
 \mathfrak R^{\rm ar}[G]-\sum_\xi\mathfrak A_\xi
 =\mathfrak R^{\rm ar}[R_1].                      \tag{36.M2}
\]

After summing the three hierarchical shares before residue extraction,
\(\sum_r\Theta_r=1\) and all derivative connectors cancel. The complete
finite two-axis operation on the endpoint/arithmetic package therefore
reduces, by (36.M2), to (36.M1). This is an equality of **combined finite
functionals**, not termwise convergence of their branch pieces.

The accepted physical theorems establish the joint limits of the pieces in
(36.M1): both endpoint prefixes and \(R^{\rm ar}[R_1]\), with their common
stars and actual profiles. Hence, if (36.M1) is indeed the whole image after
the finite outside transfer, its physical limit is already target-safe and
no new connector convergence is required.

The exact audit question is whether the symbol \(\mathfrak D_{\xi;U,V,S}\)
in (36.M1) already includes the ordinary outside-axis displacement faces and
residues generated by the Round-34 transfer. Since it is itself an integral
over the original positive outside \(u,v\) contours, a transfer that moves
those contours would normally express it as a negative-line term plus
horizontals and residues, not erase them. Equation (36.M1) can be the full
image only if the intended “axial vector removed” is precisely the total
difference between these contour presentations and product Stokes returns
that difference to the original positive-line functional. The final graph
statement must make this convention explicit; otherwise it risks calling
an identity of full contour integrals an estimate for one selected residue
vector.

Thus promotion requires the reports to verify two facts:

1. the complete transfer is used as a full equality, including its final
   negative-line term, rather than only as the axial/residue subtotal;
2. the endpoint/arithmetic module is removed as the entire original-line
   functional (36.M1), so its alternate contour representation need not be
   estimated term by term.

If either fails, an ordinary transferred endpoint face remains open.
