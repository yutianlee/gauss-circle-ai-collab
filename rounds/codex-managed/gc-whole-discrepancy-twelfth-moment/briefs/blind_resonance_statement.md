with |a_n|<=C tau(n)n^(-3/4), prove or refute a cutoff-uniform bound

\[
 \sum_{\epsilon\in\{-1,1\}^k}
 \sum_{\epsilon_1\sqrt{n_1}+\cdots+\epsilon_k\sqrt{n_k}=0}
 \prod_{j=1}^k |a_{n_j}|\ll_{k,C}1.

a Gaussian pairing. Derive the squarefree-class criterion rigorously,
