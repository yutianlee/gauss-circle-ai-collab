# Statement-only Round 151 problem

Let

$$
 R=X^{1/4},\qquad N=\lfloor X\rfloor,\qquad
 DE\asymp M\leq R^2,\qquad D\leq\sqrt M,\qquad
 Q=2\sqrt{ND/E}.
$$

For squarefree (d\asymp D), consider the exact compressed row

$$
 G_U(d)=
 \sum_{\substack{(L,q)=1\\q\asymp LQ}}
 \chi_4(Lq)\frac{B_{d,U}(L)}L
 \mathscr W_{d,U}(L/q)e(NdL/q),
$$

where (q) is odd, the finite prefix is literal, and

$$
 \sum_L\frac{|B_{d,U}(L)|}{\sqrt L}
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon.
$$

For two cells write

$$
 q_i=hr_i,\qquad(r_1,r_2)=1,\qquad
 \delta=L_1r_2-L_2r_1,
$$

and choose the centered (k\in\mathbb Z) so that

$$
 \rho=N\delta-khr_1r_2,
 \qquad |\rho|\leq hr_1r_2/2.
$$

Assume as proved that every fixed (k) contributes at most
(DQX^\varepsilon) in coefficient-weighted absolute mass.  Hence any
selected (O(1+R^2/Q)) wraps, including (k=0) and a symmetric packet
(mathcal K_0), are target-safe.  Exact (
ho=0) and bounded (M)
are also excluded as already controlled.

Independently determine whether the complete signed contribution from

$$
 k\notin\mathcal K_0,\qquad
 0<|\rho|\leq hr_1r_2/D
$$

is

$$
 \ll_\varepsilon R^2D X^\varepsilon
$$

uniformly in every aspect and every actual clipped prefix.  The atom
retains both (B)'s, both sampled profiles, all incidence masks,
common factors, imprimitive denominators, and
(chi_4(L_1L_2r_1r_2)).

For (k\ne0), you may begin with

$$
 A=NL_1-khr_1,\qquad B=NL_2+khr_2,
$$

$$
 AB=N^2L_1L_2+kh\rho,
$$

but every divisibility, parity, sign, and recovery condition must be
proved.  Test whether factoring (2^{\nu_2(k)}) transfers the
mod-four character to the two shifted factors and whether that transfer
survives joint summation over all shifts.

The (D=1,L_1=L_2=1) endpoint is compulsory.  There the row contains

$$
 S_U(N,Q)=
 \sum_{q\asymp Q}\chi_4(q)
 \mathscr W_{1,U}(1/q)e(N/q).
$$

Use the actual reciprocal profile inherited from
(mathscr W_{1,U}(1/q)=\mathscr A_{1,M,U}(1,4N/q^2)), including its
dyadic, cone, radial, and clipped-prefix transitions.  Derive, or
rigorously obstruct, a discrete (B)-process using

$$
 \chi_4(q)=\frac{e(q/4)-e(-q/4)}{2i}.
$$

State all endpoint and derivative hypotheses explicitly.  Determine
whether the transform gives a new target-sized (M)-range or returns
to a square-root wave with no power gain.  Check the bounded-(M),
intermediate, and (M\asymp R^2) regimes separately.

Prove the full target, a strict owner-complete range, or the first exact
character-transfer, (B)-process, endpoint, coefficient, source, or
power obstruction.  Do not sum fixed shifts absolutely, replace the
actual profile by an arbitrary bounded smooth function, hide a dual
main sum in an error, or turn an adverse capacity into a signed lower
bound.  The growing-(M) generic complement is outside scope.
