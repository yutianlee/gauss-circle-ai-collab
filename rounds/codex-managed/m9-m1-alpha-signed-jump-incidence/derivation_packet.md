# Round 51 derivation packet: signed alpha jump incidence

This packet freezes one finite local seam problem.  It does not assert a
global estimate.

## 1. Accepted multiplier and obstruction

Let \(\lambda=\mu+\nu\) and

\[
 m_\lambda(\beta)=(1-\psi(\beta))\psi(\beta+\lambda).
\]

The inverse log kernel has zero mass, but on separated bounded-alpha
slices it is a modulation of a fixed low-pass kernel with fixed nonzero
\(L^1\) norm.  Therefore a local jump is not small merely by BV.

## 2. Local actual seam model

Choose exactly one actual discontinuity from the finite alpha antecedent:

1. an \(H_j\)-floor crossing;
2. a product equality carrying the inherited star convention;
3. a boundary of a dyadic denominator profile;
4. a lower or upper radial endpoint.

Let \(L\) be its exact logarithmic coordinate and write locally

\[
 W(L)=W_{\rm reg}(L)+\Delta W\,S^*_{L_0}(L),
\]

where \(S^*\) has the actual equality value and distributional derivative
\(\delta_{L_0}\).  Do not replace \(\Delta W\) by one unless the actual
profile gives one.

## 3. Mandatory finite incidence ledger

Starting from one common finite endpoint-free alpha antecedent, list once:

1. the terminal fundamental-line contribution;
2. the \(A\)-strip Cauchy--Pompeiu area connector or both sharp edges;
3. oriented \(u\)- and \(v\)-faces;
4. positive \(u=0\) and \(v=0\) axes and one joint corner;
5. connector-face, connector-axis, and mixed connector terms;
6. the signed top distribution
   \[
   (0^++i\mu)^{-1}=\pi\delta_0(\mu)-i\,\operatorname{PV}(1/\mu);
   \]
7. any moving-boundary delta created because the seam location depends on
   a contour or height variable.

The \(A=0\) arithmetic residue, artificial \(\rho=0\) residue, identity
delta in log dilation, lattice Dirac comb, and Plemelj delta are distinct.
The alpha mask owns no new \(A=0\) residue.

## 4. Frozen question

Differentiate the complete finite expression distributionally in the seam
variable and compute the coefficient of
\(\Delta W\,\delta_{L_0}\).  Decide whether the sum of all incident
terminal, connector, face, axis, corner, moving-boundary, delta, and PV
traces is identically zero.

If it is zero, state the exact identity and the regular remainder left to
estimate.  If it is nonzero, give the first surviving actual coefficient
and explain its local capacity.  A rowwise contour identity alone is not a
proof of cancellation unless the singular coefficient is evaluated.

## 5. Scope

All calculations occur at finite \((U,V,S)\), before Abel, Plemelj,
profile, or outside-height limits and before absolute values.  Local seam
cancellation does not prove the global alpha estimate; noncancellation of
one row does not prove a lower bound for the fully signed arithmetic sum.

No numerical experiment or external theorem is needed.
