# Round 84 synthesis

## Objective and outcome

Round 84 asked whether the exact stationary square-root phase of the
Round-83 centered dual correlation forces a fixed power of
\(B=C/T\).  No whole-range \(B\)-power was proved.  The round does,
however, remove a polynomially long nonzero family exactly and leaves a
strictly smaller signed survivor.

## Exact stationary interface

For each of the three local classes, the stationary Fourier transform of
the smooth Round-81 principal coefficient has critical point

\[
 c_n=\sqrt{{4bA_{\kappa,b}\over |n|}},
\]

critical phase

\[
 -\eta\left(\sqrt X+{\sqrt{\kappa k}\over b}\right)\sqrt{|n|},
\]

Gaussian \(e(-\eta/8)\), and amplitude scale

\[
 H={C\sqrt T\over J}.
\]

The conductor-authorized Round-81 smooth extension transfers through the
monotone saddle map.  The complete phase-removed profile, including its
one-pass entry and exit, has sampled \(L^\infty+\mathrm{BV}\) norm
\(O_\varepsilon(X^\varepsilon H)\).  Aggregate Morse remainders and
wrong-sign tails are smaller than the target.

## New target-safe difference range

Exact Kloosterman Parseval gives

\[
 {1\over M^2}\sum_{r\bmod M}
 |S(r+d,K;M)\overline{S(r,K;M)}-c_M(d)|\leq2
\]

for every integer \(d\).  On \(n=r+M\ell\), the stationary product
phase has curvature

\[
 |\Psi''(\ell)|\asymp {M^2|d|\over J}.
\]

Consequently, for \(1\leq D\leq J^{17/30}\),

\[
 \mathfrak Y_{\leq D}
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon\left(
 C^3J^{-17/10}D^{3/2}
 +C^2TJ^{-3/2}D^{1/2}
 +{DC^2\over T\sqrt{JQ}}
 +{DB^2\over Q^2}\right).
\]

At \(C=J^{3/4}\), the first term reaches but does not exceed
\(J^{7/5}=J^2/T\) when \(D=J^{17/30}\); every other term is smaller.
Thus all

\[
 0<|d|\leq\lfloor J^{17/30}\rfloor
\]

are target-safe, including negative differences and nonzero modulus
multiples.

## Obstructions and sources

Bare BV data cannot justify the stationary leading symbol; the actual
Round-81 smooth extension is essential.  Complete stationary summation
is involutive and returns to the pre-dual rank-one progression.  Explicit
prime-power modes rule out a uniform coefficientwise square-root
completion, and no audited current shifted-Kloosterman or trace theorem
accepts the remaining composite-modulus actual-weight correlation.

## State decision

Promote the exact stationary interface and the small-difference deletion.
The first exact smooth-principal survivor is now the centered correlation
restricted to

\[
 |d|>\lfloor J^{17/30}\rfloor.
\]

No conductor interval, full `M9-M1` estimate, `M9`, or global exponent
is promoted.  The next proof mechanism must couple the large differences
before absolute values and must not count another complete transform as
a gain.
