# Statement-only problem: truncated squarefree divisor involutions

Let $J=\sqrt X$, $y=\lfloor J\rfloor$,
$q_X=X/y^2$, $H=\lfloor yX^{-1/4}\rfloor$, and
$1\ll L\ll H$. Define

\[
 \mathcal S_{L,1}
 =\sum_{N\asymp L^2}\mu^2(N)
   \left(\frac{L^2}{N}\right)^{3/4}
   e(J\sqrt N)b_{L,X}(N),
\]

where

\[
 b_{L,X}(N)=
 \sum_{\substack{d\mid N, d\ {\rm odd}\\
                   \sqrt N\le d\le2\sqrt N}}
 \chi_4(d)\eta_L(d)\Phi\!\left(\frac d{H+1}\right)
 W\!\left(\frac{\sqrt{q_X}\,d}{2\sqrt N}\right).
\]

All shells and intervals are literal and half-open where inherited;
even $N$, profile entries and exits, floors, stars, endpoints, and zero
extension must be retained. The target is

\[
 |\mathcal S_{L,1}|\ll_\varepsilon L^{3/2}X^\varepsilon.
\]

Independently test whether prime toggles, multi-prime exchanges,
complementary divisors, or another exact signed divisor involution proves
the target or a complete nontrivial arithmetic sector. If not, derive the
smallest rigorous leakage obstruction. Distinguish a full divisor sum
from the moving near-square interval, odd from even products, and a
physical coefficient from a diagnostic model. Restore every support,
profile, endpoint, multiplicity, and $L,H,J,X$ power.

Return the required seven-section report and identify the first doubtful
or unproved step.
