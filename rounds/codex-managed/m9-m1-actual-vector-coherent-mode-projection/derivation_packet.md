# Round 100 derivation packet

## 1. Accepted input and target

Round 99 proves that the joint \((d,u)\) Hessian and a second completion
return to the exact four-row Gram. The remaining open canonical statement is
the fixed-vector coefficient

\[
 |\widehat p_U^*\widehat{\mathcal K}_U\widehat q_U|
 \ll_\varepsilon X^\varepsilon
 { (U/B)J^{14/5}\over\mathsf C_U},
\]

equal to \(J^{-1/6}\) at the top. This round does not attack the whole
coefficient uniformly. It isolates its exact coherent-mode projection.

## 2. Literal rows and trace

Use the Round-92 physical rows

\[
 \mathcal R_{b,x}(\theta)=M^{-1}\sum_n I_b(n)e_M(nx)e(n\theta)
\]

and ordered pairs \(P=(x,x-A)\),
\(P'=(x-V,x-V-B_2)\). The completed trace is

\[
 \mathfrak T_M=M\sum_x^{\rm four\ units}
 e_M(ux+K\Phi(x)).
\]

Opening it gives exactly four normalized rows. The hard set is the literal
successive complement after the unique global \(u=0\) owner and the accepted
Round-87--89 owners.

## 3. Coherent local factor

For the local modulus \(8\),

\[
 \mathfrak T_8(u,A,B_2,V)=8e_8(K(A-B_2))c_8(u),
\]

where \(c_8(0)=4\), \(c_8(4)=-4\). In particular
\((A,B_2,V)=(2,2,2)\), \(u=4\) has trace \(-32\). Treat this as one CRT
factor of the full class modulus. Determine its exact lift multiplicity,
compatibility with the other local factors, and actual-row projection.

## 4. Mandatory distinctions

- Local trace magnitude is not an actual-vector lower bound.
- Full directed degree is not a dense archimedean plateau.
- The hard mask may fragment a formal eigenspace.
- The \(u=0\) owner must not be reinserted through a positive square.
- Nonzero modulus multiples, nonunit \(K\), and the full two-part remain.
- Any conductor cancellation occurs in the scalar sum before a direct-sum
  norm is taken.
- Entry/exit, stars, zero extension, aliases, signs, and reflections remain
  attached to the actual rows.

## 5. Quantitative exit

The round succeeds if it proves one of:

1. a nonempty coherent projector contributes at most
   \(X^\varepsilon(U/B)J^{14/5}\);
2. the fixed actual vector has a rigorously nonnegligible coupling that
   obstructs a uniform gap;
3. exact one-count algebra reduces the coherent contribution to a strictly
   smaller scalar conductor/physical-row correlation.

A coefficient-blind norm, local trace estimate, or unsigned degree count is
not sufficient.

## 6. Scope

No local result proves the full canonical hard estimate unless every
complementary mode is also owned. Do not infer GAR, blockwise M9-M1, M9-M2,
endpoint uniformity, M9, or an exponent.
