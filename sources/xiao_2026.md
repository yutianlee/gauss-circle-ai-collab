## Exact theorems audited

No theorem is imported as a proof dependency. With

\[
 S(h,n)=\sum_{n/2\leq a\leq n}e(h\sqrt a),
\]

Theorem 1.1 gives

\[
 \sum_{h\sim H}|S(h,n)|^2
 \ll_{\varepsilon,\delta}Hn^{1+\varepsilon}
\]

for fixed \(\delta>0\) and \(H\geq n^{1/2+\delta}\). Theorem 1.2 gives
the fourth moment \(\ll Hn^{2+\varepsilon}\) for fixed
\(0<\delta<1/6\) and
\(n^{1/2+\delta}\leq H\leq n^{2/3}\). Theorem 1.3 obtains a discrepancy
estimate by combining the second moment with Erdős--Turán and separate
pointwise exponential-sum inputs.
