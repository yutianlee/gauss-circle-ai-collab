## Result and applicability

Theorem 1.3 derives Voronoi formulas from Hecke relations, moderate
coefficient growth, and precise functional equations for every primitive
character twist. Example 1.8 discusses certain noncuspidal isobaric sums.
The main double-Dirichlet-series proof is for degree (N\ge3); Remark 3.2
gives a separate degree-two modification.

The project's product

\[
 \zeta(s+z/2)L(s-z/2,\chi_4)
\]

is degree two, ramified at (4), noncuspidal, and has a zeta pole. The
paper is therefore structural support for the functional-equation method,
but not a directly matched black-box theorem without separately deriving
the polar and ramified local terms. It also gives no mode-integrable bound
uniform in the project's complex shift and top Perron parameter.
